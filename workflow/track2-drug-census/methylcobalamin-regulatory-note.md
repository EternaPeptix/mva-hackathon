# Methylcobalamin: bounded Japanese regulatory follow-up

The official PMDA approved-drug index lists **Rozebalamin / mecobalamin, September 2024**. Eisai's dated launch announcement reports approval on **24 September 2024** and launch in Japan on **20 November 2024**. This verifies a historical approved-product route; it does not verify present stock, price, access outside Japan, FDA/EMA approval or hackathon eligibility.

The PMDA review identifies Rozebalamin for Injection 25 mg as a prescription lyophilized product for slowing functional impairment in ALS, with a required risk-management plan. The listed adult regimen matches the trial's 50 mg intramuscular dose twice weekly; this is source identification, not an MVA regimen. Each vial contains 28.75 mg of mecobalamin in total, including an overage to permit withdrawal of the labeled 25 mg; excipients are lactose hydrate, sodium dihydrogen phosphate dihydrate and sodium hydroxide. That product detail is not evidence that ordinary oral B12, lower-dose injections or compounded products reproduce the trial exposure.

Eisai explicitly links mecobalamin, E0302 and Rozebalamin and attributes approval to JETALS. Its release is manufacturer evidence for product history, not an independent efficacy replication. The PMDA report also describes older lower-dose injectable/oral mecobalamin products with other indications; their use cannot supply a controlled ALS or MVA result for those formulations.

The source reader inspected the PMDA index product row, text of PDF pages 1–6 of the 59-page PMDA English review, and the complete three-page manufacturer release. Independent regulatory review additionally inspected those nine native pages and accepted the corrected vial-content wording. Neither stage reviewed the remaining regulatory clinical assessment, Japanese original label, current-label amendments or stock/prices. The English translation defers to the Japanese original.
