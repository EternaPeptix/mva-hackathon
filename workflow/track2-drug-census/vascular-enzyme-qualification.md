# What three further primary studies establish

The broader search retains a **KLK8 research-antibody lead for organ protection**,
a **K858 vascular antiproliferation result**, and a **drug-associated BUB1B RNA
increase after enzyme replacement in Pompe myocytes**. None establishes benefit
in BUB1B-related MVA. These are positive findings with different mechanisms and
endpoints, not three failed attempts to treat MVA.

| Intervention | Measured benefit or role | Decision relative to niacin and apitegromab |
|---|---|---|
| Anti-KLK8 M021-3 / MabB5 | Better 48-hour survival in two mouse sepsis models; reduced lung albumin leak in the LPS model | Retain an organ-protection research lead. The controlled animal outcomes merit credit, but the BUB1B link is a separate genetic experiment and no muscle benefit was tested. |
| SC79 | Restored Akt phosphorylation and FOXM1 protein in KLK8-overexpressing endothelial cells | Retain an accessible experimental pathway probe. This paper does not assign barrier, survival or BUB1B rescue to SC79. |
| MHY1485 | Increased phospho-mTOR/phospho-S6K bands without restoring FOXM1 | Retain the negative FOXM1 result. MHY1485 treatment did not reproduce SC79's FOXM1 result under these conditions. |
| AZD5363 | Attenuated FOXM1 restoration from genetic VE-cadherin overexpression | Record an antagonist control, not a restorative candidate from this study. |
| K858 | Reduced post-injury arterial neointimal lesions and stenosis in mice | Retain the actual vascular benefit. No evidence here supports prioritizing it for BUBR1 restoration or muscle compensation. |
| Myozyme / alglucosidase alfa | Corrected glycogen burden and selected signaling/energy readouts in Pompe myocytes; BUB1B RNA increased | Retain a lesion-matched enzyme-replacement precedent. No GAA deficiency is established in this MVA case, so it does not supply a rationale for GAA replacement here. |

Niacin remains a provisional protein-restoration hypothesis and apitegromab a
separate muscle-compensation hypothesis. Neither has demonstrated MVA efficacy.
The comparison credits actual pharmacological function, mechanism relevance,
genotype/tissue transfer, usable exposure and harms on the same terms. A research
compound is not excluded merely because it lacks a marketed therapeutic product.
The patient's phase and N1002K function, abundance and stability remain unresolved.

Research supply listings were checked separately from efficacy: M021-3 has an
MBL catalogue entry, [SC79](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/mm/123871)
is listed as product 123871 (CAS 305834-79-1),
[MHY1485](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/sml0810)
as SML0810/500554 (CAS 326914-06-1), and
[K858](https://www.caymanchem.com/product/22112/k858) as Cayman 22112
(CAS 72926-24-0). These are concrete routes to research reagents, not verified
delivery dates or clinical formulations. The study-specific preparation and
exposure still matter; catalogue membership is not therapeutic evidence.

## KLK8: a real antibody result, a separate BUB1B bridge

[Zhao et al., PMID 40291017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12032894/)
administered M021-3 antibody at 20 micrograms/kg intravenously 30 minutes before
LPS or cecal ligation and puncture (CLP), with an IgG control. The paper reports
30 mice per survival arm and six per tissue/marker group. At 48 hours, the plots
show approximately 60% versus 27% survival after LPS and 63% versus 37% after CLP;
these are visual estimates, not reconstructed event counts. Both comparisons
are marked P<0.01. LPS-associated albumin extravasation fell, and histological
injury was partly attenuated in both models. The direct leakage panel is for
LPS; it should not be counted again as a CLP measurement.

This is pretreatment in acute injury. It does not isolate post-injury regeneration
or establish durable recovery. Random allocation and blinded measurements are
reported. All animals were male; the 3–4-week donor age belongs to isolated-cell
experiments and cannot be assigned to the survival cohorts. No delayed treatment,
long-term function, muscle force, pharmacokinetics or MVA model was reported.

The [manufacturer's M021-3 record](https://www.mblbio.com/bio/g/dtl/A/?pcd=M021-3)
identifies a mouse-reactive rat IgG2a antibody, clone MabB5. Its current stock
formulation is PBS/50% glycerol; the study does not report the final injected
formulation, dilution or carrier matching. This leaves formulation uncertain,
without establishing that a vehicle confound occurred. The identity does not
extend the result to every KLK8 inhibitor or establish human neutralization.

The only direct Bub1b measurement is **RNA after lentiviral FOXM1 overexpression**
in KLK8-overexpressing mouse endothelial cells (S7, n=4). Bub1b RNA and EdU-positive
fraction partly recovered, with group means remaining below vector-control means;
the panel does not supply a baseline-versus-rescue equivalence test. The antibody and SC79 arms
did not measure Bub1b/BUBR1, chromosome fidelity or checkpoint rescue. Increased
proliferation alone proves neither benefit nor harm in MVA.

SC79 at 10 micromolar restored pAkt/Akt and FOXM1 at the 48-hour endpoint (n=4).
MHY1485 at 10 micromolar raised phospho-mTOR and phospho-S6K bands without rescuing
FOXM1. The MHY1485 panel lacks total-mTOR/total-S6K denominators, so it is not a
quantified phosphorylation-fraction comparison. AZD5363 at 10 micromolar reduced
the FOXM1 restoration produced by genetic VE-cadherin overexpression. These are
three distinct pharmacological roles in one study, not independent replications.

The next discriminating experiment for this route would connect the administered
agent to BUBR1 protein and faithful divisions in relevant cells, alongside a
functional endpoint and matched normal controls. The mouse survival result
should remain visible even if that bridge proves absent.

## K858: vascular lesion benefit does not imply muscle restoration

[Wang et al., PMID 39166113](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11333341/)
tested K858 after carotid guidewire injury in eight-week-old male mice: 50 mg/kg
intraperitoneally every other day for two weeks, with DMSO-treated injury and
sham references. Figure 5 reports n=5 and P<0.01 for reduced intimal area,
intima/media ratio and lumen stenosis. It measures histology, not blood flow,
organ performance or survival. The 1-micromolar cell experiment suppresses
PDGF-stimulated S-phase entry and proliferation; it does not measure faithful
chromosome segregation. Limited apoptosis and body-weight observations do not
establish a general therapeutic window.

Bub1b is a network candidate, but its PDGF-versus-control qPCR comparison in
Figure 1E is nonsignificant. No K858-induced BUBR1 restoration is shown. The
Figure 3A–B caption says K858 where panel labels and Results show siKif11;
actual-drug EdU panels are 3F–G, despite a caption calling them CCK-8. The CCK-8
time course in 3H extends to 72 hours; the 24-hour caption cannot describe every
endpoint. The Figure 2A enrichment FDR is 0.3435. A
supplementary rapamycin experiment lowers KIF11, with a timing discrepancy
between legend and plot; it is not BUBR1 rescue. These distinctions preserve
the drug's real vascular result without converting a genetic result or an
association into a BUB1B-directed benefit.

## Enzyme replacement: BUB1B RNA responds in a different causal lesion

[Yoshida et al., PMID 29044175](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5647434/)
used Myozyme in induced myocytes from three unrelated infantile Pompe patients.
The [EMA product record](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/myozyme)
identifies Myozyme as alglucosidase alfa. This is not evidence for other GAA
formulations. At 0, 10 and 50 nanomolar over the final three differentiation
days, glycogen fell in most of six patient clones; Pom2b lacked a significant
dose response. The six clones represent three patients, not six independent
patients.

Other experiments use the paper's default 1-micromolar exposure for three days.
After amino-acid/insulin stimulation, pS6K/total S6K partly improved in two of
three patient clones, while p4E-BP1/total 4E-BP1 did not. Adenylate energy charge
improved in one patient clone across three experiments. MitoTracker fluorescence
also fell in two of three patient clones (three microscopic fields per condition);
this readout is not a direct measurement of mitochondrial performance. Figure 7C reports a
BUB1B qPCR increase across three patient lines (P<0.05). This earns credit as
an actual-drug transcript response, not BUBR1 protein stabilization or checkpoint
restoration. MyoD induction suppresses proliferation, and the authors explicitly
limit this model's use for cell-cycle analysis. No force or MVA experiment was
reported. Rapamycin was a signaling-assay control, not a disease-rescue arm.

The finding also warns against treating all muscle disease as excess mTORC1:
these Pompe myocytes had lower stimulated readouts, and enzyme replacement partly
restored them. That observation does not determine the direction in MVA. The
missing therapeutic bridge is GAA deficiency in the proposed target population,
not merely an additional BUB1B assay.

## Evidence depth

The [structured assessments](vascular-enzyme-qualification.json) distinguish
drug, genetic and assay-control results and record the exact reading depths.
KLK8 includes the main text, supplement methods and all figures; K858 and Pompe
include the complete main text/methods, all legends and specified key figures.
Pompe's full metabolite table was not audited. Raw articles, abstracts, figures
and supplements remain outside Git. These three qualifications add source depth;
they do not add three previously unread abstracts or establish exhaustive review.
