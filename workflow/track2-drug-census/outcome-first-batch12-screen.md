## Candidate-level signal from the batch

The 2015 HMB-containing drink study (PMID 26529601) is the only new full-paper follow-up from this batch so far. Its primary SPPB outcome was null; an open-label, no-drink-control comparison reported several unadjusted secondary torque, walking and endurance signals. The source-specific appraisal records why this is a low-cost functional comparator rather than a BUBR1/MVA lead. Magnesium oxide (PMID 25008857) and the 2021 collagen-peptide study (PMID 33946565) already have separate qualifications in the existing evidence base; they are not new candidates here.

The remaining records include exercise/nutrition bundles, protocols, reviews, biomarker-only studies, nulls and task-specific performance tests. Their source-level endpoint and preparation distinctions remain in the linked JSON; no component efficacy transfers from mixtures.
