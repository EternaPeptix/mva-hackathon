# MFGM fractions under shared exercise: source-specific function qualification

The cited papers support a preparation-family reserve for selected objective function under exercise. Soga reports a controlled primary leg-strength benefit; Ota 2015 reports controlled agility and local muscle-area signals. Minegishi's strength, chair-standing and muscle-area comparisons were nonpositive, although a small male subset had a conduction-velocity positive. These results cannot be assigned to MFGM without exercise, isolated sphingomyelin, ordinary milk, or the different MFGM-plus-glucosamine mixture.

One source reviewer read all three target scientific mains completely, including every main outcome cell and scientific declaration. That reviewer also recovered and read the exact Ota 2016 choice-stepping main to assess the requested two-2016 cohort issue. Native tables were inspected for all four papers: 5+4+6+2 tables; none contains a main figure. Reference lists were not individually qualified. Minegishi's publisher explicitly lists zero supplements; Ota/Soga full JATS and publisher pages exposed no original scientific supplement. Own registration/protocol/SAP and participant data remain unrecovered. This is not proof that the trials were unregistered.

The three fractions have materially different reported compositions. Phospholipids and sphingomyelin are lipid constituents, not additional whole-product masses to sum. Retail brand/lot equivalence is not established. The source statement that 1 g MFGM corresponds to approximately 600 mL milk does not establish ordinary-milk efficacy. No constituent-alone trial permits assigning these results to sphingomyelin or protein. All exercise arms used supplementation comparisons under the same prescribed exercise; there is no randomized exercise-versus-no-exercise factorial or formal exercise interaction.

Minegishi's abstract/conclusion overstate controlled strength/mass/performance evidence: the native tests above remain nonpositive. Its Discussion acknowledges that physical performance cannot be distinguished from exercise alone, then suggests distinguishability. Preserve that reporting tension and the physiological positive. Female age balance is also uneven: all six active women were 60–62, while placebo included two women over 70.

Ota's completer analysis loses 9/44, with more placebo losses; no all-randomized efficacy sensitivity is demonstrated. Soga explicitly calls isokinetic extension the primary and supplies a power calculation; its linear mixed model includes group, period, period baseline, group×period and participant random effects. Authors state apparent carryover was nonsignificant without a numerical test or period-specific results, and separately warn that the four-week washout may be insufficient. A nonsignificant carryover statement does not remove that limitation. No first-period-only sensitivity was reported. These studies use nominal tests and selected Bonferroni comparisons; global control across the wider outcome families is not demonstrated. Nonsignificance is not equivalence.

## Cohort identity, safety and mechanism

The two 2016 reports are **not identical reported protocols**: 22 completed/10 weeks/whole-milk control/8.03% SM versus 25 randomized→23 analyzed/5 weeks/maltitol control/3.5% SM, with different principal tests. Ota 2016 supplies no calendar conduct dates, linked participant identifiers or explicit cohort-overlap statement. Participant-level overlap and complete independence remain **UNKNOWN**. The other reports also differ in age/design, but incomplete conduct/participant provenance prevents claiming universal independence. Four papers are not counted as four independent replications; no pooled unique-participant denominator follows.

Minegishi reports no AEs or tablet-related effects in 22 participants; Ota 2015, Soga and Ota 2016 report no tablet-related adverse effects. This does not substitute for complete event/person tables or long-term/pediatric safety. Ota's laboratory interactions were nonpositive. Soga reports higher RBCs (P=.013) and hemoglobin (P<.001); neither proves erythropoiesis, renewal or improved endurance. Native Table 4 prints hemoglobin's unit as mg/dL; it is recorded without silently converting to customary g/dL. Other measured serum/WBC comparisons were nonpositive. Soga/Ota 2016 explicitly report Kao funding; all four papers have Kao-affiliated authors. Soga/Ota 2015 declare no competing interests; Minegishi's original main has no separate funding/COI declaration. These distinctions remain visible.

No read study measures BUB1B/BUBR1 rescue, faithful mitotic completion, actual Leu737Ter/Asn1002Lys response, productive progenitor renewal or MVA efficacy. Separate animal Dok-7/NMJ findings do not establish the human mechanism. Give the objective human functional positives their actual preparation/task credit, using the same limits applied to niacin/NMN/apitegromab; no stricter MVA-only threshold applies to this challenger family, and no cross-disease superiority is inferred. The existing MFGM-plus-glucosamine result remains mixture-specific rather than component attribution.
