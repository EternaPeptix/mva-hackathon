## Antitumor and clinical observations

The alisertib conference abstract PMC:PMC9165206 reports greater survival with continuous direct osmotic-pump delivery than a short-term delivery comparison in DMG mouse models (P=0.0002), with reduced burden and on-target effects in long-term survivors. Delivery duration is part of the result; dose, denominator, toxicity and full primary publication remain necessary.

A different conference case, PMC:PMC7715388, reports no disease evidence 60 months after diagnosis in a child diagnosed at seven months with metastatic anaplastic ependymoma. Treatment followed subtotal resection: five induction cycles including high-dose methotrexate, then three marrow-ablative cycles and autologous progenitor-cell rescue, without irradiation. This positive multimodal observation cannot isolate methotrexate or imply treatment targeted the newly identified SPECC1L-RAF1 fusion. Other regimen constituents, developmental effects and toxicity require the full report.

Trastuzumab plus lapatinib produced partial brain and lung metastatic responses in one HER2-amplified gallbladder-cancer case (MED:31106632). Duration, doses, toxicity and its linked erratum remain unresolved here. RC48/disitamab vedotin outperformed T-DM1 in cellular antiproliferation and retained activity in resistant BT474 sublines (MED:41754950); MMAE is the payload, while lapatinib/neratinib also appear as resistance backgrounds. Neither abstract establishes clinical transfer to MVA.

The BUB1 inhibitor 2OH-BNPP1 reduced cervical-cancer xenograft growth (MED:42602342); NAC rescue implicated ROS in apoptosis, and kinase-dead BUB1 failed genetic rescue. BUB1 must remain distinct from BUBR1. AZ3146 reduced endometrial-cancer growth/invasion and enhanced paclitaxel apoptosis (MED:36829176); TTK shRNA separately reduced EMT and chemoresistance. Unnamed PLK1/HSP90 inhibitors synergistically suppressed glioma-stem-cell proliferation/stemness (MED:37468549), while reduced tumorigenicity belonged to a separate RNF8-overexpression result. BTYNB interfered with experimental mouse tumor growth through IGF2BP1-RNA/E2F effects (MED:32761127). These are substantial antitumor observations with unresolved exposure/control boundaries, not restoration of faithfully completed divisions.

NPD11033 inhibited SIRT2 **deacetylase but not defatty-acylase** activity, increased eIF5A acetylation and reduced PANC-1 growth (MED:29685974). GW809897X/GW806742X induced K-562 apoptosis/arrest, with stronger AURKA/AURKB expression reduction by GW806742X (MED:33277547). Neither protein-expression direction nor tumor-cell death establishes BUBR1 restoration. PLK1-inhibitor responses were stronger in p53-intact cells, while surviving p53-null cells retained DNA damage and colony formation (MED:23574746).

The EXOMA cohort provides a relevant null comparison (MED:31923800): PFS2/PFS1 >1.3 occurred in 23.5% of matched-treatment and 23.7% of standard-treatment recipients. Of 506 enrolled, 456 had somatic results, 386 paired results, 342 a therapeutic proposal and 79 matched treatment. Discontinuation included progression in 50 (63%), toxicity in 18 (23%) and death in four (5%). Profiling feasibility is not improved clinical outcome.

## Interpretation limits retained

- CEP57 overexpression associated with better prostate-cancer recurrence-free survival despite mitotic impairment (MED:26692530). AR nuclear transport was unchanged; cytokinesis-defect-like morphology and a proposed reduced-daughter explanation do not establish faithful-division benefit. Docetaxel is background context in that abstract.
- Calcium-ionophore activation benefits in selected ICSI populations (MED:40868259), menopause/estrogen timing (MED:37047549), and luspatercept/imetelstat or vitamin B5/COASY ideas in MDS (MED:40124717) remain at their stated review depths. Proposed strategies and absence of reported significant safety signals are not defined-regimen efficacy or long-term safety proof.
- Cisplatin/tretinoin/bortezomib/metformin in MED:35918384 and docetaxel/AZD5582/VE-822 in MED:42510982 are computational suggestions or sensitivity associations. Transcript or pathway validation does not establish administered drug response.
- Angiotensin receptor antagonists in MED:25365520 changed hub-gene expression; functional tumor reduction is not supplied. MED:41803973 tested lactic acid, Oxamate, 2-DG and GLUT probes, but does not allocate each drug's functional direction. Expected pharmacology cannot fill that gap.
- Auxin is an engineered degradation trigger; nocodazole is a stress/release condition; IL-4/anti-CD40 are stimulation reagents; CCK-8/EdU are assays. Their occurrence does not itself establish therapeutic use. APC in the EB1 paper means adenomatous polyposis coli, not APC/C.
- MED:36605491's CDK5 PFS association is nonsignificant (HR 1.26, P=0.17), despite broader narrative wording. MED:42512087 explicitly treats AUC 1.00 as a discovery-cohort signal. Nominal SNP findings in MED:21607584 did not survive Bonferroni correction.
