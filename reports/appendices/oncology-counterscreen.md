# Track 2 oncology comparison: benefit must survive a tumor-protection test

20 September 2026 UTC. **Prospective research addendum; no drug or cell experiment
was performed.** Arimoclomol remains a conditional proteostasis probe and
acetylcysteine remains an exploratory downstream comparator. Neither is advanced
as a treatment by this review.

The recorded phenotype includes rhabdomyosarcoma (RMS). A candidate that improves
nonmalignant cells could have a different effect on tumor cells. The proposal
therefore needs a direct tumor comparison alongside the existing tests for
segregation errors, arrest and selection. This is our experimental inference;
it is not evidence that either candidate harms this child.

## 1. Evidence that changes the experimental design

| Primary source | Finding used here | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Sabnis et al., PNAS 2016](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1603883113), Figures 1 and 3 | Pharmacologic inhibition and combined cytosolic HSP70 knockdown reduced RMS-cell survival. | [Kwong et al., Oncotarget 2025](https://doi.org/10.18632/oncotarget.28764), Figures 2 and 4 | MAL3-101's xenograft effect was transient. A different proteostasis inhibitor, CB-5083, had heterogeneous effects across RMS xenograft models. | Cell-culture activity, exposure and tumor context must be distinguished. Neither compound supplies arimoclomol efficacy or safety evidence. |
| [Allegakoen et al., Scientific Reports 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53526-z), Results/Discussion | DNAJC17 loss disrupted splicing and cell-cycle progression; its phenotype differed from MAL3-101 treatment. | This does not establish arimoclomol-induced arrest or BUB1B rescue. It reinforces the need to measure function separately from chaperone abundance. |
| [MIPLYFFA label, revision March 2026](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=5feffc0e-453d-47fa-91dd-38d4952309bc&version=5), sections 12.1 and 13.1 | The label reports no increased tumor incidence in two-year rat and 26-week rasH2 mouse studies at approximately 8- and 11-fold human AUC exposure. The label's negative carcinogenicity findings must accompany the mechanistic
concern. We cannot reverse an inhibitor result into a claim that arimoclomol
promotes cancer, or transfer findings between HSP70, HSP90 and p97 interventions.
No new anticancer drug is added to the repurposing shortlist.

for arimoclomol/MIPLYFFA plus RMS, and three matches using broader indexed fields.
The latter included a myostatin study using an RMS-derived cell line; arimoclomol
appeared in its discussion of other work, not as a reported treatment arm.
[Sachdev et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12035-018-0997-9).

## 2. Separate what is recorded from what a laboratory would need

| Information | Current status and consequence |
|---|---|
| RMS in the clinical phenotype list | Recorded; supports adding this comparison. |
| Current disease activity, anatomical site, histological/molecular subtype, FOXO1 fusion status, treatment history and present regimen | Unconfirmed in this project. Do not infer these from the diagnosis or substitute a standard regimen. |
| Tumor BUB1B genotype, expression, copy-number state and relevant acquired alterations | Unmeasured here. A constitutional candidate does not establish the tumor's state. |
| Patient-derived nonmalignant or tumor material and permission to use it | Not available to this project. No sample request or collaborator contact has been made. |
| Candidate exposure in either cell type and compatibility with actual treatment | Unmeasured. Requires a pharmacology plan and a qualified model. |

The initial nonmalignant arm remains the model-qualified comparison in the
[pilot protocol](pilot-protocol.md), including the unresolved phase
alternatives and genetic correction controls. Keep endogenous alleles and model
identity explicit. A tumor-derived line must never be relabeled a normal muscle
control merely because it expresses muscle markers.

For an exploratory tumor panel, propose four independent authenticated RMS model
slots: two fusion-positive and two fusion-negative. This is a coverage proposal,
not a power calculation or a set of acquired lines. If clinical subtype becomes
available, prioritize models representing it and document the change before
confirmation. Related derivatives are paired controls, not extra independent
tumors. One available line can qualify an assay but cannot represent the panel.
A patient-derived model, if appropriately available later, remains a separate
comparison rather than inheriting another line's results.

## 3. Test the actual candidates in each relevant cellular context

Evaluate arimoclomol and acetylcysteine separately. The following matrix concerns
each candidate paired with a **confirmed oncology treatment**, not a combination
of the two repurposing candidates:

| Arm | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Vehicle | Baseline cell output and spontaneous death |
| Candidate alone | Detect growth promotion, cytostasis, death and recovery |
| Confirmed oncology treatment alone | Establish an interpretable response in that model |
| Candidate plus the same oncology treatment | Test preservation of tumor cells or interference with treatment |

Until the regimen is confirmed, only the candidate-alone comparison can be
specified concretely. Leave the treatment identity, active species, schedule and
exposures unfilled in the plan. A nonresponsive treatment-control arm cannot
establish compatibility. Validate any metabolic activation needed by the model;
do not interpret exposure to an inactive parent compound as a treatment test.

Use a concentration range and exposure schedule justified with the existing
[arimoclomol](mechanistic-bridge.md) and
[acetylcysteine](nac-exposure.md) reviews and new measurements.
Compare measured exposure across media and cell types; equal nominal
concentrations do not establish equal intracellular exposure. Keep deliberately
high mechanistic controls separate from the plausible-exposure range. Candidate
pretreatment, concurrent exposure and recovery after treatment are distinct
questions; an oncology collaborator must select clinically relevant timing before
confirmation. Do not choose the most favorable schedule after seeing results.

Randomize well positions within independent preparations, blind scoring, retain
all qualified models and report passage, density, medium, oxygen and exposure
stability. Validate assay interference with compound-only and orthogonal controls.
For NAC, retain the existing distinction between reduced/free drug, total measured
thiols and an intracellular response. Direct effects on an assay or on the
treatment reagent are not proof of cellular rescue.

## 4. Measure survival and function without changing denominators

The nonmalignant arm retains both primary first-division endpoints from the pilot:
visible errors among scorable anaphases, and observed first-anaphase yield without
a scored error per starting founder. Preserve arrest, death, slippage, unscorable
events and tracking loss. Continue copy-number and lineage observations. An
improved protein signal cannot replace these measurements.

In tumor models, record absolute viable-cell output from a known starting count,
division and death over time, and post-washout regrowth or a validated colony assay.
Record both colony number and size where the assay supports them. Some models may
need a different validated regrowth assay; inability to form colonies is not proof
that the candidate is safe. Keep the assay's observation window explicit. A short
recovery window cannot exclude later resistance, recurrence or clonal selection.

Let `V(condition, t)` be the number of live cells at time t divided by that
condition's starting live-cell count. Report candidate/vehicle and
candidate-plus-treatment/treatment-alone comparisons at the same elapsed times,
with uncertainty. Ratios with zero denominators are undefined; show underlying
counts rather than adding an undisclosed pseudocount. Analyze the full four-arm,
multi-concentration design under a prespecified interaction model. A single ratio
does not distinguish baseline growth effects from treatment antagonism.

Retain the yield from the **original exposed culture** when plating for recovery.
If equal numbers of survivors are replated, also record total survivors and the
fraction replated so survival loss or protection is not erased. Correct cumulative
output for sampled fractions and passages. Report clonal composition alongside
absolute output; selective replacement is not restoration of the original cells.

For arimoclomol, distinguish inducible HSPA1A/B, constitutive HSPA8, and ER HSPA5
readouts where technically feasible; record antibody/transcript specificity and
any family members that cannot be resolved. Measure BUBR1 abundance, localization
and segregation separately. Stress-pathway readouts such as eIF2alpha/ATF4/CHOP
provide context, not a universal surrogate for death or therapeutic benefit.
Do not use a change in DNAJC17 or an HSP70-family measurement as a validated
predictor of this child's response.

## 5. Decide using both benefit and adverse-effect uncertainty

Before confirmation, a separate pilot must establish reproducibility, treatment
dynamic range, observation windows and independent preparation variability.
Choose a minimum useful nonmalignant benefit and maximum acceptable tumor-growth
or treatment-interference effect with the relevant specialists. These numerical
margins and sample sizes remain **unset**. Photographing more cells or adding
technical wells does not replace independent models and preparations.

| Result | Research decision |
|---|---|
| Reproducible nonmalignant functional benefit; adequately sensitive tumor comparisons exclude the prespecified adverse-effect margins within the tested scope | Eligible for further translational review, not a finding of clinical safety or benefit. |
| Nonmalignant benefit with credible tumor-growth promotion, increased tumor recovery or reduced treatment effect at plausible exposure | Withhold advancement; retain and independently investigate the adverse signal. Do not convert it into a claim of observed harm in this child. |
| Nonmalignant benefit with wide tumor-effect intervals, absent exposure measurements, weak treatment controls or discordant models | Inconclusive; resolve the specific limitation before advancement. |
| Protein/stress marker changes without the required functional benefit | Mechanistic observation only; does not pass the existing rescue criteria. |
| Benefit confined to high exposure or accompanied by arrest/selection | Does not support practical repurposing under this design. |

For an acceptable negative tumor comparison, the prespecified upper confidence
bound must exclude the adverse-effect margin under a valid design. A nonsignificant
p-value alone is insufficient. Account for multiple candidate exposures, models,
schedules and endpoints. Do not hide one adverse model inside a favorable pooled
average. Even a well-controlled negative panel cannot exclude all long-term or
patient-specific risks.

This module adds laboratory work beyond the existing pilot's capacity estimates;
its tumor models, combination matrices, follow-up, storage and cost are **not yet
budgeted**. Laboratory execution and clinical interpretation require collaboration.
The revised report and this addendum are local research drafts. Neither changes
Track 1 EPCR, establishes phase, substitutes for oncological care, or represents
