# EPA+DHA formulations: controlled strength signal and a distinct long-term null

**Integrated source qualification, 26 September 2026. Lovaza receives a separate appraisal; earlier free-EPA and other decision objects remain unchanged.**

Smith 2015 supplies a real active-versus-control grip/strength signal for **Lovaza omega-3-acid ethyl esters** in healthy older adults. It does not establish a general fish-oil, EPA-monotherapy, pediatric, MVA or BUBR1-restoration effect. The completer analysis, four unadjusted primary outcomes, limited outcome reporting and incomplete historical registry/protocol access constrain confirmation. One deliberately selected counterstudy, the MAPT functional secondary analysis, found no strength benefit with a different, substantially lower-dose, DHA-heavy fish-oil preparation over three years. Neither result cancels the other regimen by class membership.

## Smith 2015: tested treatment, population and hierarchy

The intervention was **four 1-g Lovaza capsules/day**, supplied by GlaxoSmithKline, nominally **1.86 g EPA plus 1.50 g DHA daily as the ethyl-ester mixture**, split between breakfast and dinner for six months. Control was four identical-looking corn-oil capsules/day. Current label description gives at least 900 mg omega-3 ethyl esters per 1-g capsule, predominantly approximately 465 mg EPA ethyl ester and 375 mg DHA ethyl ester. This is not free EPA, purified EPA alone, food intake or an arbitrary supplement. No assay establishes the chemical content of the historical trial batch here.

The trial enrolled sedentary, generally healthy adults aged 60-85, excluding serious chronic disease, relevant musculoskeletal limitations, recent muscle-affecting medicines and low baseline performance. Completers had median Physical Performance Test (PPT) 34/36. Generalization to frail or diseased muscle is therefore untested. The paper calls the design double blind and 2:1 randomized but does not report sequence generation or allocation concealment sufficiently to reproduce it. Fishy aftertaste could reveal assignment; diet was not measured or controlled, although habitual intake was requested.

The main declares **four primary outcomes**: MRI thigh volume, grip strength, composite four-exercise 1-RM and composite four-condition average isokinetic power. The current registry has broader 24-week primary categories of muscle volume and physical function; physical-function wording also includes isometric force and a usual-activity PPT. The article reports baseline PPT but no post-treatment PPT or isometric-force result. This is an unresolved registry/reporting-scope mismatch, not proof of intentional selective reporting. Registry first posting preceded trial start, but the retrieved record is a later version: the original prospective hierarchy remains unverified. Registry enrollment 77 includes a separate young, noninterventional comparison arm; do not replace the article's **60 older randomized participants** with 77 or infer an exact young-arm denominator.

## Denominators, outcomes and interpretation

Native Figure 1: 109 screened, 49 excluded, 60 randomized (40 active/20 control), 35/18 completing baseline, 31/15 completing three months, and **29/15 completing final testing**. All efficacy analyses explicitly use completers. Figure 2 further gives **28 active/15 control for grip and 1-RM** because an active completer could not perform those tests owing to shoulder/arm pain. MRI uses 29/15. These are not intention-to-treat estimates for all 60.

| Six-month endpoint | Reported active-minus-control effect | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| MRI thigh volume | **+3.6%**, 95% CI **0.2 to 7.0**; P<.05 | Structural muscle volume; not strength or directly measured muscle quality. Baseline-adjusted ANCOVA. |
| Handgrip strength | **+2.3 kg**, 95% CI **0.8 to 3.7**; main Results P<.01 | Actual controlled strength signal; not a within-arm-only result. Repeated-measures mixed-model analysis. |
| Composite 1-RM | **+4.0%**, 95% CI **0.8 to 7.3**; P<.05 | Sum of leg press, chest press, knee extension and knee flexion; do not credit four independent positive tests. |
| Composite average isokinetic power | **+5.6%**, 95% CI **-0.6 to 11.7**; P=.075 | Non-significant; includes zero. Three-month contrast also non-significant (P=.12). |

Figure 2's black bars are treatment contrasts, while its double asterisks mark within-arm changes. The main Results supplies the between-group claims above; bar heights were not digitized as exact data. MRI, force and power remain separate constructs. No walking, daily-living improvement, fall reduction, muscle renewal or chromosome-fidelity benefit is established by these outcomes.

Tests used P<=.05 without a reported family correction for the four primary endpoints; the authors explicitly acknowledge increased false-positive risk. Power calculations covered volume/grip and expected 48 completers, versus 44 observed. No imputation, randomized-population sensitivity analysis or raw participant data was provided in the retrieved materials. Similar dropout proportions do not establish noninformative missingness. The reported grip P<.01 deserves retention; this qualification does not erase every signal because the family was unadjusted or manufacture unreported corrected estimates.

## Harms and source inconsistencies

Safety Table 3 includes **35 active/18 control treatment initiators**, not only efficacy completers. Twelve active participants reported fishy aftertaste (34.3%); gastrointestinal symptoms were reported in 11 active (31.4%) and six controls (33%). Native Figure 1 records **three active withdrawals for pill side effects**, an active melanoma exclusion, and a control DVT exclusion. Table 3 classifies melanoma/DVT as unlikely related; this does not prove causality or absence of risk. The paper reports no serious treatment-related event, which is narrower than no serious event. Laboratory/BP treatment contrasts were non-significant.

Two reporting defects are retained: Figure 1 describes two controls discontinuing **after** baseline testing, whereas Table 3's footnote groups seven randomized exclusions as not completing baseline; and native HTML Table 2 prints malformed AST/ALT control CI bounds (missing negative signs are plausible but not verified from a native main PDF). Neither is silently repaired. Exact event timing and malformed bounds remain unresolved.

The 2025 product label identifies an approved adult severe-hypertriglyceridemia drug, not a muscle or MVA indication. Pediatric safety/effectiveness are unestablished. Relevant labelled limits include fish allergy, LDL monitoring, coagulation-drug monitoring and possible recurrent AF/flutter in susceptible patients. These are product-context constraints, not evidence that this small muscle trial measured those risks adequately. No individualized regimen is proposed.

NIH support and a GSK gift of active/placebo pills are reported; authors state funders had no role in design, implementation, analysis or interpretation and declare no study-related conflict. Those statements do not independently establish absence of sponsor influence.

## One counterstudy: MAPT, a different EPA+DHA regimen

MAPT randomized 1,680 adults aged at least 70; 1,679 contributed to its modified intention-to-treat analysis. Eligibility included memory complaint, instrumental-activity limitation or slow gait. The comparison retained here is **V0137 natural fish oil alone versus flavored-paraffin placebo**, without assigning combination-arm results to fish oil: **800 mg DHA plus 225 mg EPA/day** for 36 months. The functional report does not resolve its ester/triglyceride form. This is lower total EPA+DHA and a different ratio/preparation from Lovaza.

The parent trial's registered primary endpoint was cognition. Strength is a secondary trial outcome even though this secondary paper labels grip/chair outcomes primary for its analysis. Native Table 3 standalone contrasts are:

| Outcome | Three-year difference, omega-3 alone minus placebo | P raw / Hochberg-adjusted |
|---|---|---|
| Five-chair-stand time | **-0.053 s**, 95% CI **-0.680 to 0.572** | .866 / .866 |
| Grip | **+0.382 kg**, 95% CI **-0.575 to 1.341** | .433 / .914 |
| Four-meter walking speed | **-0.015 m/s**, 95% CI **-0.053 to 0.023** | .435 / .737 |
| SPPB | **-0.110 points**, 95% CI **-0.370 to 0.149** | .403 / .646 |

The mixed models include baseline and 6/12/24/36-month measurements. Hochberg adjustment concerns the three intervention-versus-placebo comparisons per model, not a demonstrated correction across all endpoints/subgroups. The table's 422 active/420 placebo are analysis-arm counts, not verified counts completing every 36-month test. This paper does not provide complete endpoint-by-visit missingness or a harms table; no zero-harm claim is made.

Native tables contain additional reporting inconsistencies: combined/multidomain chair estimates are interchanged in the abstract versus Table 3, the abstract gives an inconsistent combined-arm P value, Table 5's generic direction footnote conflicts with lower-is-better chair time, and some subgroup entries/denominators appear malformed. The standalone Table 3 contrasts used above remain identifiable and non-significant. Unstable subgroup detail is not used to infer a responder population.

MAPT funding included French public sources and Pierre Fabre Research Institute, the supplement manufacturer; authors declare no conflict. The original cognitive-outcome paper, its harms and historical protocol/SAP were not independently qualified here. This one counterstudy bounds broad claims about omega-3 efficacy; it is not a dose-matched refutation of the Smith trial.

## Consequence for the comparison

Retain Lovaza as a **preliminary controlled human-strength research comparator**, with its power null and analysis limitations. It has no measured BUBR1 abundance/stability, actual-allele rescue, chromosome fidelity or renewal endpoint. Apply the same functional, exposure, renewal and fidelity criteria used for niacin/apitegromab; absence of MVA rescue does not uniquely exclude this challenger, and this trial does not establish clinical superiority. Existing `new-epa` must remain unchanged. If later admitted, exact Lovaza and MAPT preparation/source scopes must remain distinguishable, with no broad fish-oil aliases or inherited monotherapy efficacy.

The most useful remaining work is original registry/protocol/SAP recovery and clarification of omitted physical-function outcomes, then a prespecified replication of the actual strength contrasts with complete randomized outcome accounting. The source reading adds no original-queue credit, nomination change or retail-stock claim. Root integration records a distinct Lovaza appraisal while preserving the separate MAPT preparation and all earlier decisions.
