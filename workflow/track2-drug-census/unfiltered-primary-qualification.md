The same missing bridge applies to the current [niacin restoration / apitegromab muscle-compensation comparison](decision-report.md), tested as separate agents; everolimus remains conditional. The judgments below compare observed function, mechanism, tissue relevance and liabilities, not inferred efficacy scores. Published exposures describe experiments, not proposed treatment regimens.

## Qualifications that change interpretation

- **SH1/MZ242 and berberine:** SH1 Figure 8 quantifies integrated acetyl-tubulin fluorescence, which also depends on outlined cell area. Its individual-cell workbook has no culture/batch identifiers; cell counts cannot supply independent-culture replication. No matched inactive degrader or degradation-resistant rescue establishes the morphology's mechanism here. Berberine's BUB1B increase passes the source table's FDR threshold only at 47 μM/24 h among the reported concentration/time points. Increased NADH with unchanged NAD+ is redox disturbance, not useful NAD restoration. Exposure-specific results cannot define human safety.
- **NAC:** Figure 2C p16/p21, Figure 1C–D DNA-damage panels and Figure 3E–F VEGF/CFH panels have no NAC comparison. Do not transfer their stressor-associated changes to drug rescue. ROS and cytokine assays specify three independent experiments; Figure 2D's independent biological count is unclear. There is no delayed-start treatment arm.
- **Immune combination:** Colon26 progression occurred in 2/8 combination versus 8/8 anti-CD4 mice; 4/5 selected complete rejectors resisted rechallenge, without a concurrent naive rechallenge control. These are tumor outcomes, not lifespan evidence. The four-arm experiment supports added benefit without establishing formal synergy. Nude-mouse panels omit the combination, so they do not directly prove combination-specific T-cell dependence. Melanoma improvement required a revised schedule. CD4 depletion extends to draining nodes; systematic normal-tissue safety was not assessed. Human HMGN1 in mice and the separately discussed humanized antibody are not human combination efficacy.
- **Collagens:** Figure 7C/D supports absolute secretion, but equal aggregate counts do not establish equal cell counts or secretion per founder cell. Figure 7F's caption/image calls the endpoint glucagon while the Results calls it C-peptide; exclude that panel as unambiguous evidence of improved proinsulin processing. NAC, nicotinamide and other shared medium components receive no individually isolated benefit credit. The adult-islet comparison is external literature, not a concurrent control.
- **CFI402257:** The low-dose genomic observation is substantive but cannot separate fewer new errors from selection or altered division rates. Micronucleus and three-week copy-number panels lack endpoint-specific variance/replication detail. The reported “65%” concerns the etoposide-associated increase prevented, not all copy-number events; drug alone does not visibly improve that spontaneous endpoint. The [same-compound counterstudy](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1700234114) reports checkpoint failure/missegregation at higher exposure in HCT116 cells; it does not prove harm at the EAC study's 10 nM condition.
- **Jasplakinolide:** Monastrol/nocodazole comparison arms themselves suppress growth/viability, and combination ALP is not normalized to cell number. The newer paper's nocodazole legends conflict (20 versus 100 ng/mL); retain that uncertainty. Calcium-deposition evidence belongs to the earlier paper, whose overlapping authors do not provide independent-laboratory replication. Its non-monotonic 50 nM responses do not demonstrate catastrophic toxicity or apoptosis above untreated controls.
- **Bub3 / sFRP3:** NaCr biochemical assays used HEK293T and localization assays used HeLa, with 50 mM NaCr versus 20 mM NaCl, an unmatched sodium comparator. Figure 1 segregation/timing experiments used Bub3 K222Q/K222R after endogenous depletion, not NaCr/NAM treatment; both fixed modification states impair segregation. Separate SIRT1 knockdown increased the modification; NAM-induced chromosome errors were not tested. In the sFRP3 paper, total microglial density is unchanged and some activation comparisons are nonsignificant; earlier developmental/growth results belong to a distinct 2019 study.
