Exact-title follow-up also found a related full paper with a selected PGT-A comparison and a sibling-oocyte pilot. ## What P-138 establishes at abstract depth

Oocytes received **1.25 mM caffeine for four hours after retrieval**, were washed into fresh fertilization medium, and mature oocytes underwent ICSI. The comparator was previous untreated cycles in the same patients. This is an external culture manipulation, not caffeine administered to patients.

The abstract reports euploidy **23.5% versus 15.6%, P=.02**, and mosaicism **18.6% versus 29.4%, P=.0048**. It also reports fertilization 77.5% versus 71.8%, day-three top-quality cleavage 64.0% versus 57.7%, blastocyst utilization 39.5% versus 32.8%, and top-quality blastocysts 40.4% versus 30.2%, all reported P<.0001. The last difference is **10.2 percentage points**, not a 10.2% relative increase.

PolScope spindle assessment involved **205 of 783 patients**; normal location, defined as an angle below 30 degrees, was 79.2% versus 58.6%, P<.0001. The complementary abnormal/no-visible-spindle percentages are the same classification contrast, not an independent replication or direct chromosome count.

The 10,474 retrieved oocytes are not 10,474 independent patients. Numbers of treatment/control cycles, mature oocytes, fertilized embryos, biopsied blastocysts, tested patients and euploid/mosaic embryos are not supplied. Patient/cycle clustering, paired analysis, selection into testing, losses/deaths, multiplicity and confidence intervals are not described. No live-birth, transfer, miscarriage or harm counts are supplied. The assay platform, mosaic thresholds, segmental-abnormality policy and inconclusive-result handling are unknown at this depth. Do not multiply the reported ploidy percentages by all retrieved oocytes.

Previous-cycle matching controls some stable patient attributes; it does not remove treatment-order, calendar/practice, stimulation or selection effects. Nonsignificant age comparisons do not establish exchangeability. Myt1/Wee1/MPF is a proposed mechanism, not a measured pathway response or BUBR1 result.

## What the related full paper adds—and does not add

The full paper specifies **Sigma-Aldrich caffeine C8960 in SAGE Quinn's Advantage Fertilization Medium**, four hours at 1.25 mM, with cumulus removal at two hours and repeated washing before ICSI. It does not measure residual caffeine or establish any oral/systemic exposure equivalence. These product details cannot silently be assigned to P-138.

All caffeine cycles occurred **after** their paired untreated cycles. Entry exclusions include abnormal retrieval, abnormal fertilization and abnormal embryo development by day two. These are material selection limits for claims about yield per starting cycle or oocyte. Paired Wilcoxon and McNemar tests are described, which is stronger statistical detail than the abstract; however, exact mappings of embryo-level outcomes to analysis units, within-cycle clustering and zero-denominator handling are not fully explained. No multiplicity adjustment is described.

Higher tested-euploid fraction could reflect altered selection as well as altered error generation; it cannot by itself distinguish them.

## Birth observations are selected, caffeine-only follow-up

Native Figure 2 shows **704 caffeine cycles → 344 attempted transfers → 225 analyzed transfers**. No transfer occurred in 360 cycles: 167 total embryo-cryopreservation failures, 70 with all embryos abnormal/mosaic after PGT-A, and 123 unused for other reasons. Another 119 mixed caffeine/non-caffeine transfers were excluded. These categories must not disappear behind a favorable tested-embryo percentage; they also are not all demonstrated embryo deaths.

Clinical pregnancy occurred in 88/225 analyzed transfer cycles. The report gives 20/88 pregnancy losses and one ectopic pregnancy. Neonatal data remain for **20 women and 24 live-born infants**, after the flow excludes 20 spontaneous abortions, 33 transfers to other clinics, five missing records and ten ongoing pregnancies. There is no comparable untreated-cycle birth cohort. Accordingly, neither 24/704 nor 24/225 is an ascertained comparative live-birth rate, and 24 infants are not 24 independent mothers.

Among observed neonates, five had birth weight below 2,500 g and three required NICU admission; four of 20 pregnancies delivered at 32–36 weeks and four were twin pregnancies. No neonatal death or birth defect was observed in the small followed subset. This does not establish general safety or exclude uncommon/long-term harms. Table 6 prints two low one-minute Apgar scores as 10.0% while labelling the denominator per neonate; 2/24 is not 10.0%. Preserve this reporting inconsistency rather than silently repairing it. The favorable pregnancy comparison to unrelated historical literature in Discussion is not a controlled treatment effect.

## Sibling-oocyte supplement is another bounded comparison

The single supplementary DOCX table reports **30 patients**, 206 caffeine versus 202 untreated MII oocytes. High-quality cleavage was 36.7% versus 25.2%, P=.040, and high-quality blastocysts 14.5% versus 4.6%, P=.034. Fertilization was nonsignificant, P=.757, as was blastocyst utilization, P=.786. No PGT-A, live-birth or safety comparison is supplied. Allocation, masking, detailed analysis and overlap with the main cohort are not described. It is not a verified randomized experiment or independent replication; do not replace the utilization null with the discussion's broader blastulation-positive wording.

This source set provides a more direct human embryo chromosome-status endpoint than a generic mechanistic association. It deserves a **selected-embryo ploidy/development reserve**, while causal fidelity rescue and absolute productive yield remain **UNKNOWN**.
