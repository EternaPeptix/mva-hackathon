## Functional positives beyond drugs

Actual rescue and productive outcomes are retained with their modalities. A Mad1-Golgi fragment and exogenous α5 integrin rescued secretion/cytokinesis in Mad1-depleted cells (MED41912567). Engineered Mad1 tethering to nuclear pores restored checkpoint-inhibitor production and normal M-phase control (MED24581499). Nucleus-restricted MTHFD2 was sufficient for correct mitotic progression (MED39532843). Exogenous Cyclin B1 restored arrest in the MED20927403 setting; transcription/translation inhibitors instead shortened arrest and promoted slippage. These are constructive mechanistic results, with no automatic inference of drug delivery or euploid daughter production.

A culture shift from37°C to30°C increased specific recombinant-tPA productivity1.6-fold and extended cell survival (MED26991106); tPA was the product, not administered treatment. Timp1 knockout reduced MWCNT-associated fibrotic foci/collagen and fibroblast responses (MED27852133), without stated lung-function or survival benefit. STIL depletion inhibited tumor growth/metastasis (MED35365182), subject to an unresolved erratum. Variant-specific Astrin/SKA3 function (MED38361632) cautions against assuming every truncation has the same functional effect; it does not establish another patient's variant effect.

## Harms and endpoint boundaries

Survivin deletion caused marrow ablation, progenitor loss and rapid mortality in mice (MED17576776), establishing a genetic normal-tissue safety concern without proving toxicity of a particular drug. BRCA1 downregulation restored growth after crisis but produced multinucleation (MED21792894). Prometaphase lasting **>1.5 hours** could lead to durable daughter-cell G1 arrest despite completion of maternal division (MED20832310). FZR1-deficient oocytes completed meiosis I faster but had **25% nondisjunction** despite an intact checkpoint (MED22918942). These actual outcomes prevent earlier completion or greater growth from being treated as synonymous with faithful, sustained division.

CENP-E, Kif2a, Cdc20, Parkin and Aurora perturbations retain their distinct adverse segregation, cytokinesis, genome and cell-cycle effects. Bleomycin is a fibrosis inducer in MED38343538, nocodazole an arrest/challenge agent where indicated, BrdU/EdU assay labels, and radiation a genotoxic exposure. Computational cell-cycle regression and statistical predictive improvement are analytic outcomes rather than treatment effects.

Full mode replays both original corpora,32 raw reading logs and two audit logs. A fresh repository-only copy passes structural mode without private corpora or logs. Meaningful altered fixtures are rejected for prior-record overlap, absent end-marker observation, a truncated raw log despite consistently recomputed hashes, and dropped retraction metadata despite recomputed object hashes/counters.
