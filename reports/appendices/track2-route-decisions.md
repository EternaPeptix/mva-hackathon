# Track 2 route decisions and reopening conditions

**Research review, 21 September 2026.** This appendix supports the [main report](../jeweled_track2_report_submission.md) and [prospective evidence pipeline](track2-evidence-pipeline.md). It records the considered routes, including those declined as submission leads. We performed no drug experiment. “Reserve” means a specified missing result could justify promotion; “decline lead” is a prioritization decision, not experimental falsification of an entire drug class.

The elected research pair is **niacin for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. Niacin has the closer gene-mechanism bridge; apitegromab has stronger randomized human motor-function evidence in a different disease. **Everolimus remains a conditional mTOR mechanism probe**, outside the core confirmation family unless prospectively promoted. The [broad comparison](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md) supports this change; no candidate has demonstrated superiority or rescue of Leu737Ter/Asn1002Lys. Missing actual-allele evidence applies equally to all candidates and does not establish that no competitive hypothesis exists. Selective senescent-cell clearance remains a strong same-gene genetic functional precedent to qualify in parallel; dasatinib and dasatinib–quercetin remain drug reserves.

A subsequent [catalog-independent intervention comparison](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md#interventions-recovered-beyond-the-catalog-name-filter) recovers 7,8-DHF, melatonin, folate, EUK-207, a Phyllanthus preparation, LiCl and pifithrin experiments outside or behind the clinical-name filter. 7,8-DHF merits an experimental chromosome-fidelity comparison; none currently displaces the elected pair for the muscle proposal. The table gives a reason for each decision and preserves unreviewed literature as unknown.

Every promotion requires pharmacologically justified exposure and **greater absolute muscle output in at least one functional cohort, with preserved output in the other cohort, renewable progenitors and chromosome fidelity**. Restoration additionally requires improved function of the relevant BUBR1 product; a downstream benefit can remain useful without correcting the initiating chromosome-distribution defect. A pathway marker, higher protein abundance, a higher surviving normal-cell percentage or fewer divisions alone cannot meet this standard. Full confirmation and multiple-comparison rules are in the pipeline. An inadequate assay is not a resolved drug failure.

**Genetic stop:** confirmed cis `[L737Ter;N1002K]/WT` retires the proposed
compound-heterozygous explanation. The current biallelic-MVA1 premise stops.
A newly demonstrated pathogenic allele in trans or a separate dominant mechanism
would require a new causal justification; neither restores these two variants as
a compound-heterozygous pair.

The [catalog and comparative assessment](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md) expanded the route set and changed the second nomination. These are research priorities, not measured efficacy estimates or clinical recommendations. The pipeline's four core claims are niacin/apitegromab × progenitor/mature-muscle benefit; conditional probes remain separately prespecified exploratory work until promoted.

### Why a C-terminal truncation cannot inherit historical rescue

The [2010 MVA study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20516114/) used a splice-associated **S714fsX731** construct, not Leu737Ter. Ectopic expression after depletion supported checkpoint arrest and several chromosome-alignment/segregation endpoints. Its cDNA expression bypassed endogenous RNA surveillance. Reduced RNA in a heterozygous 731X/WT line supported an NMD interpretation, but was not a direct drug reversal of allele-specific NMD.

A [2020 study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108397) found that a 731X truncation and selected pseudokinase-domain changes impaired phosphorylation of the KARD recruitment region, B56 recruitment, checkpoint silencing and alignment under its assays. Similar kinetochore abundance did not restore these functions. The C-terminal domain can therefore matter structurally even when catalytic kinase activity is dispensable. Different constructs, expression conditions and endpoints may explain differences; **the 2010 and 2020 findings have not been directly reconciled**. They cannot establish the function of the actual 1–736 product or N1002K.

There is also a local-function caveat to stabilization. K668, the acetylation site implicated in SIRT2-dependent turnover, lies within KARD residues 665–682, adjacent to the B56-binding core. In [Wang 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4930772/), a 647–720 fragment bearing K668A bound B56γ1 somewhat less tightly than WT (Kd about 5.65 versus 2.91 µM); L669A, I672A or E674A abolished detectable binding. Alanine substitution is not acetylation or a drug experiment. Protein abundance and bulk binding cannot replace measurements of recruitment at chromosomes and proper checkpoint exit.

[Patent Example 11](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2015116735A1/en) reports reduced adipose markers in BubR1 hypomorphs. | The patent supplies no functional rescue, sample sizes or doses; its p16 result was not conventionally significant. Dungan's young-mouse regeneration finding and [2026 oligodendrocyte/demyelination findings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843680/) counter any assumption of universally beneficial clearance. Those are contextual mouse harms, not MVA outcomes. | Satisfy the same causal and functional requirements, demonstrate why the combination beats its components, and verify nominated formulation/approval jurisdiction. A [Ukrainian manufacturer lists quercetin 40 mg as a medicine](https://bcpp.com.ua/en/product/kvertin); a global “quercetin is not approved” claim is unjustified, while exact submission eligibility remains unverified. |
| **Chloroquine / hydroxychloroquine / autophagy blockade — decline lead** | [Tang 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3532042/) found chloroquine-sensitive trisomic fibroblasts. [Goutas 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10149375/) found increased BUBR1 with hydroxychloroquine in late-passage WJ-MSCs. | Tang's BUBR1-low experiment tested AICAR/17-AAG, not chloroquine; chloroquine did not suppress the tested human CIN/MIN lines. Goutas measured abundance, not restored function, at high culture exposure. [Chloroquine](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f4ac1556-0522-76e3-e053-2a95a90a5ba8) and [hydroxychloroquine](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f2002851-852f-4ee8-e053-2a95a90a242d) labels warn about muscle weakness/myopathy, particularly relevant to this objective. | An exposure-qualified, actual-allele experiment must establish a favorable functional window and no loss of mature-muscle performance or renewal. Neither trisomic-cell killing nor abundance alone reopens the lead. |
| **Metformin / AICAR / selective energy stress — decline lead** | Tang reported weaker inhibition of trisomy-13/16 fibroblast accumulation by metformin than by AICAR, suggesting context-specific metabolic vulnerability. | Antiproliferation is not muscle rescue. [MASTERS randomized trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6826125/) found metformin blunted resistance-training muscle hypertrophy in older adults. That result does not settle pediatric MVA, but opposes an assumed anabolic benefit. AICAR reference experiments cannot be assigned to metformin. | Demonstrate a useful selective or downstream effect in relevant muscle, at justified exposure, with greater absolute output and preserved repair. A slower-growing culture cannot count as correction. |
| **N-acetylcysteine (NAC) — redox reserve, decline lead** | [Aneuploid fibroblast stress experiments](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.101) provide a protective precedent under a particular imposed oxidant stress. | No demonstrated BUB1B-dependent redox cause or rescue. The [randomized RYR1-myopathy trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7274912/) failed its primary oxidative-stress endpoint and did not establish a functional benefit. It is a different myopathy but stronger than a generic antioxidant rationale. | Establish causal oxidative injury in the actual model, qualified exposure and increased useful muscle output. Control assay interference; a lower fluorescent ROS signal cannot stand in for benefit. |
| **Elamipretide — mitochondrial-function reserve, decline lead** | [FDA accelerated approval in 2025](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/215244s000lbl.pdf) for muscle strength in Barth syndrome at body weight ≥30 kg provides a drug/tissue precedent. | [Randomized Barth primary endpoints](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41436-020-01006-8) were not met. No causally established BUB1B mitochondrial lesion or rescue. Approval population and surrogate basis do not transfer to MVA. | Show a relevant, reversible mitochondrial defect and durable absolute muscle benefit at justified exposure; qualify formulation and age/weight applicability separately. |

## Interpretation and translation limits

Confirmed cis retires the proposed compound-heterozygous explanation under the genetic stop above. Apparent total-protein abundance alone does not establish functional competence; timing and localization remain mechanistic questions, but cannot override that genetic stop. Uncertain phase does not permit calling an engineered model patient-equivalent. Normal NAD does not exclude a useful NAD-related effect; higher NAD cannot establish one. Missing evidence is recorded as missing, rather than converted into experimental rejection.

The broad review changed the elected pair and sharpened the conditions for reserves. Other-disease failure does not prove failure in MVA, and positive evidence from another mutation does not prove benefit here. Current approval for another indication establishes a medication's status only within that formulation, jurisdiction and population. This appendix does not create a clinical avoid-list. The mecasermin malignancy contraindication is a specific label boundary, distinct from the many untested hypotheses.

Before translating any positive experiment, the [oncology review](track2-evidence-pipeline.md#rhabdomyosarcoma-evidence-and-treatment-compatibility) must address actual tumor status, prior/current therapy and relevant combination effects. Limited everolimus reports and mixed temsirolimus trial outcomes cannot establish cancer protection. Neither nephrocalcinosis nor failure to thrive alone supplies missing filtration or endocrine measurements. No pediatric dose is inferred from a culture concentration, animal injection, adult trial or unrelated pediatric indication.
