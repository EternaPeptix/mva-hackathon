# Astaxanthin: qualified human-function/compensation reserve

**Source qualification, 27 September 2026 UTC.** Astaxanthin is a **qualified human-function/compensation reserve**, supported by evidence beyond the earlier culture-only lead. The provisional niacin/restoration and apitegromab/compensation nominations are unchanged. This is a research comparison, not clinical advice.

The [existing composite-hydrogel qualification](astaxanthin-qualification.md) remains a separate, unchanged source appraisal: culture-formulation benefits do not establish isolated-astaxanthin effects or BUBR1 restoration. The human evidence below supports a different functional rationale.

The Liu aged-wild-type-mouse pilot additionally reported higher post-training specific force (p=.004), but absolute force was null (p=.12); it is not an MVA model.

**Qualifications:** neither retrospective [Liu registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03368872) nor [Tsao registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06593535) establishes prospective hierarchy. Nakanishi’s 23/24 denominator, reversed walking sentence and safety-unit errors remain unresolved. Harms reporting is incomplete; taekwondo injury withdrawals have unspecified attribution. Industry support matters: Astavita funded/designed Liu work; BioReal/AstaReal provided counterstudy products. Nakanishi/Tsao/Zhang declare public/university funding; Bloomer funding remains unverified. No formal notice was identified by the packets’ bounded checks; publisher/access gaps and internal errors remain, not certified away.

**Competitiveness:** randomized human performance signals justify a compensation reserve alongside the culture lead. They do not establish consistent strength benefit, transfer unmatched-mixture effects to isolated astaxanthin, or warrant replacing niacin/restoration or apitegromab/compensation nominees. Compare population relevance, controlled contrast and endpoint directness separately; no cross-trial effect-size ranking or MVA efficacy follows. Missing BUBR1 assays alone do not negate useful functional evidence.

**Scope:** bounded primary-source synthesis, not exhaustive coverage, biological replication or regimen advice. Study amounts identify exposures. Cohorts are deduplicated; no independent replication is inferred. The companion JSON records formulation/outcome/depth limits, source URLs and hashes of accepted external packets; raw sources remain external and unchanged.
