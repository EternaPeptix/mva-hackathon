# 10-HDA: a lymphoma-cell viability comparator, not demonstrated BUBR1 restoration

**23 September 2026.** Retain the measured cell-assay positive from [PMID 39204193](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11357620/), but deprioritize this source as a restoration or muscle-compensation lead. The actual administered material is named **10-hydroxy-2-decenoic acid**, not royal jelly. Supplier, purity, lot and independent chemical authentication are not given in the read main paper. Figure 1's wider application labels are background, not outcomes of this experiment.

## What was measured

SU-DHL-2 lymphoma cells received nominal 250–1000 µg/mL 10-HDA for 24 hours. The reported **IC50 is 496.8 µg/mL**, at a stated 3×10⁶ cells/well in 96-well plates. The endpoint is reduced CCK-8 OD450, converted by the authors to “cell death.” This supports metabolic/viability suppression, not direct death counting or demonstrated apoptosis. The higher-dose response plateaus. No standard antitumor positive control was included.

The HSF fibroblast comparator remains a useful positive feature: smaller assay changes and no 50% inhibition reached in the displayed range. However, its generic identity/donor/authentication details are not supplied. The overall reported ANOVA is nonsignificant (F(5,12)=2.473, P=.0923), while Figure 2C marks 1000 µg/mL with an asterisk. This does not justify declaring all doses nontoxic.

LO2 showed increased CCK-8 signal at lower doses and suppression at higher doses, with a reported IC50 near 1000 µg/mL. **This is not a normal-hepatocyte selectivity window:** [Cellosaurus L-02/LO2](https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_6926) identifies it as a HeLa derivative, ICLAC-00575. Increased assay signal is also not a measured proliferation or liver-repair endpoint. The authors' stock was not independently authenticated here.

## BUB1B and mechanism

Figure 6 and Results explicitly show **lower BUB1B RNA**, measured by selected-gene RT-PCR with β-actin reference. Table 3 supplies primers. No exact BUB1B fold, gene-specific P/FDR or significance marker is supplied, so no numerical value is digitized from the graph. The PCR subsection does not independently restate treatment dose/time; the IC50/24-hour regimen is explicit for the proteomics arm.

A validated **BUBR1 protein direction is not established**. The authors themselves note discordance between selected RNA and protein findings. No BUBR1-specific binding, occupancy, Western validation, genetic mediation, checkpoint, chromosome-fidelity or muscle-function experiment is supplied. The complement/coagulation pathway enrichment (adjusted P=.0124) is an exploratory pathway statistic, not a BUB1B result or causal mechanism. Protein-identification FDR and nominal differential-expression tests are separate; the former does not correct the latter.

All experiments are described as triplicate with mean±SEM, but biological preparations versus technical wells are not resolved. No raw-data replication was performed.

## Reporting and transfer limits

The figures preserve several unresolved discrepancies:

- Methods/Results specify **750 µg/mL**, while Figure 2A labels **800 µg/mL**.
- Text calls 250 µg/mL significant; Figure 2A gives it no asterisk.
- The stated stock, **50 mg in 100 µL ethanol**, implies **0.2% ethanol at 1000 µg/mL**, whereas the paper calls **0.02%** a matching solvent control. This tenfold arithmetic discrepancy needs clarification; it does not prove ethanol caused the observed effect.

The paper reports repeated **failure to establish its xenograft model**, not successful animal antitumor efficacy or a treatment null. Royal-jelly/10-HDA plus R-CHOP is only proposed; no combination or synergy experiment was performed. The current results establish neither useful normal-cell rescue nor human exposure, royal-jelly efficacy/safety or a clinical regimen.

Different model alone does not exclude a challenger: niacin and apitegromab also lack established MVA rescue. Here, however, the actual compound result is lymphoma viability suppression with BUB1B RNA lowering, with no protein-restoration or muscle-output bridge. That supports an **in-vitro anticancer comparator**, not displacement of the provisional nominees. It also does not demonstrate harm in normal BUB1B-deficient tissue.
