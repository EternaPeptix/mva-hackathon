# Metformin geriatric-function follow-up: access-limited qualification

**Reported functional positives are retained, but their direct randomized contrasts remain unverified.** This assessment covers the complete original abstract of [Qaisar et al., PMID 39084174](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39084174/) and available indexed [publisher preview excerpts](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167494324002632). It does not qualify the complete scientific main, native tables/figures, outcome captions, supplement or protocol/SAP. Direct publisher main/PDF requests returned 403 HTML; the public Elsevier XML endpoint supplied metadata only, and its full-view request required authentication. No access controls were bypassed, purchase made or author contacted.

## Population, exposure and analysis population

The primary Methods excerpt describes 220 non-diabetic men assessed, 72 excluded and **148 randomized by a simple method: 77 placebo and 71 metformin**. The abstract instead reports **70 placebo and 62 metformin**, with ages 73.1 ± 4.2 and 70.1 ± 4.5 years. The resulting differences of seven and nine participants are arithmetic, not verified withdrawals or an established per-protocol population. Reasons, endpoint-specific denominators, missing-data handling and allocation concealment remain unread. PubMed indexes double blinding, but its implementation is not verified from the available Methods.

The abstract literally states **1700 mg twice daily for 16 weeks**. That wording arithmetically implies 3400 mg/day; it is not independently verified exposure or a regimen recommendation. It must not be silently rewritten to 1700 mg/day or 850 mg twice daily. Exact route, formulation, salt, release type, manufacturer, titration, adherence and placebo composition remain unresolved.

## Outcomes and mechanism

The abstract reports improved handgrip strength, gait speed and cumulative SPPB with metformin, each P<.05, without corresponding placebo observations. It explicitly describes CAF22 and NfL reductions relative to baseline and reports oxidative-stress reduction. These favorable observations remain part of the source record. **No direct treatment-minus-control estimate, confidence interval, exact functional test or interaction is recovered.** A difference in within-group significance does not establish a between-group effect, but these access limits also do not establish a null result. The statistical section is unavailable; paired-test methods from other Qaisar papers must not be assigned to this trial.

The preview reports no age/body-composition differences between groups at the observed timepoints. Table 1, its definitions and the full outcome inventory were not obtained, so this is bounded narrative evidence, not a verified muscle-mass null. Endpoint hierarchy, power assumptions, multiplicity and the individual SPPB components remain unresolved.

CAF22 correlations with function and changes in circulating CAF22/NfL **do not establish NMJ mediation**. The accessible text supplies neither a verified formal mediation analysis nor a direct NMJ structural or transmission measurement. The unavailable main cannot be assumed to contain no additional analyses. Biomarker associations are not BUBR1 restoration, neuronal renewal or chromosome-fidelity evidence.

## Safety, planning and access

The target publisher excerpt reports **five metformin participants with metallic taste (8.06%)**; the next event is truncated. Other event counts found in secondary-review search results were not adopted. Placebo events, unique affected-person totals, serious events, withdrawals and systematic safety methods remain unknown. This is not evidence of no harm.

No matching registry or original protocol was identified in the bounded search. A PMID query returned zero records; an author/preparation query returned **NCT07788378**, excluded because it is a different Fudan observational diabetes study by institution, population and design. Its 2024 administrative study-start field coexists with a retrospective 2018–2023 hospital population and is not verified first-participant chronology. This does not prove the target was unregistered. The paper’s cited geriatric quality-of-life report has unresolved cohort overlap and receives no independent-study credit here.

The available publisher declaration states no conflicts and identifies University of Sharjah grant **22010901121**. Its ethics excerpt names the Burns and Plastic Surgery Center committee in Peshawar; approval date/identifier were not obtained. No article-specific correction was identified in the bounded metadata/search check, without an exhaustive notice-history claim.
