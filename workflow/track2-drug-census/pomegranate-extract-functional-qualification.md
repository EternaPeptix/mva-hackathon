# POMx pomegranate extract: abstract-qualified strength-recovery evidence

**Retain a preparation-specific functional research reserve at original-abstract depth.** The source reports better measured isometric strength recovery with POMx than placebo at 48 and 72 hours after damaging exercise. The complete scientific main and native results were unavailable through the bounded lawful routes. That limits methodological confirmation; it is not negative efficacy evidence. This preparation-specific assessment leaves the provisional nominations unchanged and does not establish clinical efficacy.

## Direct original finding

[Trombold et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19952825/) (*Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* 42:493–498; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e3181b64edd)) identifies **Wonderful-variety pomegranate extract, POMx**. Recreationally active men were randomized to a crossover with POMx and placebo, each given during a nine-day period. The challenge was two sets of 20 maximal eccentric elbow-flexion exercises with one arm. The abstract reports measurements of maximal isometric strength, soreness, CK, myoglobin, IL-6 and CRP at baseline and 2, 24, 48, 72 and 96 hours after exercise.

| Reported direct treatment comparison | POMx strength, % baseline | Placebo strength, % baseline | Reported P |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 48 hours | 85.4 ± 2.5 | 78.3 ± 2.6 | .01 |
| 72 hours | 88.9 ± 2.0 | 84.0 ± 2.0 | .009 |

The arithmetic differences are **7.1 and 4.9 percentage points**. They are not absolute-force gains, relative percentage improvements or published treatment-effect confidence intervals. The abstract does not define the ± dispersion, statistical model, paired-analysis implementation or multiplicity handling. The displayed P values must not be relabeled as corrected or uncorrected tests.

The abstract describes similar early strength reduction to about 72% of baseline at two hours and incomplete recovery to about 91% at 96 hours. It does not supply formal equivalence tests or exact pairwise null results for those times. Soreness has no quantified abstract result. The statement that serum markers did not explain possible mechanisms cannot be turned into a null result for every biomarker. The evidence supports a time-specific functional signal, not complete recovery, demonstrated structural muscle protection, reduced pain or durable strength gain.

## Preparation and methods limits

The source labels an extract; it does not provide its administered mass or volume, standardization, carrier, supplier/batch, or placebo composition and matching. Its title and rationale concern ellagitannins, but the abstract does not isolate their contribution from the whole preparation. **POMx is not an alias for POM Wonderful juice, generic pomegranate, isolated ellagitannins, ellagic acid or urolithin A**, and current retail equivalence is unverified.

Randomized, treated, completer and assay denominators are unknown from the original abstract. Sequence generation, concealment, detailed masking, washout, contralateral-arm assignment, attrition, adherence, baseline timing relative to supplementation, period/carryover analysis, prospective primary hierarchy and registration also remain unknown. The source's nine-day treatment-period description is not a reconstructed regimen recommendation.

Do not import **16 participants, double blinding, a 14-day washout or dosing details** from Lamb's background description, manufacturer material or reviews as verified original Methods. Neither an assumed washout nor a crossover label would by itself establish absence of repeated-bout or cross-limb effects.

Adverse-event counts, tolerability, sponsor identity and conflicts are not supplied by the original abstract. PubMed's “Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't” indexing does not identify the funder, amount or sponsor role. These omissions establish neither harm nor safety, and do not establish absence of commercial involvement.

## Distinct juice counterevidence

The separately full-qualified [Lamb 2019 trial](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11071593) tested **POM Wonderful undiluted juice**, not the source-labeled POMx extract. Its preparation, native outcomes, missing assays, omnibus tests, chemistry and reporting limitations are retained in the [cherry blend functional qualification](cherry-blend-functional-qualification.md) and its [structured evidence](cherry-blend-functional-qualification.json).

That parallel trial allocated 12 men per arm and reports strength n=11 per arm. Mean post-exercise strength losses were 19.4% with pomegranate juice and 17.3% with placebo. Group P=.77 and group×time P=.91 are **three-group omnibus tests**, not published pairwise juice–placebo tests; a pairwise confidence interval is not supplied. These results provide contextual functional counterevidence without establishing equivalence or disproving the distinct extract result. The POM Wonderful arm belongs to the already reviewed 2019 cohort, not a new cohort created by citing it here.

Different preparations and incompletely verified 2010 methods prevent product equivalence, pooled effect estimates, dose-response explanations or cross-trial potency ranking. The broader preparation class does not inherit either study's result.
