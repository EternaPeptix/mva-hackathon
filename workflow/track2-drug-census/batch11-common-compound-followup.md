Vitamin D2's supplementary change-score analysis differs from the main final-score analysis, including mental quality-of-life statistical status. The reported infection risk-difference CI excludes zero while its P=.313; that conflict supplies no infection-benefit credit. Current registration predates enrollment, but original historical fields and prospective multiplicity decisions were not recovered. No numerical result was supplied for every registry/protocol safety category.

Inulin's MAR imputation does not establish that missingness was random or remove possible temporal confounding. GLP-1 analyses were exploratory; nominal correlations failed FDR and do not establish mediation. Registry-versus-graphic raw changes are distinct from the adjusted +4.62 kg treatment contrast.

**Competitive decision:** calcium-HMB remains a credible common-supplement functional reserve in the targeted HMB/inulin comparison. Inulin retains a useful secondary grip signal with task-test nulls. Vitamin D2 is a conditional deficiency/malabsorption route, with unresolved exposure fidelity. These are research priorities based on the source populations and endpoints, not an MVA-efficacy ranking. Niacin remains a restoration hypothesis, NMN a fair mechanistic benchmark and apitegromab a downstream functional comparator; none has demonstrated MVA superiority. Muscle mass, biomarkers and static morphology do not establish productive renewal or chromosome fidelity.
