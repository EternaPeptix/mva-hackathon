# Astaxanthin: controlled cycling signals and a limited torque report

**27 September 2026 UTC.** Three additional primary reports strengthen the case for retaining astaxanthin as a **human-function research reserve**. They do not settle its competitiveness for MVA. Read alongside the [earlier human evidence and counterevidence](astaxanthin-functional-qualification.md) and [separate composite-culture appraisal](astaxanthin-qualification.md); neither is superseded.

| Source, design and preparation | What the comparison actually establishes | Limitations and reading depth |
|---|---|---|
| [Earnest 2011](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0031-1280779): parallel double-blind trial, 21 trained men randomized; reported 7/7 retained. Four weeks of 80 mg algal extract providing 4 mg astaxanthin plus 120 mg MCT, versus 200 mg MCT and caramel. | Twenty-kilometre time improved more with the active preparation, **between-group change P<.02**. Arm changes were −121 and −19 seconds. Active participants were not faster at the absolute endpoint; improvement is the reported advantage. | Substantial exclusions; two outcome outliers are not fully reconciled with flow categories. Published intervals describe **within-arm change**, not treatment-difference precision. Full scientific text/captions read; native graphs unavailable. |
| [Brown 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsams.2020.06.017): randomized double-blind crossover, 12 recreationally trained men; AstaReal 12 mg/day for seven days, 14-day washout, appearance-matched placebo. Complete composition not reported. | Self-paced 40-km time was **51 seconds faster**, 95% CI **6–96 seconds**, paired P=.029; mean power +6.1 W, CI 0.1–12.1, P=.047. This is a controlled within-person effect, not within-arm significance. | Small cohort, no endpoint-specific denominator table or prospectively verified hierarchy. Uptake and washout were not biochemically verified. Full accepted main and native Table 1/Figures 1–2 read. Online-2020 and issue-2021 citations are **one report**. |
| [Wang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scispo.2025.06.003): abstract describes 32 moderately trained men randomized across placebo and 6/12/24 mg/day for four weeks. Actual formulation and arm/analyzed counts unavailable. | Retain the reported selected knee-extension peak-torque signal at **180°/s in the 12-mg arm**, alongside CK/soreness findings. The 24-mg arm has reported biomarker positives without a clear strength benefit. | **Publisher abstract only**: no numerical torque effect, CI/P, significant timepoint, native contrast or multiplicity methods. A significant result at 12 mg and a nonsignificant result at 24 mg do **not** establish dose superiority or an optimum. |

Earnest's power change was significant within the active arm, but a direct power-superiority P value was not independently verified. Its reported active power means imply a change different from the stated +20 W; the abstract and Figure 3 text also give different time-change interval bounds. Those issues are retained without discarding the separately explicit time contrast.

Brown's fat-oxidation signal concerns the **final kilometre**, not the entire trial or an established mediator of performance. The printed condition SDs (.04/.05) cannot yield the printed difference SD .13; native error bars also differ from the Results dispersions. No value was repaired. This reporting issue is separate from the directly reported time/power contrasts. Performance-order tests were nonpositive, which does not establish absence of carryover.

Harms reporting is incomplete across the reports. Earnest was funded by Gatorade, with Fuji-donated capsules; Brown reports AstaReal doctoral-project funding and professional relationships. Wang's full declarations were inaccessible. Absence of a located registration or correction is not proof of absence.

**Competitive decision:** repeated controlled performance signals deserve credit, including Brown's interpretable paired effect interval. The [trained-cyclist null and other functional nulls](astaxanthin-functional-qualification.md) remain part of the assessment; preparations, populations and tasks differ. These are distinct cohorts, but not replications of one common protocol or proof of reproducible MVA rescue. No cross-trial ranking against niacin or apitegromab is justified. A relevant model must demonstrate useful function and chromosome/renewal safety; BUBR1 engagement is necessary only for a claim of protein restoration. Nominees remain provisional and unchanged.

Study amounts identify exposures, not regimens. The companion JSON preserves source-specific findings, reading depth and accepted external source/review hashes. Raw papers remain outside Git.
