# Recovery of the 19 title-only gene records

**21 September 2026.** Further source retrieval recovered **nine complete short
bodies and one complete patent abstract**, with two partial texts and seven
records still limited to titles/metadata. Each record has a source link,
interpretation and access limit in [the supplemental assessment](missing-records-followup.json).
All 19 received an access assessment; they were not all fully read.

Together with the nine bodies previously reviewed, the 28 records originally
missing a saved abstract now have this evidence depth:

| Current depth | Records | What the count means |
|---|---:|---|
| Complete accessible short body | 18 | Mostly corrections, commentaries and news; not 18 new experiments |
| Complete newly recovered abstract | 1 | Antibody patent; all claims also read, description only partly read |
| Partial body/preview | 2 | Cannot support a negative full-text conclusion |
| Title/metadata only | 7 | Scientific content still unverified |

The original corpus, 1,432 saved-abstract assessments and their historical reading
depth are unchanged. This supplemental layer is tied to those identities; it
does not silently turn an inaccessible original record into a reviewed paper.

## What this resolves

- The SIRT2 commentary reiterates **genetic** survival/cardiac evidence. It is
  not another drug experiment supporting niacin or NMN survival benefit.
  [Commentary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194083/).
- The antibody patent concerns acetylated-BUBR1 detection and analytical kits,
  not administration as an MVA therapy. The translated abstract and claims were
  read; the complete patent description was not.
  [Patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20110019495A/en).
- Two oocyte commentaries discuss genetic checkpoint experiments. Nocodazole
  is a checkpoint-disruption probe, not rescue. Their publisher PDFs bundle
  other articles, whose drug passages were not assigned to these records.
  [Cdh1 commentary](https://doi.org/10.4161/cc.9.6.11181),
  [meiosis commentary](https://doi.org/10.4161/cc.9.8.11405).
- Figure corrections qualify the original sumoylation and caspase-cleavage
  studies; they add no independent treatment result. A TAp73 correction changes
  an author spelling only.
  [Sumoylation correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4697180/),
  [cleavage correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1820460/),
  [TAp73 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6140530/).
- A recovered paragraph from the 2026 pancreatic-cancer corrigendum reports an
  incorrect Figure 7T image outside the study dataset and its replacement. The
  complete notice and replacement image remain unverified; the earlier 2025
  corrigendum remains title-only. Do not assume either is inconsequential.
  [2026 notice](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cellsig.2026.112467),
  [2025 notice](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cellsig.2025.112087).

No newly verified restorative drug experiment emerged from these recovered
texts. That result applies to the recovered content only, not to the seven
unavailable bodies or every source in the larger search.
