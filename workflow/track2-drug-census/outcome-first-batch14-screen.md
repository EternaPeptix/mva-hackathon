## Adjudications

Five first-pass routes changed. Defined ingested ice slurry (order 6) fits the administered-drink lane despite a thermal intervention and nonpositive functional results. Actual viral-vector administration (58) fits preclinical biological-preparation function, without evidence for purified hydrolase or gene-only manipulation. The diagnostic D3-creatine tracer within an exercise/vest study (77) is context; its pooled grip change is not creatine efficacy. Shared carbohydrate/amino-acid intake in both exercise-intensity arms (111) supplies uncontrolled preparation evidence, with the randomized exercise comparison retained separately. The abstractless letter (48) remains unresolved under the missing-source rule; the title establishes a candidate letter role, not absence of original data.

Evoked first-twitch contraction recovery (47) remains a narrow human physiological-function observation. Preserve the independent mechanism-only interpretation: ratio normalization cannot establish voluntary strength or complete twitch recovery. Diagnostic sestamibi uptake (53) remains human nonfunctional exposure evidence; electrical stimulation supplies its treatment contrast. The root’s initial context interpretation is retained and corrected.

Orders 115, 123, 129 and 137 keep unresolved bowel, ocular or mental-fatigue scope, with the independent alternative lanes preserved. Known administration, comparator and symptoms are retained. These taxonomy limits do not make the preparations scientifically ineffective.
