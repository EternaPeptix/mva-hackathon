The explicit food-overlay reconciliation admits **64 unique records once** from the remaining outer queue: 26 previously read literal-food records plus 40 common-name records, minus the two shared IDs **MED:21753893 and MED:37096022**. The original 3,598 selection objects and all 1,210 prior acceptances remain unchanged. The separate package gives **1,274 cumulative accepted revalidations, 3,435 outer-ledger records and 2,324 pending records**. This reconciliation is integrated separately from the two reading overlays.

This is admission of existing reading evidence, not a new reading batch: **zero new literal-reading or appraisal credit** is claimed. Both food overlays retain zero automatic outer credit. MED:18082453 has no saved abstract and is excluded. Each admitted record preserves its original selection fields and points to exact source, review-file, annotation-object, metadata and reading-evidence hashes; scientific annotations and raw abstracts are not duplicated.

The root declared completion of **12 predetermined spot-checks**, six from the 26 literal-food pending IDs and six from the 38 common-only pending IDs. Annotation comparisons were declared complete in `cb0737` and `2840bd`, with no substantive correction. This is a bounded sample gate, not an independent scientific review of every admitted source. Structural checks cannot prove actual viewing.

The verifier retains the existing batch08 baseline gate, pins the original selection and prior-review files, recomputes the queue intersection, and rejects duplicate admissions, changed source/annotation hashes, missing per-record credit or reading evidence, and overlap with earlier acceptances. Compact checks use the repository's metadata-compacted literal-food review; full replay verifies its unchanged annotations against the external original and checks all referenced literal logs against the corpus. The common review is already compact and is referenced byte-for-byte.

`verify_food_admission.py` exports `check(root=...)` for repository integration. Its CLI supports `--root`, `--admission`, `--food-review` and `--common-review`; `--work-root` plus `--corpus` enables full food-source replay. The default common-review path in a copied repository is `food-substances/common-abstract-review.json`. The work root is the directory containing the two external food-review folders.

No source is promoted to demonstrated MVA efficacy by this reconciliation. Publication warnings, derivatives, mixtures, assay reagents, actual functional positives and nulls remain in the referenced original annotations.
