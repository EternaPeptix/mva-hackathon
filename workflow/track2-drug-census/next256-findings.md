## Findings that warrant further work

Mebendazole's reported YTHDC1-associated liver benefit is the strongest new
other-tissue pharmacology question in this batch. Monastrol supplies another
lesion-specific spindle observation, while several other studies show why
protein abundance, arrest, morphology and completed accurate divisions must be
kept separate. None of these abstract-stage observations establishes MVA benefit.

## Primary qualification changes the mebendazole inference

The [full source check](mebendazole-qualification.md) credits higher YTHDC1
protein and improved HFD-mouse liver pathology, lipids and injury markers.
Thermal/protease resistance supports target engagement. It does not establish
that mebendazole reverses the protein's lactylation/ubiquitination or restores
its half-life or reader function. The supplement compares two treated genotypes
without concurrent knockout plus vehicle; it cannot show abolished drug benefit
within knockout mice. This is a real other-tissue protein-availability lead,
with no demonstrated muscle benefit or reason yet to replace the current pair.
The ledger held **104 route/compound appraisals** at that stage. The table above preserves
the original abstract questions; this follow-up does not add another reading.
