Shoden enrolled 57 and completed 43. Its reported ITT/LOCF analysis does not account clearly for all randomized participants; symptom sequence counts and hormone exclusions are unresolved. After withdrawal, DHEA-S fell significantly while testosterone did not, so the broad claim that both effects were unsustained exceeds the reported testosterone test. A separate reviewer confirmed the tablet-mass/glycoside distinction and native Tables 2–3. It did not reread the entire main. No scientific supplement was present in the recovered source assets; registry fields/history, protocol/SAP and participant data remain unavailable.

The [same competitive criteria](../../reports/appendices/common-compound-priorities.md) apply to incumbents: niacin has open-label human function and NAD engagement, NMN has a direct compound-to-wild-type-BUBR1 bridge with mixed human function, and apitegromab has controlled pediatric SMA motor evidence with hierarchy and safety limits. None establishes BUB1B/MVA rescue. These three preparations do not gain priority from biomarker or shared-training changes, and their nonpositive findings do not establish a global efficacy ordering.
