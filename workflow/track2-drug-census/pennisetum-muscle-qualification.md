# Pennisetum extract: final source qualification

The paper reports a nominal grip group-by-time interaction (P<.05), while visit-level grip contrasts, walking and controlled mass comparisons remain nonsignificant. Keep each statistical question separate. The current registry says NON_RANDOMIZED matched pairs, conflicting with the paper’s randomization claim. This limits causal certainty without erasing the observed comparative function measurements.

The preparation is a hot-water, concentrated, freeze-dried botanical extract supplied by Natural Keeper, reported as 300 mg active powder three times daily for 12 weeks. Its botanical wording, chemical markers, purity and retail equivalence remain unresolved; aliases are empty. This evidence does not establish BUB1B/MVA rescue or superiority to niacin, NMN or apitegromab.
