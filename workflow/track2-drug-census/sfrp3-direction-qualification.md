# sFRP3: the desired direction depends on the starting defect

**22 September 2026.** [Fu and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0049-25.2025)
report useful neural-stem-cell findings in J20 APP mice. This is not a general
instruction to increase sFRP3 in BUBR1 deficiency: the paper contrasts its
excessive-division model with earlier BubR1-hypomorphic models, where division
was deficient and reducing sFRP3 was beneficial. Our earlier
[BUBR1-model qualification](unfiltered-primary-qualification.md) remains intact.

## Keep the administered interventions separate

| Intervention | Observed result | What the experiment does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recombinant sFRP3 protein | Intracerebroventricular infusion reduced the fraction of proliferating neural stem cells in APP mice, versus PBS (Figure 4B, P=.009; 5–8 mice per group). | No protein-arm cognition, long-term pool preservation, muscle or chromosome-fidelity result. |
| AAV-delivered sFRP3 | Retained the stem-cell pool, partially restored dendritic features and produced a spatial-discrimination pattern. | A gene-delivery result cannot be assigned to infused protein or an existing medicine. Immature-neuron counts were not rescued. |
| Levetiracetam | Frzb RNA increased in the selected seizure-responsive APP group (Figure 2E, P=.037). | Two nonresponding APP mice were excluded; this is not an all-treated effect or BUBR1-restoration measurement. |

The protein study used R&D Systems material, without a catalog number, species,
tag or lot. A [current human sFRP3 listing](https://www.rndsystems.com/products/recombinant-human-sfrp-3-protein_192-sf)
shows research supply; it does not identify the administered product. This
protein is not interchangeable with an inhibitor, the FRZB gene or AAV delivery.
Exact historical formulation and a feasible clinical exposure remain unresolved.

## Statistical and endpoint limits

The AAV behavioral result uses paired training-versus-test comparisons within
each group: treated APP P=.010, control APP P=.399. A significant result in one
group and a nonsignificant result in another does not itself demonstrate a
treatment interaction. Retain the behavioral pattern without inventing a
between-treatment effect. Spine analyses include multiple segments per mouse;
handling of clustering is unclear. Semirandom allocation and blinded analysis
are described, without formal randomization. No raw-data reanalysis was done.

Levetiracetam's Figure 2 caption gives an internally inconsistent sample count:
the displayed points and ANOVA degrees of freedom do not support its literal
“36” per group. We do not silently substitute a presumed range. Earlier cited
levetiracetam stem-cell or behavioral findings are not newly replicated by this
RNA comparison. Kainate/pilocarpine are seizure-induction agents here; ancillary
medications do not become therapeutic candidates merely through mention.

## Competitive decision

Add **recombinant sFRP3** as a direction-dependent research reagent and
**levetiracetam** as a conditional seizure-context comparator. The AAV arm is
mechanistic evidence, not a third compound. Neither displaces the provisional
restoration/compensation comparison. Reopening sFRP3 requires an experimentally
demonstrated excess-division/depletion state and appropriate protein direction;
a proliferation fraction alone cannot pass the absolute-output rule.

The main Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion and all six figure captions
were read. Figures 2, 4 and 5 were visually inspected; other downloaded images
were not visually reviewed. The separate supplementary-file request returned
an XML error, not a readable archive. No new independent reviewer or biological
replication is claimed. This qualification adds **zero abstract-reading credit**.
[Structured assessment](sfrp3-direction-qualification.json) retains the
intervention identities, denominators, source hash and unresolved questions.
