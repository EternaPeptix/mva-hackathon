# Additional unfiltered evidence: restoration and compensation

**22 September 2026.** Two readers assessed 512 complete saved abstract strings,
256 each, disjoint, at unfiltered indices 1217–1728. Outer-corpus coverage is now
**2,161 distinct source/ID records**; **3,598 originally saved outer abstracts
remain unread**. These records include reviews, preprints and publication
versions, not 2,161 independent experiments. This pass flags **115 source
questions**, 50 in part A and 65 in part B; a flag is not a qualified medicine.

## What this tranche does to the restore/compensate distinction

The Track 2 pair is two mechanisms before it is two drugs. Niacin proposes to
restore a cellular function upstream of the BUBR1 deficit; apitegromab proposes
to raise useful muscle output while the deficit persists. Everolimus stays a
conditional probe. What these 512 abstracts add is that both mechanisms now have
populated failure modes, and the two failure modes are not the same. That
difference determines what each route's research test has to measure.

Every restoration here that reaches a fidelity-adjacent endpoint delivers the
missing molecule itself. [MAD2L1BP cRNA, PMID37334967](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37334967/)
microinjected into patient oocytes with biallelic variants restored polar-body
extrusion; [K40R tubulin mRNA, PMID30459823](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30459823/)
restored spindle morphology in Kif18a-depleted oocytes;
[FoxM1 induction, PMID30026603](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30026603/)
prevented aneuploidy in aged and progeria fibroblasts. None is a medicine, and
each bypasses the supply chain that a precursor route depends on. The nearest
administered analogue is [EGF, PMID20862286](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20862286/):
continuous supplementation partly restored proliferation and mitotic-regulator
expression in Met-deficient hepatocytes, in culture, while the in-vivo
regeneration deficit stands. Where amount rather than identity was varied,
restoration is two-sided. [USP44 excess, PMID21853124](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21853124/)
strengthened MAD2–CDC20 association and still produced aneuploidy; only a narrow
[p31comet:Mad2 ratio, PMID24131926](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24131926/)
supports viability; both excess and loss of
[Septin7, PMID22895176](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22895176/) harm meiosis.
A restoration claim therefore has to name a target concentration in the deficient
cell, not a direction.

[Niacin, PMID24847987](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24847987/) is the only
administered-niacin skeletal-muscle record in the outer abstracts read so far:
780 versus 30 mg supplemental niacin/kg diet for four weeks in obese Zucker rats,
changing 42 of 259 measured muscle miRNAs. It measures transcript regulation, its
target assignments are computational predictions, and the muscle phenotype it
explains belongs to prior work. That is not a reason to demote the route; it
locates the missing experiment as muscle exposure plus a force endpoint at a
stated dose, which is a more specific requirement than absent MVA data. The same
tranche carries the opposite NAD direction:
[AZD5153 with FK866, PMID35399501](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35399501/)
strengthened growth suppression in hepatocellular carcinoma, with NAPRT falling
and NAMPT rising under BRD4 inhibition, and nicotinic acid and nicotinamide
appear there as pathway context rather than treatments. Pathway membership does
not carry a sign. Cancer-directed suppression of NAD biosynthesis and precursor
supply to a deficient normal tissue are opposite interventions, so the
restoration route must specify cell type, enzyme and direction rather than NAD
support in general.

The compensation side takes its cleanest formal demonstration from genetics.
[Cyclin B3 with a dynein heavy-chain mutation, PMID25486568](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25486568/)
improved fitness and suppressed sterility in MAD1-deficient worms over 470
generations without delaying anaphase onset: organismal output rose and the
checkpoint defect remained. Two cooperating genetic changes cannot be assigned to
one agent, but compensation for an uncorrected checkpoint defect is now a
demonstrated result rather than an assumption. Its failure mode differs from
restoration's. Three records improve a readout by removing what reports or
responds to the defect: [Mad2 suppression, PMID23001180](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23001180/)
overrides persistent-EB1-acetylation arrest without demonstrated corrected
attachment; [p53 deletion, PMID24706806](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24706806/)
rescues acentriolar embryonic apoptosis while centrioles, cilia and Hedgehog
response stay unrestored; [centrinone, PMID27791030](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27791030/)
restores mitotic progression after an artificial Golgi block by depleting
centrosomes. [STIL overexpression, PMID39466849](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39466849/)
states the cost directly: fewer tumors, with microcephaly, perinatal death and a
shorter lifespan. A compensation route has to report what it removed and what
that cost, not only the endpoint it raised.

The model hierarchy does not apply evenly to the two routes.
[Tissue architecture, PMID30146160](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30146160/)
shows that native architecture and integrin function improve merotelic-error
correction, and that epithelial cells missegregate more outside their tissue.
Almost all fidelity evidence in this tranche is cell culture, and culture removes
the context that supports fidelity; almost all compensation evidence is
whole-animal mechanical output measured in situ. At equal nominal depth the two
routes are not equally close to their endpoints, which is an argument against
ranking them on model hierarchy alone.

The everolimus probe is unchanged, for a plain reason. Rapamycin is the only
mTOR-family agent named anywhere in these 512, and it appears twice: as the
chemical dimerization reagent that triggers recruitment in the reconstituted
kinetochore system of [PMID40410156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40410156/),
and as one listed intervention in the
[frailty review, PMID30926563](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30926563/).
Neither is therapeutic evidence in either direction, and the probe cannot inherit
the reconstitution result as an mTOR checkpoint finding.

Three constraints follow, and they bind the Track 2 text rather than the
literature. Direction belongs to the lesion, not to the target: PLK1, SET,
Septin7 and p31comet all harm in both directions, so no candidate is justified by
the phrase restores checkpoint signalling alone. Amount is bounded on both sides,
so a proposed intervention needs a target range and a way to measure where a
treated cell sits in it. Timing is part of the intervention, and Nutlin-3a is the
demonstration: the same administered drug caused marked cardiomyocyte loss early
in human iPSC cardiac differentiation and not later, so an adult or aged-mouse
exposure is not evidence for a growing child. The third constraint is the one
that most changes what a pediatric research test must measure, and it applies to
both nominated routes.

The complete [record-level assessments](outer-unfiltered-next512-review.json)
also retain genetic rescues, negative observations, exposure hazards and

and [ocriplasmin/pamidronate, PMID41588777](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41588777/),
which tested no drug. No unnamed agent is assigned to an approved medicine. The

proTAME is the clearest restore-without-function case in the ledger: the
substrate layer downstream of the lesion is preserved and no corrected division
is shown. Apcin did not reproduce the effect and the combination suppressed it,
so the result is specific to one APC/C interaction and does not transfer between
inhibitors. Monastrol revises the Eg5 route rather than adding a compound. Force
rebalancing is compensation, and here its cost fell on the cells without the
lesion.

Two corrections to the fisetin record should be read with that appraisal, and
they supersede its statement that an exact effective dose cannot be resolved. In
the [2025 aging study, PMID40437670](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40437670/)
the administered regimen was fisetin **100 mg/kg/day** by oral gavage, one week
on, two weeks off, one week on, with functional testing about two weeks after the
final course. Methods print 50 mg/kg/day, but that paragraph reproduces the same
group's [2024 fisetin cohort, PMID38062873](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38062873/)
down to the vehicle, the schedule and the group sizes, and that study states 100
mg/kg/day; 50 mg/kg/day is this paper's ABT-263 dose; and Figure 1A states 100.
Treat 100 mg/kg/day as administered and the Methods value as a transcription
error. The 2025 paper also reports that morphological characteristics for all
groups were published previously, so its muscle outcomes are a new readout on
that earlier cohort rather than an independent replication of it. Old-mouse
forelimb grip was 3.30 ± 0.1 versus 2.90 ± 0.1 g force/g body weight, p=0.0038,
n=30/group. Absolute force was not measured, but Table 2 shows no effect on
soleus, gastrocnemius, tibialis anterior or quadriceps mass, so this is force per
unit mass rather than a denominator artifact, and equally not a mass rescue.

The [progeria arm, PMID36218080](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36218080/) is a
different exposure and a weaker design. Zmpste24-deficient mice were dosed from
four months of age with fisetin 100 mg/kg by oral gavage once weekly for four
weeks, four doses in total, in 10% ethanol/90% PEG-400, against mutant controls
given the same volume of that vehicle. That is one dose a week against seven
daily doses in each on-week, in a different vehicle, so the whole-animal strength
gain in the more MVA-adjacent model came from four oral doses. Mean holding
impulse improved, Figure 5B, p<0.01, n=6, in a study reporting no randomization
and no blinding, with the endpoint scored from the single longest inverted-grid
hang multiplied by body weight; the aging study randomized, matched baseline body
weight and blinded data collection. The two endpoints depend on body weight in
opposite directions, impulse multiplying by it and grip dividing by it, so no
single body-weight artifact explains both positives.

Selectivity is where a compensation route meets an MVA-specific hazard. Fisetin
raised TUNEL-positive apoptosis in Zmpste24-deficient fibro-adipogenic
progenitors but not in wild-type ones, and 20 µM in transwell co-culture improved
wild-type progenitor proliferation and fusion, with wild-type progenitor and
wild-type FAP control arms; selective killing is demonstrated in vitro. In vivo
only marker counts on sections were measured, and under the first house rule
those show what is present, not what was cleared. Neither study tests an
aneuploid tissue, where the p16-positive compartment may be the aneuploid cells
this patient currently depends on for tissue mass. That specific question, not a
generic unknown, is what an MVA research test would have to measure before any
clearance agent is considered.

Dasatinib plus quercetin stays combination-only. Dasatinib 5 mg/kg with quercetin
50 mg/kg, gavaged three consecutive days every 15 days for seven months in female
[Sod1-knockout mice, PMID38233728](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38233728/),
improved isolated EDL specific force in N/cm², genotype by treatment p=0.0016,
without restoring body composition, muscle mass, motor-neuron count,
neuromuscular-junction innervation or protein-synthesis rates. The maximal-force
plot in mN carries an interaction p=0.0334 but not the within-knockout contrast,
so absolute-force rescue is not credited.

Taken together, the administered-drug compensation evidence in the ledger is
normalized output in aged or progeroid mice: fisetin grip, D+Q specific force and
the revised navitoclax result. Absolute force in a growing animal is the endpoint
apitegromab's rationale turns on, and no reserve supplies it. The human evidence
points the same way. The [ORS 2025 conference report, paper 429](https://www.ors.org/wp-content/uploads/AM25/429.pdf)
randomized 74 adults with knee osteoarthritis, 34 assigned fisetin and 40
placebo, and found no between-group advantage in walking, timed up-and-go or knee
strength, in a one-page report whose power calculation concerned cartilage rather
than strength. Its historical claim that fisetin was used in an earlier fibrosis
trial is not accepted: the cited reference is the dasatinib-plus-quercetin study.
The twelve-participant [D+Q IPF trial, PMID36857968](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36857968/)
was a three-week feasibility design whose functional confidence intervals include
zero. Both keep reserves as reserves.

## Reading and source-quality boundaries

The [fixed selection](outer-unfiltered-next512-selection.json) continues the same
SHA-256 order at indices 1217–1728, excluding the full 1,460-record gene-query
set and 1,649 previous outer readings. No drug name, outcome, publication date or
biological filter selected the batch. Each reader owned 256 disjoint records,
provenance fields were preserved, and raw abstracts remain outside Git.

`verify_next512.py` replays the fixed selection, exclusions and content hashes
against the original retrievals. It **PASSED with full source replay**,
`source_selection_replayed: true`, so each selected record's saved abstract hash
was reproduced from the original corpus rather than taken from the review file. It
does not prove biological efficacy, experimental reproducibility or independent
study counts.

Six source-quality items constrain what may be quoted from this tranche.
[MED39392349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39392349/) is a Significance section
only; a fresh PubMed XML response reproduces the same fragment, nothing was
inferred, and it is not counted as a recovered full abstract.
[MED42131347](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42131347/) is quarantined: its title
concerns cadmium gut–liver toxicity while the saved abstract describes morin and
cisplatin sensitization, so none of those drug effects is credited anywhere.
[MED37384650](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37384650/) is explicitly retracted
and retained as an unreliable catalog record, not as evidence. Six records,
[PPR915962](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR915962),
[PPR930682](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR930682),
[PPR497110](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR497110),
[PPR650161](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR650161),
[PPR164040](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR164040) and
[PPR870002](https://europepmc.org/article/PPR/PPR870002), are preprints; reading
an abstract does not establish peer review or final publication identity.
[PMC7715339](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7715339/) is an interim
pediatric panobinostat conference report, where H4 acetylation and remaining on
study are not tumor efficacy and myelosuppression is reported as dose-limiting.
[MED36900109](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36900109/) conflates HCC metastasis
with primary glioblastoma and repeats a dataset accession; its target claims need
source scrutiny before use.

Nothing in this tranche displaces the niacin/apitegromab comparison. No record
supplies an administered medicine that restored faithful division in a
BUBR1-limited cell, and none supplies an absolute force gain in a growing animal.
No record in these 512 tests apitegromab or any myostatin-pathway agent at all,
which is a coverage fact and not evidence for or against that route. What the
tranche adds is two conditional reserves on the compensation side, two mechanism
probes that preserve proteins or rebalance forces without a completed division,
and a sharper statement of what each route must measure. Unanswered scientific
and coverage questions remain, 3,598 saved abstracts among them. Completion of
this batch does not trigger publication or Track 2 submission.
