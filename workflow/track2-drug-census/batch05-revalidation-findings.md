## Evidence that growth, survival and fidelity diverge

Transient **Nutlin-3a** followed by withdrawal caused endoreduplication and stable tetraploid clones resistant to radiation/cisplatin apoptosis (MED18922897). **Enzastaurin** preferentially killed CIN colorectal cells while inducing metaphase delay, tetraploidy and micronuclei; MSI comparisons used isotoxic, not necessarily identical, doses (MED25486357). BEX4-mediated SIRT2 inhibition increased survival together with aneuploidy and tumor growth (MED27512957). Survivin K129E retained anti-apoptotic activity but impaired division (MED25788861). Nuf2 depletion caused premature polar-body extrusion with aneuploidy, while excess Nuf2 also disrupted meiosis (MED26054848).

Mps1 RNAi survivors were relatively less aneuploid by selected chromosome-copy measurements (MED21402910), but survivor selection is not demonstrated repair of segregation. Caspase-7 knockout increased luciferase expression 1.7-fold while lowering growth and viability (MED35016732), without proof of greater total culture output. Constitutive Gs signaling increased bone volume alongside partial cortical-structure loss (MED25704759), without a strength endpoint. Later NK proliferation after dendritic-cell depletion occurred despite declining responsiveness (MED27905484). The porcine ACL study found no early histological cartilage advantage of reconstruction/repair over untreated transection (MED28671352).
