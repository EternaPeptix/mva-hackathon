Actual functional positives remain visible. Resveratrol alleviated methylglyoxal-associated maturation, spindle and DNA-damage abnormalities in mouse oocytes (PMID 24194906). PD166285 rescued meiotic resumption and progression to MII in EML1-deficient mouse oocytes (PMID 34124073), without abstract-level proof of chromosome fidelity or offspring benefit. Unnamed CDC20/cathepsin B inhibitors restored aspects of CCDC41-deficient oocyte progression (PMID 41331237). A periodontal-cell secretome restored osteogenic potential in an inflammatory culture model (PMID 38539170). These are model-specific leads, not demonstrated BUB1B/MVA efficacy or consumer-product effects.

Anticancer benefits also remain distinct from normal-tissue restoration. The abstract reports no tumors after daily lapatinib plus naproxen; subsequent root primary review narrows this to no large palpable tumors at the study endpoint. The OH-BBN regimen and its prevention timing are separate from the established-tumor five-day RNA arms (PMID 38993610). BI-2536 monotherapy and prexasertib/cisplatin had different animal efficacy arms (PMID 35302600). The partner notes retain MPS1-IN-3/vincristine orthotopic survival, LBH589 and 6-OAP animal effects, and siHIF2 activity with one highlighted clinical partial response. Genetic mouse efficacy, drug-treated cellular arms and clinical biomarker associations are not interchanged.

Nulls, harms and pharmacological identity matter. MELK-null cells retained OTS167 sensitivity, undermining MELK mediation without erasing drug activity (PMID 28337968). BPA changed granulosa-cell expression only at the highest supraphysiological concentration; lower exposures were null (PMID 27979917, with an unread erratum link). Arsenic caused primary-lung-cell DNA damage but chromosome damage was absent in epithelial cells at both reported times (PMID 24291234). Methamphetamine disturbed astrocyte cycling (PMID 25290377). Diclofenac cytotoxicity required approximately 170–200 μM in HeLa assays (PMID 35624874). Nicotine is not niacin, assay labels are not nutritional treatments, and genetic checkpoint bypass is not necessarily accurate chromosome segregation.
