# Zoledronic acid, farnesol and geranylgeraniol: CENP-F source qualification

**The source demonstrates partial restoration of kinetochore CENP-F localization by trans,trans-farnesol in zoledronic-acid-treated cancer cells. It does not demonstrate BUBR1 restoration, correction of completed chromosome segregation, or treatment benefit in MVA.** Geranylgeraniol has a dose-qualified result: 50/100 µM comparisons were nonsignificant, while reversal at 150 µM was reported without shown data.

This qualification adds four source-specific assessments to the 207-object baseline: the two alcohol intermediates, zoledronic acid and the administered clodronate null control. These are four intervention assessments from one paper, not four studies or four beneficial candidates. No new outer-abstract reading credit is assigned. It addresses Batch 15 n122/original unfiltered 4883; the unavailable quantitative normal-cell/cytotoxicity comparisons keep the original composite question partly unresolved.

## Cancer and normal-cell outcomes

CENP-F was detected with antibody 610768 and DAPI microscopy. The measurement is the **percentage of scored mitotic cells with punctate localization**, not total CENP-F abundance, RNA expression, or percentage of individual kinetochores. Diffuse staining persists in treated cells. No direct farnesylation, binding, mutant-rescue or target-specific epistasis assay establishes CENP-F as the sole causal target; the paper itself discusses other prenylated proteins.

Fig. 5's unselected mitotic-phase fractions are not uniformly positive: significant early-phase increases are limited to MDA-MB-436 at 25 µM/48 h and MDA-MB-231/MCF-7 at 10 µM/24 h. Larger responses in cells lacking kinetochore CENP-F partly condition on the localization phenotype. These fixed-cell fractions do not measure individual-cell transit time. Short-pulse captions specify later incubation in medium up to 48 h, without an exact cancer-cell pulse-to-assay mapping; do not infer 48 h total or an immediate 4–8 h onset. Fig. 2 explicitly uses 24 h drug plus 24 h drug-free medium. Separately, the MCF-10A Results explicitly specify a total of 48 h.

In MDA-MB-436 after 48 h, misalignment with absent kinetochore CENP-F was 0.3% in control versus 4.7%/7.3% at 10/25 µM, reported P<.05/P<.001 (Fig. 7). These are chromosomes outside prometaphase/metaphase plates, not measured daughter-cell aneuploidy. The tripolar-morphology increase was **nonsignificant at every tested dose**; images use DAPI/CENP-F rather than tubulin staining. FOH/GGOH correction of these abnormalities was not tested.

Nonmalignant MCF-10A reportedly showed no localization/mitotic-progression effect under 25 µM pulse or continuous schedules, and no alignment/spindle effect in the repeated alignment experiment. **All these normal-cell results are data not shown**, without numerical groups or P values. MCF-10A is one cell line grown in different medium; these comparisons do not establish general normal-tissue safety or prove proliferation-rate-specific action.

Cancer cytotoxicity is also not demonstrated here: preliminary, unshown tests reportedly found no apoptosis effect in MDA-MB-436 at 25 µM/72 h or MCF-7 at 25 µM/48 h. There is no quantitative cancer-versus-normal viability panel, animal tumor endpoint or clonogenic rescue. The localization positive should therefore be retained without promoting the paper's proposed anticancer mechanism to measured tumor killing.

## Replication, relevance and disposition

Figure captions report three experiments, means±SEM. Methods specify 40 pro/pro/meta-phase cells for localization scoring and 100 mitotic cells per drug concentration for alignment; individual experiment values and exact allocation are unavailable. Counted cells are not independent biological experiments. Dose/time analyses used two-way ANOVA/Bonferroni; single-time analyses used one-way ANOVA/Dunnett. Randomization and blinded scoring are not reported, and statistics were not reproduced.

BubR1 appears in background and references about CENP-F silencing/SAC. **No BUB1B RNA, BUBR1 protein or drug response was measured.** Genetic results in cited papers were not reproduced by these interventions. The source also acknowledges sustained culture exposures above its cited plasma concentrations; this assessment makes no human exposure translation.

Retained distinct source-scoped assessments are zoledronic acid as a mechanistic perturbation, trans,trans-farnesol as a direct localization-rescue comparator, and source-labelled trans-geranylgeraniol with its mixed dose-specific evidence. Clodronate is retained as a separately identified administered null control, with no positive rescue credit. No joins are forced. Farnesol's positive is relevant to protein-localization restoration, but none of these experiments establishes useful normal-tissue function, BUBR1 rescue or MVA benefit. Provisional niacin and apitegromab likewise remain unproven MVA therapies; this source alone cannot rank clinical benefit or harm among them.
