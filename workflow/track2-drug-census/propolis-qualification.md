# Poplar propolis ethanol extract: BUB1B-down cytotoxic-cell observation

**28 September 2026.** This is a source-specific appraisal of one Chinese poplar-propolis ethanol extract (EEP), found through NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database group **1666**, a generic **Propolis** ingredient name. Database membership establishes neither product prevalence nor equivalence to every retail propolis preparation. This is not an efficacy result for all propolis products, CAPE, or another bee product.

## What the primary study tested

The [open primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9857396/) treated the DLBCL SU-DHL-2 cell line with EEP made from poplar propolis harvested in Qinglong, Hebei, China. The authors identified 51 extract constituents. At **5.729 μg/mL for 24 hours**—the reported CCK-8 IC50 at a starting density of 5 × 10⁵ cells/mL—the extract inhibited this lymphoma line relative to its DMSO solvent control. The study did not include a nonmalignant B-cell or normal-cell comparator.

Label-free proteomics reported **115 differential proteins** (61 up, 54 down), using p<0.05 and fold-change cutoffs above 2 or below 0.5. A cell-cycle pathway enrichment (p=0.0201) listed BUB1B, PLK and cyclin B2 among the associated proteins. The article does not provide an isolated BUB1B protein effect estimate or a checkpoint-function test. RT-PCR at the same extract IC50 reports **BUB1B mRNA down** relative to solvent control; the text supplies no BUB1B-specific numerical effect or p-value. The extract also increased ROS and was interpreted through ferroptosis/cell-death pathways. Thus the transcript result occurred alongside cytotoxicity and broad cell-cycle effects, not as a selective BUB1B intervention.

## Relevance to BUB1B and Track 2

This is an adverse-direction cell-context observation: it does not support restoring BUB1B, BUBR1 abundance, spindle-checkpoint function, chromosome fidelity or muscle output. Lower BUB1B in a lymphoma line at its extract IC50 could be downstream of cytotoxicity or cell-cycle arrest; it does not show that BUB1B was the causal target. No MVA/BUB1B-deficient cells, patient variants, normal muscle, faithful-division assay, animal efficacy or human outcome was tested.

Accordingly, retain the extract as an **adverse-direction cytotoxic-cell comparator**, not as a treatment candidate or a challenger to niacin/apitegromab. The result must not be assigned to generic retail propolis or any one of the mixture's constituents.

## Identity and source limits

The NIH DSLD inventory's generic Propolis name has a string-only cross-reference to ChEMBL `CHEMBL4298075`, preferred name **PROPOLIS WAX**. That is not chemical or formulation equivalence to this regional ethanol extract. The source's 51-component profile and extraction method define the intervention; CAPE, caffeic acid, propolis wax and other propolis extracts remain separate.

The appraisal used the available primary article text for extraction, IC50, proteomics criteria, pathway results and the direction of BUB1B RT-PCR. Figure 4 was not digitized, supplemental tables were not independently analyzed, and no raw proteomics or qPCR data were reprocessed. The exact BUB1B magnitude remains unquantified here.
