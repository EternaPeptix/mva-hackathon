# Common-compound priority matrix

**27 September 2026 UTC · Current priority synthesis.** Seventeen decision-relevant candidates, not a ranking of all reviewed compounds. Two independently qualified trial protein preparations now join the functional comparisons. Source-specific positives and nulls remain paired; no nomination change or original-queue reading credit follows. Two gut-source preparations add within-arm functional observations with unresolved randomized contrasts.

**Several challengers have better-controlled evidence for specific human functions than niacin; none has demonstrated a larger clinical benefit or superiority in MVA.** NR, creatine, urolithin A, resveratrol, the tested Lovaza formulation, astaxanthin study preparations, FSHD antioxidant combination and the COCOA-PAD beverage are substantive comparisons. The tested BODYBALANCE collagen and MPC80 drink add secondary strength or mobility observations with distinct controls and limits. L-carnitine and calcium-HMB add narrower controlled signals. NMN and vitamin K2 have more direct compound-associated BUBR1 observations. These are separate reasons to prioritize experiments, not a single efficacy order.

Niacin’s human muscle-NAD engagement remains useful, but five myopathy patients and ten **treated** healthy controls provide no placebo-controlled functional contrast; follow-up durations also differ. Pediatric progression despite NAD correction shows why engagement alone is insufficient. Its BUBR1 bridge relies on **NMN and genetic SIRT2**, not niacin rescue. [Existing evidence table](../../reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md).

Apitegromab retains the strongest documented pediatric **primary motor-function** comparison in this selected set: pooled SAPPHIRE HFMSE difference 1.8 points (95% CI 0.30–3.32). It was measured in SMA on SMN therapy. Higher-dose standalone efficacy was nonsignificant; fractures were 5/53 versus 1/50 at the labeled-dose comparison, and EMBRAZE supplied no notable functional gain despite lean-mass retention. The inherited SAPPHIRE review used the primary abstract and full official label, **not the full trial main/SAP**; incomplete source access therefore cannot uniquely disqualify challengers. [Existing appraisal](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-review.md).

**Three high-value unresolved questions:** resolve the three existing creatine DMD full papers and their direct contrasts; compare NMN and niacin fairly in an exposure-qualified model; establish the exact vitamin K2 preparation and whether its tissue signal reflects functional BUBR1 per cell. These remain important decision gaps during broader discovery; unresolved source access does not make new qualified evidence ineligible. **The NMN comparison uses the same evidence threshold as the nominees.** The earlier reopening trigger demanded an advantage over niacin without a reciprocal hurdle. The [current rule](track2-route-decisions.md#elected-hypotheses-and-within-pathway-references) separates approved-drug submission eligibility from scientific priority:

> Demonstrate actual-allele functional rescue at justified exposure using the same function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the nominees. Compare NMN with niacin without assuming incumbent superiority; assess the nominated product’s approved-drug submission eligibility separately. A useful mechanistic comparator need not become a submitted medication.
