# Creatine monohydrate plus calcium-HMB: a narrow rowing-power comparison

The tested **Creapure creatine monohydrate plus FullGas calcium-HMB preparation** has a reported controlled benefit for power at one blood-lactate threshold in a small elite-rower trial. The other power thresholds and all body-composition interactions were nonsignificant. The paper's large descriptive “synergy” percentages do not establish a formal ingredient interaction. This admits **one preparation-specific appraisal**, bringing the ledger to **306**, with **zero new eligibility records, chemical entities or verified independent trials**. Eligibility coverage remains **1,960 screened / 54,257 unscreened**. No BUB1B/MVA efficacy or nominee change follows.

The source is Fernández-Landa et al., *Nutrients* 2020, 12, 193: [PMID 31936727](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31936727/), [DOI 10.3390/nu12010193](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12010193). ## What the trial compared

The reported randomized, double-blind parallel trial assigned **28 elite male traditional rowers from one Spanish club, seven per arm**, for **10 weeks**. An independent statistician generated stratified blocks; exact strata, concealment and successful-blinding checks were unspecified. A baseline exam intended to verify absence of disease/previous injury is described, and no diseases were reported; this is not a verified injury-count or participant-flow account. [Original Methods; audit C01–C07 and independent F05.]

| Arm | Named exposure | Shared background |
|---|---|---|
| Placebo | No added test substance | Chocolate recovery shake and team training |
| Creatine | Creapure monohydrate, **0.04 g/kg/day** | Same background |
| HMB | FullGas **HMB-Ca**, nominal **3 g/day HMB** | Same background |
| Combination | Both assigned doses | Same background |

The shake supplied **1 g/kg carbohydrate and 0.3 g/kg protein**, within half an hour after exercise on six weekly training days. Off-day supplement timing was 30 minutes before bed; the exact off-day placebo procedure was unspecified. All arms shared training and individualized diets. The comparison does not test exercise synergy or supplementation without training. Nominal HMB mass does not resolve salt-versus-moiety dose, batch purity or calcium matching. Component names receive no automatic preparation aliases. Daily compliance checks were asserted without a numerical rate. Figure n=7 per arm does not establish complete endpoint accounting. [Original Methods; C02, C05–C07.]

## Functional finding and counterevidence

WAT is power at the D-max anaerobic threshold. **W4 and W8 are power at 4 and 8 mmol/L blood lactate, not weeks four and eight.** They were derived from the lactate curve during an incremental rowing-ergometer test at baseline and after ten weeks. This is narrower than directly measured maximal oxygen uptake, race endurance, voluntary muscle strength or patient mobility. [Methods; C08–C11.]

| Power endpoint | Reported four-group × time test | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| WAT | **P=.228; η²p=.168** | Nonsignificant |
| W4 | **P=.196; η²p=.181** | Nonsignificant |
| W8 | **P<.001; η²p=.766** | Reported comparative change signal |

For W8, reported mean individual changes were **placebo −1%, creatine +5%, HMB +6% and combination +15%**. Active changes reportedly exceeded placebo; the combination exceeded each component (**P<.01**). Figure 1 annotates those W8 comparisons only. Its error bars are labelled standard error, whereas general Methods describes SD; the printed prose uncertainty is not silently relabelled. Absolute group-mean comparisons at T1/T2 were described as nonsignificant. Separate within-arm improvements do not establish added benefit at WAT/W4. [Results, Table 3, Figure 1; C22–C25.]

All body-composition interactions were nonsignificant: **body mass P=.883, skinfolds P=.790, fat mass P=.207 and predicted muscle mass P=.442**. Placebo's within-arm muscle decline does not establish comparative preservation by active supplementation. Muscle and fat used anthropometric prediction equations rather than direct tissue imaging. Diet was assessed by FFQ and seven-day recalls; it was not controlled feeding. The independent audit adds the exact second-method paragraph missing from C13's original anchor. [Table 2; C12–C13, C20–C21, independent F04.]

“Nonsignificant” means no statistically significant comparison was reported. It does not establish a zero, negative or equivalent effect. Some historical source-routing objects retain “null/nonpositive” shorthand with this limited meaning. No endpoint hierarchy, global multiplicity strategy, power calculation, protocol/SAP or missingness sensitivity was recovered. Bonferroni pairwise testing alone does not resolve all those questions. [Methods; C15–C19, independent F02.]

The more precisely printed abstract decimals give **1293.4−877.56=415.84 percentage points**. They are not verified unrounded participant data. Smaller Table 4 discrepancies—**134+203=337** versus printed **338**, **403−338=65** versus **66**, and **397+1309=1706** versus **1705**—may reflect unavailable precision. The abstract WAT decimals give **65.67 percentage points**, consistent with rounding to 66; the much larger W8 conflict remains unresolved. [Native Tables 3–4, abstract; C23, C26, C31; D01–D02; independent F01/F03.]

The stated normalization is `(Treatment/Control−1)×100`. If the inputs are signed percentage changes, positive active W4/W8 changes divided by negative placebo changes would yield negative normalized values, unlike Table 4. Exact inputs/sign handling are unknown. No absolute denominator or undocumented formula is asserted as the correction. Near-zero placebo change also makes ratios unstable. The percentage change of group means need not equal the mean of individual changes. [Methods/Table 4; D06 and independent arithmetic audit.]

The four-group-by-time test and superiority over either single component do not establish a **creatine × HMB × time** interaction. No coefficient, confidence interval or hypothesis test for that contrast was reported. Conditional contrasts of printed rounded mean changes are documented in JSON, with separate scenarios for contradictory cells; they have no inferential uncertainty and are not presented as efficacy estimates. Table 4/prose cites reference 51 for the model, but the bibliography identifies food-composition tables; Methods uses reference 53, a cooling/hypoxia paper. Those cited papers were not independently read here. [D03–D06; independent F06.]

## Competitiveness relative to current leaders

This randomized task-related comparison has a stronger causal design than niacin's open-label human functional context, but its derived endpoint and healthy elite-athlete population have less clinical relevance than the separately qualified dystrophinopathy creatine and SMA apitegromab comparisons. Their different populations and endpoints do not supply a numerical superiority ranking. Existing component positives and counterevidence remain separate.

The trial measured no BUB1B/BUBR1, faithful chromosome segregation, progenitor renewal or actual-allele rescue. Retain the tested combination as a bounded performance comparator under the same justified-exposure, useful-function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the nominees. It does not establish clinical muscle benefit, restoration, formal synergy or a reason to replace the provisional niacin/NMN/apitegromab comparison. These judgments concern this tested preparation; other creatine/HMB products inherit no result.
