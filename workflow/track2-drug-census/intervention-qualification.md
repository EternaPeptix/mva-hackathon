# Which intervention produced the rescue?

**Research decision, 21 September 2026:** retain niacin for restoration and
apitegromab for muscle compensation as the nominated research comparison.
Additional experiments below identify alternatives and counterevidence. The
nominees share the absence of demonstrated actual-allele muscle benefit; that
gap alone is not grounds to reject a challenger. Advancement instead depends on
the relevant defect, actual intervention, useful function and effects on normal
controls.

This resolves the intervention/endpoint questions flagged in the
[remaining 253-abstract review](outer-ranked-completion-review.json): 14 flagged
records received targeted primary-source qualification. It does not close all
biological uncertainties or extend reading to the 5,469 unselected outer records.
Original abstract assessments remain unchanged; this document supplies the
deeper interpretation, including journal-version and review-to-primary links.

## Kinase balancing: include what happens to the controls

| Primary study | Actual drug and measured benefit or harm | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [MPS1 after B56 depletion, PMID 28441529](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5477644/) | **Reversine, 200 nM for 50 minutes**, improved cold-stable kinetochore fibers after B56 depletion in RPE cells (three experiments, at least 40 cells per condition per experiment). MG132 blocked exit throughout the assay. | Retain an attachment-balancing experiment. The result did not fully normalize the plotted defect or demonstrate completed faithful divisions. Rapamycin elsewhere in the paper controlled an engineered recruitment system; it did not supply a therapeutic mTOR result. |
| [Eg5 after HMMR depletion, PMID 29386294](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5905292/) | **STLC and monastrol** shortened delayed mitosis and improved selected spindle-fiber defects in HeLa cells. STLC's main timing experiment used 10 µM, three experiments and more than 40 cells per bar. | Retain conditional motor-force balancing. Timing was measured only in cells already displaying aligned chromosomes and normal bipolar spindles. Spindle mispositioning persisted; drug-treated chromosome counts and whole-population output were not established. Supplemental experiments used different concentrations, including an unresolved 10/50 µM discrepancy. |
| [PLK1 after UBAP2L depletion, PMID 37039032](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10240199/) | **BI2536, 10 nM for 45 minutes**, reduced misalignment, bridges and micronuclei after cytokinesis (three replicates; at least 100 cells per stage/condition). | **Strengthen the conditional PLK1 route:** this is actual post-division defect reduction. The same regimen blocked control-HeLa mitotic progression; the public author response additionally reports death after prolonged arrest, without a quantified death denominator. Treatment order/continuation around monastrol release is unclear. This is not a demonstrated therapeutic window in healthy tissue. |
| [PLK1 with APC-gene truncation, PMID 29549256](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5856809/) | **Volasertib/BI6727**, a different compound, allowed abnormal surviving cells and increased small-intestinal adenomas in ApcMin mice. APC here means the adenomatous polyposis coli gene, not APC/C. | Actual genotype-specific counterevidence, not proof that every PLK1 regimen is harmful. The animal schedule omits dosing frequency; stock/volume arithmetic conflicts with the stated dose, and Methods group size ten conflicts with the figure's analyzed n=3. Keep the direction of the reported result without overstating its precision. |

## Protein removal is not necessarily drug inhibition

| Primary study | What was actually done and observed | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Kv10.1, PMID 32854244](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7564071/) | KCNH1 siRNA reduced lagging-chromosome-positive HeLa anaphases from 35.78% to 29.32% (400 anaphases per experiment, four independent synchronizations). The actual blockers **mAb56** (Kv10.1) and **AnCoA4** (ORAI1) reduced microtubule dynamicity, opposite to knockdown. Their dynamics assay used an Eg5 inhibitor to impose monopolar spindles. | Retain mechanism tools; the blockers did not demonstrate the genetic chromosome benefit. Their different targets are not interchangeable. The mAb56 caption has an unusually high concentration unit that remains unresolved; no clinical exposure inference. |
| [Myeloma checkpoint restoration, PMID 22132115](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3223182/) | **Retroviral MAD2 delivery** modestly improved normal anaphases in RPMI-8226 cells; the figure counted at least 50 anaphases, without a stated independent biological n. Unchanged growth was reported with data not shown. | Correct the abstract-level MAD2/CDC20 wording: **MAD2 restoration was tested; CDC20 restoration was not demonstrated**. This is a genetic control precedent, not a restoration medicine or rescued-cell karyotype result. |
| [CDC20, PMID 39838194](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11850905/) | Genetic CDC20 reduction lengthened metaphase and reduced some errors under **reversine-induced** MPS1 inhibition; it also protected cancer cells from the challenge. Separately, **MG132, 10 µM for 90 minutes**, improved alignment while blocking mitotic exit. | Do not assign the genetic result to a CDC20 inhibitor or the MG132 result to bortezomib. Alignment in arrested cells is not faithful descendant output. The study does not establish healthy-muscle benefit, and its RO3306 caption/Methods units conflict. |
| [HDAC6/SAE2, PMID 40836035](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12489036/) | **ACY-738, 2.5 µM**, increased multipolar spindles after synchronized release, including in untransfected U2OS cells. Specific SUMO1 genetic interventions rescued spindle phenotypes. Micronucleus results in SAE2-mutant arms are separate from the drug spindle experiment. | This qualifies the [tubacin/tubastatin oocyte result](translation-qualification.md): benefit in one lesion is not class-wide protection. Preserve ACY-738's adverse outcome without treating it as demonstrated muscle harm or a universal HDAC6 contraindication. |

## Credit functional benefits to the intervention that produced them

| Primary study | Positive result and implementation | Competitive position |
|---|---|---|
| [BMP4, PMID 27558554](https://www.embopress.org/doi/full/10.15252/embj.201593103) | Research **BMP4, 100 ng/mL**, partly restored cavitation and size of trisomic mouse embryoid bodies, with timing-dependent effects. Diameter plots included more than ten bodies per condition; independent culture-batch replication was unspecified. | Retain a differentiation comparator. Improved cardiac beating was not shown in the BMP4 arm. Chromosome normalization and smaller teratomas came from separate selection of engineered cells that lost an extra chromosome. Those outcomes cannot be assigned to BMP4. |
| [STEMIN/YAP5SA primary, PMID 35891703](https://www.oaepublish.com/articles/jca.2022.20), identified through review PMID 39544432 | **Two modified RNAs**, 50 µg each, delivered locally immediately after mouse myocardial infarction improved ejection fraction and reduced scar at four weeks (four combination-treated mice, six controls). Neither RNA alone significantly improved ejection fraction. | A serious organ-function precedent, beyond markers or cell counts. It is a bespoke combination RNA therapy, not an existing medicine. Accurate completed cardiomyocyte divisions, long-term tumor safety and skeletal-muscle benefit remain unestablished. Injection timing and formulation volume conflict between source sections; baseline ejection fractions also differed. |
| [SHP2/MEK and receptor trafficking, PMID 30931927](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6443781/) | **SHP099**, 60 mg/kg/day orally for six days, improved glucose tolerance (12 vehicle/10 treated mice) and insulin tolerance (11/10) after high-fat feeding. Both arms switched to normal chow before dosing. **PD0325901**, 10 µM for two hours, separately delayed receptor endocytosis in cells. | Retain distinct metabolic and receptor-feedback experiments. SHP099's signaling effect was predominantly hepatic; muscle AKT was unchanged and muscle function untested. PD0325901 is **mirdametinib**, an approved active ingredient, but it does not inherit SHP099's mouse benefit. BUBR1 deficiency already delayed receptor uptake in prior work; this study does not establish a muscle defect corrected by further inhibition. |
| [JNK, PMID 30282810](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201804205) | Genetic dominant-negative JNK/Basket with **mad2 RNAi** normalized excess intestinal progenitor frequency by day 20 in flies. Excess enteroendocrine cells persisted. | Retain a tissue-composition target. The paper separately documents dysplasia after BubR1 RNAi, but did not test the JNK rescue in that genotype. No JNK drug, restored chromosome number or functional gut rescue was demonstrated. |
| [MAD2/BIM/BCL2 journal study, PMID 38806674](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-024-00160-3), corresponding to PPR721586 | Under induced MAD2 excess, **Bim deletion** protected the gut: all seven Bim-null mice survived the plotted 17 days, versus a median eight-day survival in the 11-mouse MAD2 cohort. Separately, a **BCL2 transgene** partly protected hematopoiesis. Cultured progenitors showed less mitotic death and no detected chromosome abnormalities in limited single-cell sequencing through day seven. | Genuine genetic tissue/survival compensation. No BIM-inhibiting or BCL2-raising medicine was tested. Chronic MAD2 excess differs from BUB1B insufficiency; BCL2 gain is also opposite to BCL2-family drug antagonism. The BCL2 model's autoimmune/lymphoma liability prevented long-term rescue testing. The tumor-free MAD2-only cohort cannot establish rescue safety. Preprint and journal paper are one study, not replication. |
| [Anti-PD-1, PMID 39786467](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-24-2589) | Preventive **anti-mouse PD-1 clone RMP1-14** increased Pold1-mutant median survival from 133 to 342 days (19 control/14 treated mice). Another clone, 29F.1A12, did not significantly benefit that model; results also differed in Pole mutants. | Retain a distinct preclinical cancer-prevention route. These are research antibodies, not tested human PD-1 medicines. Among sequenced tumors, 0/6 treated versus 2/6 controls were aneuploid, but the total-CNV comparison was nonsignificant (P=.3357). Tumor selection is not chromosome correction; mutation-driven immunogenicity and normal-tissue safety cannot be assumed in MVA. No muscle outcome was tested. |

Mirdametinib's identity and February 2025 NF1 approval are supported by the
[FDA substance record](https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/86k0j5ak6m)
and [approval notice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-mirdametinib-adult-and-pediatric-patients-neurofibromatosis-type-1-who-have-symptomatic).
That notice also identifies cardiac and ocular risks; approval does not establish
the therapeutic relevance of a 10 µM receptor-trafficking assay. The anti-PD-1
paper calls BE0146 “RMP-14”; the
[manufacturer](https://bioxcell.com/invivomab-anti-mouse-pd-1-cd279-be0146)
identifies it as RMP1-14. Neither clone's result transfers to pembrolizumab or
nivolumab.

## Implication for the proposed assay

Record completed divisions, new chromosome errors, renewal and absolute useful
output from the starting population. More stable attachments during arrest,
better-looking survivors after selection and survival of drug-challenged cancer
cells answer different questions. These are measurable distinctions, not reasons
to discard positive findings from other models.

## Reading limits

All findings use primary Results, Methods and figure captions. For the Kv10.1,
myeloma, CDC20 and HDAC6 studies, original figure images and complete supplements
were not independently inspected. The four kinase studies additionally used
selected original figures; HMMR supplements and UBAP2L's public author-response
control figure were checked. BMP4's selected supplementary figure, the heart-RNA
study's main function figure and selected supplement, and the JNK, MAD2 and
anti-PD-1 studies' relevant original figures were additionally inspected. SHP2
source-data values were not independently reconstructed. Supplemental inspection
was targeted, not exhaustive; no raw-data or experimental reproduction is
claimed. Source concentration, timing and replication ambiguities remain
unresolved. Raw source bodies and figures remain outside the repository.
