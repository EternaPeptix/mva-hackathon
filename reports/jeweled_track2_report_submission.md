# jeweled — Track 2: testing two ways to preserve muscle

**Research proposal, revised 23 September 2026.**

We provisionally nominate **niacin (nicotinic acid) for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. The aim is useful muscle function with preserved renewal in a child with recorded muscle atrophy and failure to thrive. Everolimus remains a conditional signaling probe. Neither primary candidate has demonstrated MVA rescue; this is a research comparison, not a treatment recommendation.

**Media updated:** the report, slides, narration and captions now use this pair. Vimeo V3 replaced the old recording at the same URL, with signed-out visual playback and matching captions verified on 21 September 2026. Track 2 remains unsubmitted.

## The genetic condition on this proposal

**BUB1B encodes BUBR1, which helps cells distribute chromosomes correctly.** Our [Track 1 hypothesis](jeweled_track1_report_submission.md) combines Leu737Ter, predicting a 736-amino-acid protein, and Asn1002Lys, still a variant of uncertain significance with unmeasured function. [Patient-cell experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/) support low BUBR1 from reduced transcript supply and unstable missense proteins in selected MVA1 cases, not drug responsiveness here.

**Confirmed cis would give `[L737Ter;N1002K]/WT` and retire this pair as the compound-heterozygous explanation.** Translation stops before Asn1002Lys. Continuing a BUB1B explanation would require an independently demonstrated additional pathogenic allele in trans or dominant mechanism. Uncertain dosage cannot preserve the discarded biallelic premise. Phase, endogenous RNA/protein and allele function require measurement.

## Niacin: test restoration of working BUBR1

The 2010 HeLa titration found missegregation in most cells near 6% BUBR1 but little effect near 13%; these assay-specific levels are not treatment thresholds. [North's experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194088/) linked NAD-dependent SIRT2 to BUBR1 stabilization. NMN increased BUBR1; genetic SIRT2 elevation improved selected cardiac outcomes and male survival in hypomorphic mice. Neither was niacin treatment or demonstrated skeletal-muscle rescue.

[Pirinen's small open-label mitochondrial-myopathy study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32386566/) showed niacin-mediated muscle NAD correction. Patient knee-extension strength rose about **1.1-fold after ten months**, with larger changes elsewhere, but niacin-treated healthy controls also gained strength. Without placebo, this is engagement evidence, not disease-specific functional rescue. A [pediatric case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39173541/) progressed despite biochemical correction. Niacin must therefore improve useful muscle, with BUBR1-dependent evidence to claim restoration. Other NAD precursors cannot supply its missing evidence; prescription-niacin pediatric safety and effectiveness are unestablished.

A [2025 nicotinic-acid experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40062928/) adds a different human-cell precedent: toxin-exposed oocyte maturation averaged 56.7% with co-treatment versus 31.7% with toxin alone and 73.5% in controls. The figure does not label the direct treatment-versus-toxin significance test. This is partial culture protection, not clinical fertility benefit; the mouse mechanism work concerns SIRT1, without SIRT2 or drug-induced BUBR1 restoration. The [primary appraisal](../workflow/track2-drug-census/unfiltered-findings.md) keeps this positive result separate from the missing MVA muscle evidence.

## Apitegromab: test compensation in surviving muscle

Apitegromab blocks activation of promyostatin/latent myostatin. [SAPPHIRE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40818473/) provides stronger actual-drug functional evidence: in nonambulatory SMA on nusinersen/risdiplam, pooled doses in ages 2–12 improved the 12-month HFMSE motor score versus placebo by **1.8 points (95% CI 0.30–3.32; p=0.019)**. The 20 mg/kg comparison alone was not significant; the lower-dose result was nominally significant, and older participants were exploratory. This does not establish transfer to MVA.

The [US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b1cd8ab8-8a10-48ed-9806-75a44f22b963) covers SMA from age two on SMN2-targeted treatment. Fractures occurred in **5/53 at 10 mg/kg versus 1/50 on placebo**. Regular intravenous access and one-to-two-hour infusions every four weeks add burden. This describes labeled use, not an MVA regimen. Bone, developmental and tumor risks remain material.

A [related antibody in SMN-restored SMA mice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6423420/) increased absolute torque without improving torque per muscle mass; a more affected model responded despite lower latent myostatin. Thus **excess myostatin is not required** for a compensation hypothesis. The assay must nevertheless contain the precursor/activation system and demonstrate actual-drug engagement. [EMBRAZE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04440-4) retained lean mass without notable functional differences: muscle size alone cannot pass. No myostatin-blockade rescue in MVA was identified. The [appraisal](../workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-review.md) retains these intervention and disease boundaries.

## Everolimus: a conditional mechanism probe

[Sieben Figure 5C](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863/figure/5) shows stronger phosphoprotein bands in monoallelic carrier muscle, **two mice per genotype**, with Ponceau loading control but no total-substrate denominators, quantification or inferential statistics. This does not establish harmful mTORC1 excess or rescue. Partial genetic normalization must qualify that premise before drug attribution. The [rapamycin observation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3785294/) was parenthetical and unpublished; [everolimus-associated BUBR1 loss](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10149375/) occurred in late-passage nonmutant cells, without the early-passage decrease. Neither establishes MVA benefit or harm. [Reserve-route decisions](appendices/track2-route-decisions.md) retain arimoclomol and conditional senolysis.

## What the completed work established

**[Vitamin K2 merits priority follow-up for its direct molecular evidence](../workflow/track2-drug-census/vitamin-k2-neonatal-qualification.md).** In a neonatal surgery/anesthesia/analgesia model, pretreatment accompanies improved behavior, higher Bub1b RNA and recovery of BUB1B-associated brain-tissue fluorescence (three mice per group for the fluorescence assay). This strengthens the earlier hub-gene-only description. Tissue composition, antibody specificity, per-cell function and causal dependence remain unresolved, as do the exact menaquinone form, dose and route. This is more direct drug-associated BUB1B evidence than niacin’s bridge through NMN and genetic SIRT2; niacin separately retains human muscle NAD engagement. Unresolved K2 exposure and function prevent a justified lead replacement.

**[Icariin merits priority assay clarification](../workflow/track2-drug-census/icariin-aging-qualification.md).** The exact abstract reports better rotarod performance and increased BUB1b among several markers after direct dosing in aged mice. This connects an actual compound with function and a named molecular signal. Its BUB1b assay, tissue, effect size and protein relevance remain unresolved because the main text and figures were inaccessible; fecal-transfer results are a separate intervention. These gaps limit nomination, but do not justify dismissing it for lacking MVA evidence that the leaders also lack.

**[Ursolic acid](../workflow/track2-drug-census/next576-primary-qualification.md) earns serious consideration on muscle function:** mouse grip and absolute-force preservation, plus a small randomized human secondary handgrip benefit, alongside a separate controlled null. It challenges niacin on demonstrated muscle benefit; niacin retains a distinct restoration hypothesis. Apitegromab’s stronger pediatric functional evidence comes from SMA. Product/exposure eligibility and transfer to MVA remain unresolved.

**[Urolithin A](../workflow/track2-drug-census/urolithin-human-qualification.md) is another substantive muscle-function competitor:** two randomized adult trials report exploratory hand-fatigue-resistance or hamstring-torque benefits. Neither trial met its primary endpoint, and the older cohort’s endurance advantage was nonsignificant at four months. These actual-function signals strengthen the comparator set, but do not establish BUBR1 restoration, pediatric applicability or superiority to apitegromab’s disease-specific motor evidence. The [numerical summary](../workflow/track2-drug-census/urolithin-human-outcomes.json) preserves the positives, nulls and unavailable adjusted confidence intervals.

**[BMS-309403](../workflow/track2-drug-census/lipid-rescue-qualification.md) has an actual-compound BUB1B RNA signal:** mean expression was 2.290 times palmitate alone, near control, alongside membrane/lipid improvements. Schedule differences, gene-specific significance, target attribution, BUBR1 protein and faithful division remain unresolved. Niacin instead has separate human muscle NAD engagement; its proposed BUBR1 bridge crosses interventions. Neither demonstrates genotype-specific rescue or superiority.

**[Creatine monohydrate merits priority muscle-compensation comparison](../workflow/track2-drug-census/creatine-muscle-qualification.md).** Three original pediatric DMD abstracts provide selected grip/manual-strength and energetic findings, alongside the six-month CINRG strength null and task/scale nulls in other trials. The 2010 study explicitly reports a between-group manual-strength change; the 2004 crossover abstract’s exact treatment contrast remains unresolved. This challenges niacin on direct controlled pediatric strength evidence, while niacin’s restoration hypothesis and apitegromab’s stronger documented SMA motor comparison address different dimensions. Unavailable scientific mains limit precision, not the existence of the reported positives. Comparative MVA benefit remains unknown.

**[L-citrulline plus metformin](../workflow/track2-drug-census/citrulline-metformin-qualification.md) supplies a separate combination-specific reserve.** The full trial and supplement show nominal 13-week strength (+18.3 N, 95% CI 3.0–33.5) and distal-function positives, but a missed 26-week primary (P=.09), final force/walking nulls and a nonsignificant subgroup interaction (P=.67). MRI shows slower worsening, not restored force. Forty-three children had complete primary data despite 45 study completions. The [numeric comparison](../workflow/track2-drug-census/citrulline-metformin-outcomes.json) preserves those distinctions. Neither isolated component inherits the results. The separately treated glutamine arm in CINRG also has a bounded strength-null appraisal, without a global inefficacy claim.

**The expanded human-function comparison changes NR’s assessment.** [NICE](../workflow/track2-drug-census/nr-pad-qualification.md) supplies an assigned-group walking contrast of +17.6 m after six months, meeting the trial’s one-sided P<.10 pilot rule (P=.08), with two primary comparisons and no multiplicity correction. This warrants a substantive reserve, while muscle NAD/BUBR1 benefit was not demonstrated. No added six-month benefit from trans-resveratrol was demonstrated. [Quercetin glycosides](../workflow/track2-drug-census/quercetin-glycosides-qualification.md) have a small controlled strength positive alongside matched training, with formulation, completer-analysis and longer-trial null limits. [L-carnosine](../workflow/track2-drug-census/carnosine-muscle-qualification.md) has overall functional nulls and two nominal subgroup signals after asymmetric post-randomization exclusions. These distinctions matter more than a generic supplement label; none establishes comparative MVA benefit.

**[GlyNAC](../workflow/track2-drug-census/glynac-muscle-qualification.md) adds measured muscle-metabolite and functional trajectories.** The older-adult randomized trial reports active-arm gait, grip and chair-rise improvements, but omits its planned final treatment-versus-placebo functional estimate and uncertainty. A separate 14-day trial has a primary blood-glutathione null; its favorable abstract subgroup P=.016 actually describes a within-active change. These findings warrant comparison with niacin’s open-label functional evidence without confirming comparative strength benefit or transferring combination results to NAC alone.

**[Caffeine culture](../workflow/track2-drug-census/caffeine-fidelity-qualification.md) supplies a different human chromosome-status observation.** In a selected 97-patient PGT-A subset, the reported euploid rates were 22.9% versus 10.9% (P=.002), while mosaicism did not significantly differ. All treated cycles followed earlier controls, biopsy was selective, and clinical follow-up was caffeine-only. The conference and full-paper cohorts cannot be treated as independent replication. The result supports studying chromosome status and illustrates why our assay counts all starting units and absolute usable output; it does not establish causal error reduction, birth benefit or an oral/systemic MVA treatment.

**[L-carnitine/levocarnitine](../workflow/track2-drug-census/carnitine-muscle-qualification.md) adds a limited endurance comparison.** Twelve mitochondrial-myopathy patients lasted 14 versus 11 minutes in a constant-work cycling test after active versus placebo phases, with no strength or peak-exercise benefit. The reported crossover model conflicts with unpaired-test table footnotes, and carnitine deficiency was not measured. A separate 20-patient DMD abstract reports no year-long functional benefit; its full paper and randomization remain unverified. This supports an endurance reserve, without demonstrating BUBR1 restoration.

**[Leucine](../workflow/track2-drug-census/leucine-muscle-qualification.md) and [calcium-HMB](../workflow/track2-drug-census/hmb-muscle-qualification.md) broaden the common-compound functional comparison.** The smaller leucine trial reports relative preservation of walking time and expiratory pressure, with only 42/50 analyzed; LACE found no overall performance benefit (SPPB difference 0.1 point, 95% CI −1.0 to 1.1), without establishing equivalence. Positive subgroup estimates do not establish responder selection. Calcium-HMB’s adult trial reports a secondary gait-speed benefit of 0.06 m/s (0.01–0.11); the protocol placebo omitted both HMB and calcium, and its companion paper is the same cohort. These are actual functional signals, but neither source set demonstrates BUBR1 restoration, and detailed HMB results remain inaccessible. Neither currently displaces the pediatric compensation comparators or distinct restoration hypothesis.

**[Resveratrol](../workflow/track2-drug-census/resveratrol-qualification.md) remains a substantive functional challenger:** one small randomized exercise study reports additional muscle-power and torque improvement; a separate pilot has an inconclusive walking-speed primary outcome. The [Copenhagen follow-up](../workflow/track2-drug-census/resveratrol-exercise-counterevidence-qualification.md) finds a smaller aerobic-fitness gain with resveratrol (about 308 versus 444 mL/min; P=.03), while both arms improve and knee-extensor endurance shows no significant drug difference. Its two papers share one trained cohort. Marker “blunting” based only on within-arm significance is weaker evidence. The aged-rat BUBR1-staining paper remains abstract-only, so neither protein restoration nor general ineffectiveness is established.

**[Potentilla extract and ECGU](../workflow/track2-drug-census/potentilla-qualification.md) add genuine preclinical protection signals:** the tested extract had 2/10 stomach carcinomas versus 10/10 exposed controls at day 260. Both preparations reduced premature chromosome separation in chemically exposed mice; ECGU had no corresponding tumor-assessment arm. BUBR1 was not assayed, the four ECGU protein-marker comparisons were nonsignificant, and source reporting issues remain. These results warrant follow-up without establishing congenital MVA rescue.

[Taurine](../workflow/track2-drug-census/taurine-qualification.md) retains a muscle-physiology reserve. The [comparative ledger](../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md) preserves other measured benefits and adverse findings, including formulation, intervention, source-access and tissue limits. None is converted into MVA efficacy by a gene mention or a benefit in another disease.

Source-qualified comparisons distinguish measured endpoints. [PDLSC conditioned medium](../workflow/track2-drug-census/pdls-secretome-qualification.md) has a context-dependent BUB1B RNA signal and mineralization benefit without an identified active ingredient. [Farnesol](../workflow/track2-drug-census/zoledronate-cenpf-qualification.md) partly restores CENP-F localization during a drug challenge. [MLN8054 and TCS7010](../workflow/track2-drug-census/cenpe-feedback-qualification.md) improve chromosome congression in CENP-E-inhibited, non-transformed RPE-1 cells; alisertib is a separate short-exposure null. The latter study measures neither BUBR1 restoration nor faithful daughter-cell output, and its original preprint remains unverified. [KT-NB](../workflow/track2-drug-census/kalanchoe-mitosis-qualification.md) instead causes mitotic defects and inhibits its normal-cell comparator. The [ledger](../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md) retains these positive and negative comparisons without assigning MVA efficacy.

Other common molecules contribute different endpoints. [Putrescine dihydrochloride](../workflow/track2-drug-census/putrescine-reproduction-qualification.md) reduced mouse embryo resorption; the larger aggregate live-pup count did not accompany significant litter-rate or litter-size differences. [L-ascorbic acid](../workflow/track2-drug-census/ascorbate-oocyte-qualification.md) improved normal chromosome counts and premature sister-chromatid separation in Sod1-deficient oocytes. The BUBR1 measurements in that study compare untreated genotypes, so they cannot establish drug-mediated restoration. These remain preclinical comparators.

**[Spermidine](../workflow/track2-drug-census/spermidine-reproduction-qualification.md) adds direct meiotic chromosome-count evidence:** source mean aneuploidy fell from 31.84% to 22.61% after injection and from 29.46% to 19.83% in a separate oral mouse arm. Its Bub1b RNA change was downward (source Q=0.1013), without demonstrated protein restoration; full Methods remain inaccessible. [CoQ10](../workflow/track2-drug-census/coq10-reproduction-qualification.md) preserves mouse reproductive benefits alongside a nonpositive Bub1b RNA result and null oral human trial. [Mitoquinone](../workflow/track2-drug-census/mitoquinone-reproduction-qualification.md) improves later embryo development after a mouse-oocyte culture challenge, with maturation/fertilization nulls. These separate endpoints support research comparisons, not a common efficacy score.

[TG101209](../workflow/track2-drug-census/tg101209-lung-qualification.md) adds a separate post-challenge lung-protection comparison: two respiratory outcomes improve significantly and four do not. Its favorable survival-curve direction remains descriptive. That study measures no BUB1B, and does not validate the earlier thermal-probe result or establish a human exposure bridge.

The [population simulation](../workflow/track2-model/README.md) shows why apparent improvement needs absolute-output controls: synthetic selective killing raised the chromosome-normal fraction **16.8 percentage points while differentiated-cell time fell 11.0%**. This is a selection effect with output loss, not a drug result or patient forecast; faithful individual-lineage tracking remains an experimental requirement.

The [TRIP13 worked example](../workflow/track2-transfer/README.md) reconstructs synthetic homozygous Arg354Ter and a positional RNA-decay prediction. BUBR1-restoration evidence does not transfer, and none of three reviewed routes yields a qualified lead. Two workflow executions are not two-patient validation.

[Muscle RNA reanalysis](../workflow/track2-rna/README.md) covered **nine mice, 12,989 genes and 150 pathway comparisons**. None passed global correction; mTORC1 had **P=0.219, FDR=0.983**. This neither validates nor refutes the phosphoprotein observation and provides no independent biological replication or drug ranking.

The [census and source audits](../workflow/track2-drug-census/README.md) bound discovery coverage: **15,357 main catalog/supplement entities**, separately **6,467 NIH ingredient groups**, **3,971 FDA food rows** and **249 SGC labels**—not unique-molecule totals or efficacy assessments. All **234 food-query rows** are annotated across 213 source IDs; **212 available abstracts** were read and [one unavailable abstract](../workflow/track2-drug-census/fatty-acid-binding-source-limit.md) receives no credit. The ledger has **233 appraisals**. [The original revalidation queue is complete](../workflow/track2-drug-census/batch15-revalidation-findings.md): **3,598 accepted literal revalidations; 5,759 combined outer source/ID records; zero pending in that selection**. Earlier annotations were not independently re-audited, and primary follow-up and broader discovery remain incomplete.

## A partner experiment that can answer the question

**Laboratory partnership and patient-derived model access are unestablished.** The [pipeline](appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md) starts with affected/corrected myogenic cultures, procedural controls, allele-product measurements and a reproducible functional deficit. Apply the cis stop early; engineered models must retain unresolved alternatives. Failed model qualification is not drug failure.

Test the candidates separately with measured, justified exposures: niacin versus vehicle; apitegromab versus matched nonbinding isotype. Qualify NAD/BUBR1 dependence or myostatin engagement. Measure absolute evoked muscle output, repeated-stimulation performance, progenitor replacement and chromosome fidelity in both differentiating progenitors and established muscle.

**Either cohort may improve if the other, renewable reserve and fidelity meet prespecified preservation margins.** Marker changes, arrested divisions and favorable survivor percentages cannot substitute. Freeze one regimen per candidate; confirm in independent preparations with masking and Holm correction across four drug-by-cohort claims. Everolimus remains exploratory unless separately prespecified. General benefit in corrected and affected cells may count, but does not establish BUBR1 restoration. Inadequate exposure or an absent target system leaves a nonresponse uninterpretable.

Recorded rhabdomyosarcoma requires [tumor/regimen compatibility review](appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md#rhabdomyosarcoma-evidence-and-treatment-compatibility); current status and regimen are unknown. Culture benefit cannot settle fracture, developmental or recurrence risk. The reusable contribution is the sequence-to-mechanism-to-function workflow and its absolute-output decision rule, not a new drug discovery. Drug exposures and tissue models require requalification; models remain the experimental bottleneck.
