# Creatine stroke appraisal summary

**H_CF: controlled human function evidence, with no significant controlled benefit in this trial.** Creatine at 20 g/day for seven days after acute ischemic stroke did not significantly improve the reported functional, strength, muscle-mass, or degradation outcomes versus maltodextrin under shared protein and mobility care. The small sample, broad intervals, missing denominators, and reporting conflicts preclude equivalence or a general conclusion that creatine fails.

Exploratory PGRN and creatinine interactions do not establish BUB1B rescue or functional mediation. Diarrhea, fullness, creatinine rises, severe events, and deaths remain explicit. Broad renal safety is unproven. The chemical form and brand are unknown, and preparation aliases remain empty.
