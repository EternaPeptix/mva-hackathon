# Finding functional evidence beyond gene mentions

The earlier automated food, probe and supplement screens reused a BUB1B/BUBR1/MVA query collection. That is useful for mechanisms but can miss functional intervention reports that do not mention the gene. This complementary search starts from neuromuscular disease, nutrition and measured function, without requiring gene mentions or favorable results.

It led to three targeted source appraisals: [pediatric FSHD creatine](creatine-fshd-qualification.md) did not meet its primary motor-function endpoint; the [specific butyrate and Vivomix preparations](gut-muscle-qualification.md) have reported within-arm functional improvements but no established randomized between-arm benefit in the recovered analyses. Creatine and probiotic scientific mains were read with their stated supplementary/native evidence; butyrate remains limited to its abstract and incomplete indexed primary sections. These distinctions inform research priority without proving MVA efficacy or changing the provisional nominees.

## Search coverage

Three Europe PMC queries were frozen before retrieval. The [structured companion](outcome-first-discovery.json) contains their exact text, source bindings and scope. Queries used title/abstract terms for study design and function. No outcome-direction, human, publication-type, gene, catalog-match or abstract-presence filter excluded records from the collection.

An independent audit reproduced every canonical record, query membership and count from the raw responses. All 63 pagination responses followed the declared cursor chains, with stable reported totals and no observed within-query duplicates or cross-query core differences. Raw requests, responses and derived queues remain outside Git. This establishes the recorded retrieval, not an atomic snapshot of the live index or complete literature coverage.

The known open-label niacin report was absent among an additional eleven-source reference check. The specific query component responsible was not adjudicated. This is a documented search limitation, not an efficacy negative. Unlisted disease abbreviations, endpoint names, preparations and sparse abstracts remain possible gaps. Broad terms admit exercise, device, laboratory, review and protocol records. No eligibility or efficacy conclusion follows merely from a query hit.

## First eligibility pilot

Forty fixed records from the neuromuscular collection were screened at available-abstract depth: 32 context records, four controlled human substance/function reports, one uncontrolled human report, one preclinical functional report and two unresolved records. The substance reports include physiological challenges and anesthesia studies; they are not all candidate treatments. One saved dissertation abstract is incomplete and retains a provisional context route.

A second reviewer checked four preselected records and two disputed cases scientifically, plus identities and hashes for all forty. A retracted vecuronium/stimulation-method record remains flagged rather than becoming drug evidence. Medication-use advice without resolved agents and exposure remains uncertain. No records were discarded or assigned a compound-wide negative.

The original pilot left **56,177 collection records unscreened by that pilot**. Its sampling and reading record remains unchanged. Separately targeted primary appraisals have their own source depth and do not silently increase screening coverage.

## Current eligibility coverage

Seven completed batches, including the original forty-record pilot, cover **680 disjoint source/ID records**, leaving **55,537 unscreened**. The latest batch includes 160 complete saved abstracts at their available depth; two sources explicitly truncate their abstracts at 250 words. Review order uses the same design, administration and function flags and query-stratum proportions, without ranking favorable results. Every unselected record remains queued.

The latest batch received **33 second scientific checks**: sixteen predeclared interval samples and seventeen flagged boundaries. All 160 identities, available literal source anchors and notice metadata were checked. Earlier checks and their limits remain recorded. These are bounded checks with reader discussion, not a blind double review of every record.

Measured function with an omitted treatment estimate remains eligible for follow-up, without being counted as demonstrated benefit. One classification changed from a biomarker-only route to provisional controlled function because the source explicitly reports a cycling time trial and measured completion time; its treatment performance contrast is still unreported. The original annotation and adjudication are preserved. Earlier infeasible grip testing remains distinct from actual measurement. Clinical symptoms, mechanical force, tissue mechanics, biochemical assays and exploratory clinical events remain separately identified. Formulations, mixtures and biological preparations are not unique chemical-drug counts.

Two latest records retain unresolved scope: a mixed stroke-rehabilitation package containing botulinum toxin and a pooled post-hospital pilot reporting clinical readmission/mortality outcomes. Unresolved contrasts do not erase their observations. An uncontrolled carvedilol run-in does not gain randomized functional credit from later randomization. A mixture's favorable mean time-to-fatigue estimate retains its zero-crossing interval rather than becoming a demonstrated benefit. Prior retraction/version links, missing sources and unresolved records remain intact. No compound-wide negative follows from routing or omitted results.

Targeted follow-ups retain the earlier [methylcobalamin](methylcobalamin-als-qualification.md), [sildenafil](sildenafil-dystrophinopathy-qualification.md), [adult creatine](creatine-inflammatory-myopathy-qualification.md), [L-glutamine](glutamine-dmd-qualification.md), [ACE-031](ace031-dmd-qualification.md), [2003 creatine](creatine2003-dystrophinopathy-qualification.md) and [geriatric metformin](metformin-geriatric-function-qualification.md) qualifications at their separately declared reading depths. Partial papers and registry-only observations retain their access and inference limits.

The [Myoki/CG-A1 qualification](myoki-muscle-qualification.md) credits reported lean-mass gain while retaining functional confidence-interval/P-value inconsistencies, participant-count conflicts and unresolved exposure. The [lacto-vegetarian soup qualification](lacto-vegetarian-soup-qualification.md) credits the reported final-week comparative SPPB/chair-rise coefficients, with unspecified model construction, unmatched nutrition and preparation-identity limits. Neither supplies MVA efficacy or changes the provisional nominees. The quercetin-glycoside exercise trial and MAX-EPA rheumatoid-arthritis crossover remain next source questions, without full-paper qualification credit.

The independent PASS concerns retrieval and bounded review fidelity. It does not establish that every drug or common compound has been enumerated, that every report has been read, or that the nominated candidates are superior.
