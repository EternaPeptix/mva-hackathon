# CENP-E / Aurora feedback: bounded source qualification

The published study identifies **centrinone, MLN8054 and TCS7010 as experimental routes that improve a restricted chromosome-congression endpoint after CENP-E perturbation**. It does not show improved post-division chromosome fidelity, useful tissue function, BubR1 restoration, or MVA rescue. **Alisertib is a tested negative comparator at the reported exposure.** These results support mechanistic assay comparisons; an MVA restoration nomination does not follow from this study alone.

## Genetic evidence does not broaden drug efficacy

In HeLa, induced **GFP-Hec1-9A**, a nine-site phospho-null mutant, did not compensate for CENP-E perturbation when endogenous Hec1 remained. After endogenous Hec1 depletion, Hec1-9A improved chromosome congression following **CENP-E depletion**, with chromosome spread and polar counts resembling CENP-E-intact controls. It **failed with CENP-E inhibition**, in both synchronized and unsynchronized workflows. The experiment used fixed cells under proteasome arrest, not offspring karyotypes. The proposed explanation involving fibrous-corona expansion is mechanistic interpretation. It cannot be converted into efficacy of doxycycline, MG-132, another small molecule, or a universal CENP-E/BubR1 bypass. Fig5, S3i–j and study limitations.

## Models, BUB1B boundary and later outcomes

The main non-transformed model is **hTERT-immortalized human RPE-1**, with fluorescent kinetochore/centriole reporters. U2OS osteosarcoma cells test CENP-E-T422A and other CENP-E perturbations; HeLa cervical carcinoma cells test Hec1-9A. These are cultured cell-line systems, not primary normal tissue, MVA patient cells, a BUB1B hypomorph, or an in vivo restoration model. Methods explicitly says no cell lines were authenticated. Monthly mycoplasma checks are reported negative; Methods names SiR-DNA/Hoechst whereas the reporting summary uses DAPI wording. There is no patient/donor inference.

**BubR1 is discussed and cited, not measured as a treatment response in this study.** Introduction links its CENP-E interaction to the companion publication (reference11); Discussion includes CENP-E/BubR1 in a proposed feedback balance. The measured molecular endpoints here include pAurA-T288, pAurB-T232, pKnl1-S24/S60, pHec1-S55 and Hec1/CENP-E perturbation controls. No BUB1B RNA, BubR1 protein abundance, stability, localization, kinase/scaffold activity, or BubR1-specific rescue is established in the main scientific text, native figures and supplied Supplementary Information examined. The companion paper and other references have **not** been read in this pass and contribute no imported efficacy.

The live imaging follows mitosis and some anaphase entry; that is not a post-division fidelity assay. No measured reduction in lagging chromosomes, micronuclei, aneuploid daughter cells, or chromosome missegregation rates is supplied, and no viable, faithful daughter-cell yield or durable normal-cell function is established. Methods selected only cells that entered mitosis **and subsequently entered anaphase during imaging** for LLSM tracking, and excluded multipolar spindles. Polar residence ends at either congression **or anaphase entry with the chromosome still uncongressed**. Thus a shorter residence cannot itself prove successful alignment. The paper explicitly warns, citing other work, that weak Aurora activity can impair attachment-error correction and increase missegregation; that warning is not a harm rate measured here. Direct biochemical Aurora A→Aurora B interaction also remains unproven.

## Source discrepancies and rigor limits

- **Fig4 identity conflict:** the final legend abbreviation says “MLN, MLN8237”, including in the original publisher PDF page6. The native panel labels, Results, Methods and Supplementary Table1 distinguish positive **MLN8054** from separately tested **alisertib/MLN8237**. Preserve the conflict; assign findings using the explicit panel identities, not the erroneous shared abbreviation.
- **Fig5 schedule conflict:** its schematic shows3 h from MG-132 addition to fixation, whereas Methods gives5 h for the unsynchronized Hec1-9A assay and2 h MG-132 after2 h release for the synchronized assay. The schematic also shows18 h where Methods gives16 h RO-3306. This limits precise protocol reconstruction, without erasing the genotype-specific result. Fig5's caption also mentions MLN8054 without an evident corresponding labeled drug arm; no additional result is inferred from that wording.
- Main Methods says experiments were not randomized and generally not blinded; the reporting summary describes within-regime random assignment and no other randomization. No formal sample-size calculation is reported. Cell/kinetochore observations and pooled dishes do not establish donor-level replication. No independent raw-image reanalysis was performed.
