# Wang JCI 2024: PP2A inhibition and BRCA2-deficient tumors

**Source-qualified mechanistic and tumor evidence; no MVA nomination.** The actual PP2A inhibitor is **LB100**. Its drug-treated outcomes are AURKB phosphorylation/attachment disruption, reduced clonogenic survival and tumor suppression in specific BRCA2/SAC contexts. **Ceubxn-2/NSFL1C loss supplies the worm and cell survival rescue; it is genetic and operates in the opposite checkpoint direction.** The additional drug arms use **barasertib**, **NMS-873** and **olaparib**. ## Normal controls, animal accounting and numerical limits

At LB100 2.5 µM, MCF10A normalized clonogenic means are approximately **86.7% control-siRNA, 70.8% BRCA2-siRNA, 79.5% NSFL1C-siRNA and 3.1% double-siRNA** (Fig 7A). At LB100 10 µM, VC-8 plus BRCA2 retains about **46.0%**, versus **15.2%** for VC-8 (S7B). These provide model-specific relative sensitivity, not universal normal-cell sparing or safety in BUB1B-deficient muscle. In LNCaP, BRCA2-only cells also show substantial loss at 5 µM (about 26.2% survival), so “weak effect on BRCA2-only cells” cannot be generalized across models.

The VC-8 xenograft experiment uses five-week-old female nude mice, **10 per group**, with LB100 **2 mg/kg intraperitoneally every two days**, beginning two weeks after inoculation. Native Fig 7C labels its time axis “days after treatment”; the workbook labels it after implantation. This discrepancy is retained. Reduced tumor growth is credited; body-weight nonsignificance is not comprehensive safety.

The PC3M-2B4-Luc experiment reports **35 implanted**, but its randomized allocations total **30**: DMSO 7, olaparib 8, LB100 7, combination 8. The five-animal difference is unexplained. Main Methods reports one combination death on day 28 and one olaparib death on day 30, without cause adjudication. Fig 7G and S7I workbooks label the corresponding columns “death” in the first day 10 row and leave later rows blank, leaving **seven numeric trajectories per arm at every displayed time**. This is an omission/accounting issue; it does not mean those animals died at baseline. No missing-data sensitivity analysis is supplied. Treatment follows the graphic in Fig 7G, with BLI on days 10/18/25/31; no continuous daily regimen is inferred.

Two further source inconsistencies matter:

- Fig 4C (HeLa) and Fig 6E (LNCaP) contain the **same six percentages** in B4:G4, despite distinct model labels and different native significance symbols. Their identity requires clarification; neither result is erased or counted as independently reconciled numerical replication.
- S7H's value sheet reproduces Fig 7E's PC3M-2B4 single-contrast numbers under a LNCaP/5-µM-LB100 label. It does not supply all eight genotype/drug series displayed in native S7H. Exact S7H contrasts are therefore not reconstructed from that sheet.

The figures report means ± SEM and tests, but many comparisons lack exact P values, confidence intervals, multiplicity/post-hoc details or explicit cell-to-biological-repeat mapping. BLI randomization is stated; general blinding and comprehensive exclusion/power rules were not established. No new inferential tests were run.

## Relationship to BUBR1 and the current ledger
