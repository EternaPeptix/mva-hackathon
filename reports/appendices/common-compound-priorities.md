# Common-compound priority matrix

**4 October 2026 · Current priority synthesis; source-specific reading dates retained.** Nineteen decision-relevant candidates, not a ranking of all reviewed compounds. Source-specific positives and nulls remain paired. Controlled functional comparisons, within-arm observations and mechanistic findings carry distinct evidential weight; no nomination change or original-queue reading credit follows.

**Several challengers have better-controlled evidence for specific human functions than niacin; none has demonstrated a larger clinical benefit or superiority in MVA.** NR, creatine, urolithin A, resveratrol, the tested Lovaza formulation, astaxanthin study preparations, FSHD antioxidant combination and the COCOA-PAD beverage are substantive comparisons. The tested BODYBALANCE collagen and MPC80 drink add secondary strength or mobility observations with distinct controls and limits. L-carnitine and calcium-HMB add narrower controlled signals. NMN and vitamin K2 have more direct compound-associated BUBR1 observations. Amla / *Phyllanthus emblica* is separately elevated as a common-consumer preclinical checkpoint/fidelity challenger, but not a nominated medicine. These are separate reasons to prioritize experiments, not a single efficacy order.

The current EPA [CPDat v4.1 Formulation](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/cpdat-v4.1/README.md) and [Article](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/cpdat-v4.1/articles/README.md) overlays add new product-kind coverage beyond medicines and supplements. Source-qualified ibuprofen and naproxen findings are lower BUB1B RNA in high-concentration nonhuman-primate Sertoli cells; a separate human granulosa-cell study reports lower BUB1B RNA after high-concentration MnBP exposure. Parthenolide separately disrupts kinetochore-microtubule attachment. None offers a BUB1B-restoration or MVA muscle-benefit signal, so neither nominee changes. These exposure/context observations do not establish harm from ordinary use.

Niacin’s human muscle-NAD engagement remains useful, but five myopathy patients and ten **treated** healthy controls provide no placebo-controlled functional contrast; follow-up durations also differ. Pediatric progression despite NAD correction shows why engagement alone is insufficient. Its BUBR1 bridge relies on **NMN and genetic SIRT2**, not niacin rescue. [Existing evidence table](../../reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md).

A low-dose 32-day crossover mixture (4 mg/day nicotinic acid plus nicotinamide and tryptophan) raised muscle methyl-nicotinamide but not muscle NAD+ and did not improve objective muscle or mitochondrial outcomes. This is useful counterevidence to generic NAD-precursor claims, not a high-dose niacin monotherapy test; see the [source qualification](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/nad-precursor-mixture-muscle-qualification.md).

Apitegromab retains the strongest documented pediatric **primary motor-function** comparison in this selected set: pooled SAPPHIRE HFMSE difference 1.8 points (95% CI 0.30–3.32). It was measured in SMA on SMN therapy. Full-source review confirms that the standalone 20 mg/kg primary test failed and stopped the hierarchy; the 10 mg/kg point estimate and secondary outcomes are exploratory. Fractures were 5/53 versus 1/50 at the labeled-dose comparison, and EMBRAZE supplied no notable functional gain despite lean-mass retention. This remains a compensation candidate, not MVA evidence. [Full SAPPHIRE qualification](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-sapphire-fulltext-qualification.md); [broader appraisal](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-review.md).

## Additional common-compound screen finding outside the nineteen-candidate matrix

### Amla / *Phyllanthus emblica* fruit extract — priority consumer-derived assay challenger

The 2016 study tested aqueous fruit extract at **20–160 µg/mL** in spontaneously immortalized NCM460 colon epithelial cells with high background genomic instability. It reported higher BubR1 mRNA after 72 hours and fewer measured mitotic/genomic abnormalities. The anaphase-to-metaphase proxy used a separate four-hour exposure plus one-hour release; a lower ratio can also reflect mitotic slowing. No BUBR1 protein or causal assay connects these observations. [Guo and Wang, PMID 27598149](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5037716/).

Two related papers from the same group add a cell-state question rather than independent BUBR1 replication. A 2013 study reported increased chromosomal instability and apoptosis in COLO320 cancer cells. A 2017 study reported increased chemotherapy-associated growth suppression in Colo205, and fewer measured damage endpoints with reduced colony formation, mitotic arrest and apoptosis in NCM460. Different preparations, doses and contexts prevent a matched tumor-selectivity claim. Arrest or loss of damaged cells could reduce usable progenitor output; MVA cells might respond adversely. Only the 2016 paper measured BubR1, as mRNA. [2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25305222/), [2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5742286/); [full source qualification and dosing discrepancy](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/catalog-independent-review.md#amla--phyllanthus-emblica-aqueous-fruit-extract--chromosome-endpoints-and-cell-state-risk).

NIH DSLD retains separate on-market spellings: “Amla” (340), “Amla Fruit Extract” (92), “Amla extract” (77), “Amla fruit extract” (57), and “Phyllanthus emblica fruit extract” (7). These counts are not summed and do not identify the tested extract or establish systemic exposure. Across the papers, no patient-allele, skeletal-muscle or functional full-length BUBR1 rescue was established. Amla earns a **high-priority laboratory comparison among reviewed consumer preparations**, while niacin and apitegromab retain the distinct submission roles explained in the [candidate shortlist](track2-candidate-shortlist.md).

Use authenticated, compositionally characterized extract lots, vehicle/concentration controls and affected/corrected myogenic cells. Measure full-length functional BUBR1, chromosome segregation, division rate, absolute myogenic output, viability and progenitor renewal, with a matched tumor counterscreen. An apparent fall in damage frequency must survive the absolute-output and selection checks before this preparation can advance.

The other high-prevalence gene-linked labels did not yield the same kind of restorative signal. “Chondroitin sulfate” occurs in 899 on-market labels, but the linked [intervention paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39583738/) tested fucosylated sea-cucumber chondroitin, listed BUB1B computationally, and measured BUB1/PLK1 rather than BUB1B; the separate [human glucosamine/chondroitin study](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/glucosamine-chondroitin-qualification.md) measured CRP and an unvalidated plasma antibody signal. “Saffron” appears in 45 labels, but the [checkpoint paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30710515/) used purified crocin to disrupt tubulin and arrest cancer cells. Thymoquinone appears in 34 labels, but in the co-mentioned [PLK1-inhibitor paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25975351/) the BubR1 checkpoint signal came from BI 2536/GSK461364, while thymoquinone produced S-phase/p21 changes. These are not equivalent BUBR1-restoration candidates; they help explain why Amla merits a controlled assay while the current leaders remain unchanged.

[Schisandra chinensis extract](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/schisandra-muscle-qualification.md) produced two small adult randomized-trial strength signals: one quadriceps group-by-time effect and one adjusted knee-extension torque difference. The 2017 conference abstract is treated as a probable overlap with the 2020 UMIN cohort, not a third trial. The 2021 registry specifies bodyweight-normalized torque, while the publisher-indexed primary table reports absolute Nm; the full paper, supplement and SAP were unavailable in the bounded independent check. Neither study measured daily mobility benefit, BUB1B/BUBR1, aneuploidy or MVA. Retain it as a supplementary muscle-force reserve, not a nominee or restoration claim; it remains outside the curated drug-decision ledger.

[Oral ATP supplement](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/atp-muscle-qualification.md) has small human performance signals, including a 12-week trained-male resistance study, an acute 18-man fatigue crossover with no strength-indicator improvement, and an abstract-only postoperative knee-replacement trial. The 15-day registered total-work outcome was nonpositive in a separate crossover fatigue study; oral bioavailability studies did not find increased systemic ATP, and the chronic positive publications share a trial program and placebo participants. The evidence does not establish BUB1B/BUBR1, chromosome-fidelity or MVA benefit. Retain as a supplementary low-cost functional comparator, not a nominee or an independent replication.

[New Zealand blackcurrant extract](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/blackcurrant-muscle-qualification.md) has a small, seven-day crossover signal in resistance-trained adults: selected strength and volume outcomes favored extract, while Wingate performance was null. The no-capsule period always came first and placebo itself improved leg-press outcomes, so practice/expectancy effects matter. No BUB1B/MVA result or durable daily function was measured. Retain as an appendix-level food-derived performance reserve, not a nominee or evidence for isolated anthocyanins.

[Bovine colostrum](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/bovine-colostrum-muscle-qualification.md) has one product-specific older-adult training study with a lower-body strength contrast, but lean mass, upper-body strength, muscle thickness and serum IGF-1 were not better than the whey comparator. The paper itself cautioned about type I error; the trial registry was first posted after the study began, and the preparation is not interchangeable with generic colostrum. Related trials show endpoint-specific mixed results. This is an appendix-level nutritional compensation hypothesis, not a BUBR1 route or nominee.
