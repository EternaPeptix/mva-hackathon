# What the broad drug search changes

**Adopted research comparison, 21 September 2026.** The broad search changed the proposal: **niacin for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. The report, methods, experimental plan and revised narration now use this pair. Everolimus remains a conditional mTORC1 mechanism probe. Neither primary candidate has demonstrated MVA rescue; this is a research-priority judgment, not an estimate of treatment efficacy.

The census screens 15,357 entities plus 40 FDA 2026 product entries assessed separately, with 259 curated route/compound appraisals. Retrieval and co-mentions do not establish every drug's efficacy; missing evidence remains UNKNOWN. The revised local movie is complete. At the existing Vimeo URL, **V3 replacement is verified**. Track 2 remains unsubmitted.

The [three-paper comparison](vascular-enzyme-qualification.md) retains
anti-KLK8 antibody as an organ-protection research lead, K858's vascular benefit,
and a BUB1B RNA response to alglucosidase alfa in Pompe myocytes. Catalogue-listed
research reagents are included on their measured merits. None yet justifies
replacing the provisional niacin/apitegromab pair.

## The same comparison for leaders and challengers

The separate [Broad Hub crosscheck](broad-hub-review.md) screens another catalog's
7,540 overlapping source names, without inflating the main denominator. Its
2,148 lexical mentions are discovery pointers; an unmapped name is not a new
medicine, and a co-mention is not a beneficial intervention.

We compare mechanism relevance, actual-drug functional evidence, transfer to this
genotype and tissue, exposure/eligibility, and harms. No numerical efficacy or
weighted winner score is inferred from missing experiments. A challenger does not
have to demonstrate actual-allele rescue before being considered when neither
current candidate has done so. Every candidate faces the same eventual requirement:
greater absolute muscle function with preserved renewal and chromosome fidelity.

## Cancer control and mitotic timing are distinct positives

The [unesbulin appraisal](unesbulin-qualification.md) brought the ledger to **143** at that stage.
The official randomized LMS trial reports an incremental combination benefit:
unesbulin plus dacarbazine versus placebo plus dacarbazine, median PFS 3.6 versus
2.6 months, HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.45–0.83), P=.0017 in the 209/105 modified ITT
population. The study was terminated for a stated business decision; this must
not be relabeled as demonstrated efficacy failure. Overall-survival medians
were unestimable. Safety uses 238/120 treated participants, with serious febrile
neutropenia in 8/0; raw events are not all attributable to unesbulin.

binding and GBM-model survival benefit. ATRT results remain conference-level
and potentially overlapping. These findings support cancer research, without
establishing selective BMI1 action, BUBR1 restoration or MVA muscle benefit.
LMS results cannot establish rhabdomyosarcoma treatment compatibility.
