# Ordinary curcumin and dehydrozingerone: Cdc27/APC/C qualification

Ordinary laboratory curcumin produced real, dose- and cell-cycle-dependent cancer-cell effects: delayed progression, LDH release and apoptosis-related changes, accompanied by Cdc27/APC/C biochemical changes. Dehydrozingerone (DHZ, “half-curcumin”) was an actually tested, much less cytotoxic structural comparator. Both receive distinct appraisals. This study does not establish BUB1B restoration, normal-tissue functional compensation, MVA efficacy or oral-supplement exposure equivalence. [Lee and Langhans, BMC Cancer 2012; PMID22280307](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2407-12-44).

The complete scientific main text and captions, all five native main figures, and all six supplementary TIFFs (two tables and four figures) were read. The five main-text outputs contained 39,671 characters and observed endmarkers without truncation; the abstract was read separately. Exact files, hashes and reading declarations are retained externally in `read-evidence.json` and `asset-manifest.json`. The saved Europe PMC metadata has no correction/retraction/version relation; that is a bounded metadata finding. ## Identity and overlap

The Methods specify Sigma-Aldrich curcumin and DHZ, with no nanoparticle delivery system. Purity, lot/catalog identifier and measured exposure were not supplied. This is distinct from the existing `new-gemini-cur` appraisal for mPEG urethane gemini surfactant nanoparticles in HCT116 cells and from the separate oocyte curcumin null discussed in `oocyte-metabolism-qualification.md`. Neither preparation nor a generic turmeric product inherits this paper’s results.

LDH figures specify three independent experiments and SEM. These are cytotoxicity/cell-cycle results, without a clonogenic, chromosome-fidelity or normal-function rescue endpoint. DHZ’s weak response is limited to the stated assays; it does not establish general inactivity, safety or MVA benefit.

## What the mechanism supports

Curcumin-linked Sepharose recovered Cdc27 from DAOY lysate, preferentially the higher/phosphorylated band, with matched beads and two bead preparations. A BubR1 immunoblot was included as the nonspecific-binding control. Thus **BubR1 was assayed**, but this was not total-protein restoration, BUB1B expression or functional rescue. Phosphatase experiments support Cdc27 phosphorylation, while dose/time-dependent high-molecular-weight bands and the DHZ comparison support a structure-dependent biochemical effect. [Native Figure 3](https://bmccancer.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2407-12-44/figures/3).

These observations do not establish purified direct binding, a binding constant, a mapped covalent site or exclusive Cdc27 causality. The affinity experiment used lysate; the ubiquitination assay used lysates from treated cells with added UbcH10, ubiquitin and labeled cyclin fragment, rather than purified APC/C exposed to a defined inhibitor concentration. Cdc27-resistant rescue or necessity/sufficiency experiments were not reported. The association between basal phospho-Cdc27 and sensitivity is not a validated predictive biomarker. APC/C restraint nevertheless provides a concrete timing hypothesis downstream of the spindle assembly checkpoint. This paper demonstrates restraint and death, without showing that the added time yields faithful division or healthy output.

## Normal-cell, animal and exposure boundaries

MDCK showed relatively little cytotoxicity and little change in mitotic-release profiles under these conditions. It is an established canine kidney-derived epithelial line, not primary normal human tissue; the paper gives no exact subclone authentication. [Official ATCC MDCK lineage description](https://www.atcc.org/products/ccl-34). Native Figure 4B also contains an **HBEC** blot block omitted from the caption’s line list and undefined in Methods. It is not a Figure 4C/D functional comparison arm and cannot support normal-human safety or efficacy. No normal-muscle function, MVA cells or BUB1B-genotype rescue was measured.

Supplementary Figure S1 contains a real animal-tumor protein comparison: curcumin-treated versus corn-oil-control Smo/Smo medulloblastoma specimens, with three labeled lanes per arm. The authors report lower Cdc27, but **no detectable crosslinked Cdc27 in vivo**. The caption refers to mice treated as previously described, without an animal/specimen map; this paper supplies no new animal dosing regimen, tumor-growth/survival experiment, PK measurement or measured tumor concentration. The cited 2011 survival/xenograft benefits are now separately [qualified](curcumin-animal-qualification.md). This 2012 blot is not an independent animal-efficacy replication, and exact specimen reuse is unproven. Lane count does not establish independent animal number. Broad background statements about absence of side effects do not replace measured safety endpoints. [Full primary and associated supplementary files](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3296673/).

The paper itself acknowledges limited bioavailability. Neither ordinary dietary turmeric nor an oral curcumin/DHZ product is shown to attain these experimental exposures. No pediatric or MVA harm is inferred from the cancer-cell killing results.

## Source precision and decision

Table S2 clarifies an important denominator: the main-text 91.5%, 47.7% and 69.8% “remaining G2/M” values correspond to the measured 64.16%, 33.43% and 48.95% divided by the initial 70.15% G2/M fraction. They are not whole-culture percentages or individually tracked trajectories of the originally arrested cells. Table S1’s claim that 56.9% had not reentered G0/G1 at 20 h is unresolved: the actual 20 µM values are G0/G1 27.44%, S 29.53% and G2/M 43.06%. No lineage-fate reconstruction is justified. Figure 5C also has a preparation mismatch: its caption specifies double-thymidine arrest/8-hour exposure, whereas the Results discuss G2/M release. The exact arithmetic and source cells are preserved in `table-value-check.json`.

Separate `new-curcumin-cdc27` and `new-dehydrozingerone` entries preserve these findings. Thymidine, nocodazole, DMSO, corn oil, MG132 and radiolabel/reaction components retain their experimental roles rather than becoming beneficial-treatment claims. This source does not displace provisional niacin/apitegromab or the conditional everolimus probe: it supplies neither BUB1B restoration nor normal functional compensation, while those nominees also remain unproved for MVA. The [separate 2011 qualification](curcumin-animal-qualification.md) retains actual mouse benefits and unresolved dose/exposure limits, without extending this study’s cell results to animal PK.
