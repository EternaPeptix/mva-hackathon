## Primary-source checks

The load-bearing source claims checked in this review were materially consistent with the revised report:

- **Protein insufficiency:** the original patient-cell work supports decreased transcript supply and instability of selected missense products. Its approximately 6% versus 13% HeLa titration supports an abundance hypothesis, not a therapeutic threshold. It does not measure N1002K. The report preserves that distinction. [Suijkerbuijk 2010, author manuscript](https://www.hubrecht.eu/app/uploads/2015/08/Kops_Research_2010_Suijkerbuijk_Molecular-Causes-for-BUBR1-Dysfunction-in-the-Human-Cancer-Predisposition-Syndrome-Mosaic-Variegated-Aneuploidy.pdf)
- **NAD/SIRT2 bridge:** the cited study connects SIRT2 to BUBR1 abundance, uses NMN for precursor experiments, and reports male-survival/cardiac findings from genetic SIRT2 elevation. These are distinct interventions. Neither that evidence nor the current computation establishes niacin rescue of mutant skeletal muscle. Direct PMC full-text retrieval encountered a browser challenge; the indexed primary article and publisher figure information supported the checked distinction. [North 2014](https://www.embopress.org/doi/pdf/10.15252/embj.201386907)
- **Human niacin evidence:** five patients and treated healthy controls were included; muscle NAD restoration and patient knee-extension change of approximately 1.1-fold are supported. Strength also improved in controls, and their follow-up duration differed. The report appropriately uses this as engagement evidence rather than disease-specific controlled efficacy. [Pirinen 2020, full paper](https://www.brennerlab.net/files/Pirinen20.pdf)
- **Pediatric counterexample:** the primary abstract reports metabolic correction with meager clinical response and continuing disease progression. That supports requiring functional benefit beyond NAD normalization. [Aaltio 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39173541/)
- **Everolimus rationale:** Figure 5C's legend confirms two carrier mice per genotype and Ponceau loading control; the stated statistical tests concern other panels. The current submission's qualitative interpretation is appropriately restrained. The fly paper supports retained neuroblast numbers after rapamycin; the autophagy genetic interventions did not restore progeny/brain size. These are neither everolimus nor BUB1B-mutant muscle rescue. [Sieben Figure 5](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863/figure/5), [González-Blanco 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13121444/)
- **Approved-drug scope:** nicotinic-acid prescription products and everolimus have approved indications. Niacin's cited label does not establish pediatric safety/effectiveness; everolimus pediatric experience is indication-specific. Neither approval establishes MVA benefit. These two core nominations fit the market-approved-medication premise; unverified quercetin status does not disqualify the core pair because it remains a conditional comparator/reserve. [Niacin label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=dd60fe81-3d54-4a29-9502-601be89c575e), [Everolimus label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=2150f73a-179b-4afc-b8ce-67c85cc72f04)

## Five prioritized weaknesses and actions

1. **Submission delivery does not yet identify this reviewed version.** The public Vimeo link still points to the superseded ordering, Track 2 is recorded as unsubmitted, and the documented exporter reads committed HEAD while substantive files/tools are uncommitted or untracked. Commit/package the reviewed content, export that exact revision, replace the pitch URL and verify access before delivery. The required acknowledgement exists in provenance but not in the standalone main report; carry it into the submitted/public standalone artifact. Complete the requested provider plan/tier and data-handling disclosures where currently unverified. These observations do not establish a data-use breach. Private GitHub status is permitted during the event, but the official instructions require public access after it ends.
2. **The actual disease mechanism remains conditional.** Matching a published stop-plus-missense architecture does not prove that this pair is biallelic, that N1002K is pathogenic, or that its deficit is primarily quantity. Phase plus actual-allele RNA/protein/function in a genetically interpretable model is the highest-value next evidence. The existing cis stop and model-qualification plan are appropriate; further prose cannot replace these observations.
3. **Niacin priority rests on an assembled, unclosed biological chain.** Human muscle NAD engagement, NMN-mediated abundance and genetic SIRT2 rescue do not jointly establish nicotinic-acid rescue of this mutant. The decisive test needs justified niacin exposure/utilization, functional BUBR1 and useful output, with causal attribution if restoration is claimed. Everolimus has a separate unqualified harmful-signaling premise. The current cautious language makes both legitimate hypotheses, but neither is yet a demonstrated therapeutic lead.
4. **The innovation and transfer demonstrations remain illustrative.** The synthetic counterexample proves that the chosen model can exhibit denominator bias; it does not show the frequency of this problem in real assays or improved decision accuracy. The TRIP13 execution tests curated logic on one additional SNV example. An independent user reproducing the workflow, an externally specified held-out example, or a real assay comparison would support broader innovation/scalability claims. Keep the current modest claims until such evidence exists.
5. **Execution feasibility is not yet established.** The staged design has clear scientific gates, but the full two-cohort functional, chromosome, exposure and tumor program requires considerable specialized capacity. Model access, staffing, throughput, cost and schedule remain unknown. The smallest useful improvement is a partner-confirmed scope for the first drug-free affected/corrected muscle qualification milestone, with required material, measurements and resource estimate. Do not fabricate power or a timetable before that qualification.

## Readiness judgment

**Ready for scientific review as a conditional research proposal; not ready for final delivery or therapeutic advancement.** I found no new load-bearing factual error requiring reversal of the current lead order. Niacin-first is defensible as a qualitative mechanism-based research priority, while the case-specific mechanism and actual drug rescue remain unproved. Resolve delivery/version issues before submitting; prioritize experimental qualification over further expansion of the literature appendix.
