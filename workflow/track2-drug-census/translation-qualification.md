# From experimental rescue to a candidate medicine

**Decision, 21 September 2026:** retain niacin and apitegromab as the nominated
restoration/compensation comparison. PAI-1 inhibition gains a credible human-stage
implementation, but its muscle compound and clinical compound differ. ConA and
the centromere experiment have actual chromosome-fidelity observations. These
findings justify comparative experiments, not demonstrated MVA efficacy or a
claim that the search produced no further leads.

The same actual-allele uncertainty applies to nominees and alternatives. The
discriminators below are the intervention actually administered, useful tissue
function, exposure, faithful completed division and a tolerable treatment window.
No clinical dose or quantitative MVA efficacy estimate is inferred.

## PAI-1: human experience improves feasibility, not the muscle efficacy claim

The [TM5484 muscle result](outer-corpus-findings.md#primary-follow-up-pai-1-produces-a-substantive-muscle-challenger)
is preventive mouse contraction benefit, with a weaker later-treatment result.
**TM5614 is a different compound.** In an open-label, single-arm study, 33 adults
with CML received TM5614 with stable tyrosine-kinase inhibitors for 48 weeks.
Eleven reached the molecular response endpoint; there was no muscle outcome or
TM5484 comparison. Measured systemic exposure does not establish equivalent
unbound muscle exposure. Results reported 59 adverse events in 25 patients,
including eight attributed events in six patients: these included conjunctival
bleeding and liver dysfunction. Reassuring discussion wording must not erase
those events. Three events termed severe in the paper, in two patients, were judged unrelated.
[Primary CML study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9972105/).

In a separate double-blind COVID-19 trial, 75 adults were randomized (39 drug,
36 placebo). The primary total-oxygenation endpoint was nonsignificant
(P=.22); Table 2 reports endpoint data for 30 and 31 participants. Oxygen-duration
results were also nonsignificant (P=.34). Adverse events occurred in 22/39 drug
recipients, with possibly related events in seven; no severe/serious related
event was reported. Exclusions included bleeding tendency and active/recent
malignancy. This trial supports bounded adult clinical experience, not positive
efficacy, pediatric safety or safety in the recorded cancer context.
[Primary randomized study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10761996/).

**Retain TM5614 as a clinical-stage implementation reserve.** It does not inherit
TM5484's force result. Direct reproduction with TM5614 would need PAI-1 engagement,
absolute and normalized force, delayed-start treatment, renewal and chromosome
fidelity. The [live Japanese trial registry](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/latest-detail/jRCT2021240049) lists phase III, recruiting, and explicitly **unapproved** status for TM5614 plus nivolumab in melanoma. This is development status, not an efficacy result or approval. Submission medicine eligibility remains unestablished. The manually added
route does not change the census's 15,357-entity denominator.

A [distinct administered PAI-1 siRNA-lipid nanoparticle study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12994142/)
reports thrombosis and Klotho-mouse survival benefit. This is RNA delivery, not
lifelong knockout or evidence for TM5614/TM5484. No muscle-function or BUB1B rescue
was established in the abstract and indexed primary passages inspected.

## Defined rescue interventions and their competitive limits

| Route | Primary evidence credited | Why it does not currently displace a nominee |
|---|---|---|
| **Concanavalin A (ConA)** | In vitrified mouse oocytes, 10 µg/mL improved chromosome-spread aneuploidy from 41.82% to 15.87% (n=46/32 oocytes; repeated-experiment means). Polar-body extrusion also improved. A separate MII-stage treatment increased blastocyst development after chemical **parthenogenetic activation**. [Primary Figures 5–6](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2022.876259/full). | Actual chromosome-number evidence merits a fidelity comparison. It is not fertilization/live-birth rescue or reversal of established aneuploidy in tracked cells. The lesion is cryoinjury, with no systemic medicine/exposure or myogenic-function bridge established. |
| **Tubacin / Tubastatin A, HDAC6 inhibition** | Tubacin 2 µM and Tubastatin A 10 µM improved spindle morphology and maturation in engineered human-TUBB8-D417N mouse oocytes. Tubacin benefited some additional variants. S11 includes chromosome counts (WT30, D417N34, treated26) and representative division imaging. [Primary and supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11746963/). | Low aneuploidy rates and nonsignificant S11 comparisons establish neither correction of elevated aneuploidy nor equivalence. Figure 6H shows tubacin **reducing maturation in the WT-TUBB8 mRNA control** (roughly 70%→43%, visual estimate), while R262W/N348S lacked significant benefit. A lesion-specific functional window is essential; no class-wide benefit or offspring rescue is established. |
| **AGK2, SIRT2 inhibition** | AGK2 5 µM, tubacin 2 µM, or both improved spindle/alignment morphology after RASSF1A siRNA in mouse oocytes. Exposure lasted three hours from five hours after maturation release. AGK2 did not significantly restore the measured acetyl-tubulin signal. [Primary Figure 5](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2020.601972/full). | Drug-treated chromosome-number correction and productive offspring were not demonstrated. A separate Mps1-inhibitor maturation bypass must not be assigned to AGK2. This refutes a universal favorable SIRT2 direction, not the distinct BUBR1-stability hypothesis. |
| **Indole acetic acid / AhR–Sirt2** | Dietary IAA at 50 µM extended fly lifespan by approximately 15%, with no significant extension detected in the AhR-mutant, Sirt2-mutant or Sirt2-RNAi comparisons. Survival used five cages of about 40–45 flies, not 200 independent treatment replicates. Sirt2 expression/reporter experiments supply a pathway test. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12090787/). | No BUBR1 abundance, chromosome-fidelity or muscle-force endpoint. Fly-food concentration is not human exposure; another AhR ligand cannot inherit the result. The introduction's NMN-longevity wording does not override North's separate NMN-abundance and genetic-SIRT2-survival experiments. |
| **trans-2-PCPA hydrochloride plus TP53 siRNA** | 2-PCPA (Sigma P8511), 60 µM for 72 h, plus TP53 siRNA improved centromere function and reduced lagging/bridged mitoses and micronuclei relative to p53 knockdown alone in doxorubicin-senescent human fibroblasts. p53 knockdown alone increased micronuclei. [Primary Figures 3–5 and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11852275/). | Credit actual fidelity measurements, but this is a hybrid drug/genetic intervention. The combined result cannot transfer to 2-PCPA alone, a clinical tranylcypromine formulation or an arbitrary LSD1 inhibitor. Sustained faithful descendants, muscle function and long-term tumor behavior remain unestablished; p53 dependence is a material liability. |
| **CDK1/2 inhibitor III** | Enzo compound, 10 µM, increased short-term confluence in late-passage tonsil-derived stromal cells; separate CDK1 siRNA shortened doubling time. The recruited donors were four children, not elderly adults. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9337930/). | Dual pharmacology cannot establish selective CDK1 causality. Confluence is not a count of faithful completed divisions. No treated-cell chromosome correction or organ-function rescue was shown. Recruitment n is not confirmed drug-arm replication. [Manufacturer mapping](https://www.enzo.com/product/cdk1-2-inhibitor-iii/) supports ALX-270-442/CAS443798-55-8, not a verified experimental lot. |
| **Liproxstatin-1** | Aged ICR females received 10 mg/kg/day intraperitoneally for 14 days, improving oocyte yield, maturation, spindle/alignment and ferroptosis outcomes. Spindle comparisons counted 62/39/37 oocytes, not mice. [Primary study/correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12162888/), [figure captions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39910323/). | No fertilization, euploid-offspring, live-birth or MVA-muscle benefit was established in inspected material. The authors explicitly state that randomization and blinding were not used. Figure 8j's apparent animal-count inconsistency leaves animal n unresolved; a corrected control-image mix-up must remain disclosed. DL-alpha-tocopherol (200 µM) also improved naturally aged oocytes in vitro; deferoxamine/ferrostatin injury and genetic results remain separate. |
| **Five-compound hepatocyte revitalization (5C)** | Forskolin + salidroside + CID755673 + GSK429286A + ETC-1002 improved lineage-traced hepatocyte expansion and liver function after established mouse injury. Caffeine-clearance testing used n=3/group. ETC-1002 is bempedoic acid. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10746372/). | Real organ repair deserves more weight than expression alone. It is a multi-agent liver program; no individual constituent inherits the complete effect. Human hepatocytes received a reduced **3C** mixture. BUB1B transcript induction is not restored protein/checkpoint function. Chromosome fidelity and long-term tumor surveillance remain unresolved. |

The subsequent [clearance and fidelity comparison](clearance-and-fidelity.md)
qualifies the remaining named-drug, growth and chromosome-phenotype findings.

## What would change the choice

PAI-1 currently supplies the strongest additional drug-mediated muscle-function
challenge among these routes. TM5614 human experience narrows its feasibility
gap but does not join two different molecules into one evidence chain. ConA and
the 2-PCPA/siRNA experiment are stronger fidelity precedents than expression-only
hits. Their next decisive question is transfer to productive myogenic divisions,
with survival and absolute output counted from every starting lineage. HDAC6's
control-cell harm makes dose/lesion specificity especially important.

These comparisons leave the four primary niacin/apitegromab assay claims intact.
Reserves become additional experiments only after their specific biological
premise and implementation are qualified. No nominee is exempt from the same
functional, chromosome-fidelity and renewal requirements.

  original plot images and unmentioned supplements were not independently read.

  includes comprehensive original figure/supplement inspection.
- 2-PCPA and ferroptosis: complete abstracts and targeted indexed primary text;
  the ferroptosis study also had all nine figure captions and correction note read.
  Body/access gaps remain. PAI-1 siRNA: abstract plus selected indexed passages.

Original assessments, rather than source articles or images, are retained here.
Reading a paper is not reproduction of its experiment, and unavailable material
is not a negative result.
