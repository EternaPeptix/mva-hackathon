# Yiqi Huayu Jiedu: exact-mixture lung-injury pretreatment evidence

**The tested eight-herb decoction has a reported protective histological signal in acutely injured rat lungs, accompanied by altered AKT1 and pan-Ras protein signals.** This is actual mixture-treated nonmalignant tissue, not solely a network prediction. However, the paper measures neither respiratory performance nor survival, and its regimen is pretreatment before LPS injury. Preserve an exact-preparation histology/marker comparator; do not promote it to established respiratory-function, BUBR1 or MVA rescue.

Only one batch is identified; interbatch reproducibility is untested. “Fried” processing is not further characterized.

The 369 ingredient rows are database-derived entries, not measured chemistry of this batch. ## Exposure, allocation and injury sequence

Thirty male Sprague–Dawley rats,8weeks old and190–210g, were reportedly randomized to five groups of6 after one week of acclimation: uninjured normal control, LPS model control, and three decoction groups. Randomization method, allocation concealment, assessor blinding, sample-size calculation and individual exclusions are not reported. Six rats per cage is described, but cage-level allocation and its relation to analysis are unspecified.

Decoction gavage was given for7consecutive days at **6.1,12.2 or24.4 g crude-drug equivalent/kg/day**. Controls received distilled water1mL/100g/day. LPS2mg/kg was injected into the tail vein6hours after the day7gavage; normal controls received saline. Lungs were sampled16hours after LPS under anesthesia, followed by euthanasia. Water was withheld for the last8hours before sampling. No additional post-injury treatment arm or late recovery period is described. The authors' human-equivalent-dose calculation is a study rationale, not a validated human regimen.

Group-specific gavage volumes/concentrations are not fully tabulated. If all groups used the printed1.22g/mL stock unchanged, the three doses would require5/10/20mL/kg; this is conditional arithmetic, not a reconstructed actual protocol. The text says solutions were prepared with water but does not resolve this explicitly. The saline control injection is printed as2mg/kg, without a clear saline volume; do not silently convert it. The tissue-storage line also prints80°C without a minus sign. These reporting issues are preserved rather than repaired.

There is no uninjured decoction-treated group, active-treatment reference comparator, cell-culture experiment, PK study or systematic organ/toxicity evaluation. Weights were recorded procedurally but no weight trajectories are reported. Absence of reported toxicity is not evidence of safety, and no harm in MVA tissue is inferred.

## What was actually measured

**Lung histology:** Native Figure6 shows representative H&E sections at400×. The source describes less collapse, exudation, edema/congestion and inflammatory infiltration in all three pretreatment groups versus LPS controls; the medium-dose image/description appears most preserved. This is a qualitative positive. There is no numerical lung-injury score, blinded scoring, field/animal sampling denominator, histological effect size or statistical test of the images. Do not call the morphology quantified or uniformly dose-dependent.

**Protein signals:** Figure7 reports n=5 and mean±SD, although6rats/group were allocated. The identity of those five units, missing sixth sample, pooling and individual-animal values are unexplained. The AKT1 graph marks medium dose with#P<.05 and high dose with*P<.01 versus model; low dose has no significance mark. The unusual source convention is#<.05,*<.01,**<.001. The pan-KRAS/HRAS/NRAS graph marks all three doses**P<.001 versus model, with a nonmonotonic pattern: the high-dose mean is above the low/medium means. Normal-control comparisons are also marked against the model; they are not drug effects.

These are author-reported densitometry results with β-actin lanes, not independently reproduced estimates. The y-axis is “% adj. vol.” without a complete calculation definition. Displayed representative blots do not permit reconstruction of the plotted group means or dose response. Individual normalized densitometry and full uncropped blots are unavailable. The graph is author-reported, not independently reproduced; there is no demonstrated image/summary contradiction. No raw-image quantification was performed.

The antibody recognizes KRAS/HRAS/NRAS together; the assay does not separately establish each isoform's response. Total AKT1/Ras abundance is not phospho-AKT activity, GTP-loaded Ras, direct target binding or causal pathway mediation. No inhibitor/genetic rescue establishes that these protein changes caused the histology. Several treated mean signals lie below normal-control means, so lower signal should not automatically be described as restoration to normal.

**Absent functional outcomes:** No blood gases, oxygenation index, saturation, lung compliance/resistance, respiratory rate/performance, exercise tolerance, ventilator requirement, survival or later recovery was measured. No direct permeability tracer, wet/dry lung ratio, alveolar-fluid clearance, coagulation assay or current-study cytokine panel appears in the retrieved main/supplement. Related claims in the Introduction/Discussion cite earlier work or infer mechanisms from histology/network results. They are not additional outcomes of this experiment.

BUB1B is a **known-disease-target input** in the source's terminology, not an experimentally measured biomarker or even a member of its278-entry putative drug-target list. Supplementary section5 contains BUB1B at table12 row187 including the header; main Table3 lists UniProtO60566, degree41, betweenness.00549867 and closeness.56704981. These are network properties, not expression levels, fold changes, P-values or drug engagement. The paper provides no BUB1B RNA, BUBR1 protein/localization, mitotic checkpoint, segregation, muscle or MVA assay.

The disease list was obtained by an acronym search of the TCMIP disease-target database. The label “known disease target” is the authors' database classification, not independently verified causal relevance of every gene to this lung model. Shared predicted targets cannot establish mixture synergy/antagonism. Enrichment significance likewise is not experimental pathway efficacy; the paper's ErbB count is14in prose versus16in Table4. No new network reconstruction or database-wide target validation was performed here.

## Disposition and reading limits

The **new-yiqi-huayu-jiedu-decoction** assessment uses only the full mixture name and retains a **preclinical lung-injury pretreatment histology/marker comparator**. Do not create ingredient appraisals from the network table. The noncancer tissue-protection signal deserves retention, but it is weaker evidence of useful function than a measured respiratory or organismal outcome. It does not establish superiority to provisional niacin or apitegromab; those nominees also remain unproved in MVA. A decisive follow-up would require a chemically characterized preparation, clarified exposure/animal accounting, blinded quantitative injury and respiratory-function outcomes, and an actual post-injury arm. This is research triage, not treatment advice.
