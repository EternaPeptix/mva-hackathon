# Diclofenac sodium: cancer-cell effects with unresolved potency and exposure claims

**Retain a source-qualified in-vitro anticancer comparator, not an MVA restoration or muscle-compensation nomination.** Diclofenac sodium disturbed microtubules, mitotic progression and autophagy-associated trafficking, and reduced tetrazolium viability signals. Actual combinations with 5-fluorouracil lowered signals versus vehicle in the tested cancer-cell cultures, including some conditions with little single-agent effect; universal superiority over the stronger single agent is not established. Formal synergy, a normal-cell therapeutic window and an in-vivo exposure bridge are not established. [Choi et al., 2022; PMID 35624874 / PMC9138099](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9138099/), [DOI10.3390/antiox11051009](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox11051009).

The exact source and compound were not already qualified in the inspected 200-decision repository: Batch 15 n225 supplied the previously read abstract/follow-up question. No existing diclofenac decision or qualification was found. This primary reading adds no unique abstract-queue credit.

## Tested identity, models and measurements

Methods name **diclofenac sodium, Sigma D6899**. This is a defined laboratory salt, not an authenticated clinical product, unspecified diclofenac formulation or combination tablet. Lot/purity, stock preparation and measured free/intracellular exposure are not supplied. Cellular vehicle controls are 0.1% DMSO; the cell-free tubulin assay uses 0.01% DMSO. The 5-fluorouracil source/formulation is not specified in the materials list.

All four experimental cell lines are cancer-derived: HeLa, HepG2, AsPC-1 and MIA PaCa-2. No primary normal-cell, nonmalignant matched-cell, animal efficacy or drug-PK experiment is reported. HepG2 must not be relabeled as normal hepatocytes. Mitochondrial and Golgi disruption in these cells is not a measurement of clinical hepatotoxicity.

EZ-cytox is a water-soluble tetrazolium metabolic-reduction assay, not an absolute live/dead count. Flow-cytometric and organelle observations add biological context, but no clonogenic, Annexin/caspase death validation, cell-free readout-interference control or raw individual dose-response data were supplied. This limits exact killing estimates without proving an assay artifact. Most quantitative figures state three independent experiments and mean±SD; image-cell counts are subsamples. Figure 2D reports only two independent experiments. Student's t-tests are described without a formal multiple-comparison or combination-interaction model.

## Potency and combination findings must remain precise

The authors report a **170 µM mitotic-arrest half-effect** and a **200 µM half-maximal lethal effect**, generally tied to 18 h. Native Figure1G retains approximately **75% tetrazolium signal at 200 µM**. Half of a partial maximal response is one possible interpretation, but no fitted Emax, curve or definition is supplied. Therefore 200 µM is not a verified 50%-viability concentration, and effect normalization remains unspecified rather than a demonstrated numerical source error. Separately, at 500 µM Figure1G is roughly 55% whereas Figure6B is roughly 85%, despite both being described as 18 h; this remains unresolved. These are visual readings, not digitized raw means. Discussion also changes the HeLa exposure description to 24 h. No potency estimate is repaired.

Native Figure6 specifies the following 18 h combinations, with 5-FU axes expressed in**mM** and diclofenac axes in**µM**:

| Cells | 5-FU concentrations in combination | Diclofenac concentrations in combination |
|---|---:|---:|
| HeLa |0.1/0.25mM =100/250µM |100/500µM |
| AsPC-1 |0.01/0.025mM =10/25µM |1/2.5µM |
| MIA PaCa-2 |0.75/1mM =750/1000µM |1/2.5µM |

The lower-concentration pancreatic-cell findings are real and should not disappear behind the high-dose HeLa concern. Combinations lower viability signals versus vehicle, including some conditions with little single-agent effect. This does not establish that every combination outperforms the stronger single agent; for example, native MIA PaCa-2 at 0.75 mM 5-FU plus 2.5 µM diclofenac does not visibly establish improvement over 2.5 µM diclofenac alone. But the displayed significance brackets compare combinations with control; no dose-matrix analysis, combination index, Bliss/Loewe reference or other formal interaction test is supplied. **Describe lower combination tetrazolium signals versus vehicle, without universal superiority over either single agent or established formal synergy.** This does not prove the interaction is merely additive or that synergy is absent. The prose alternates between greater-than-tenfold and 100-fold pancreatic sensitivity; no fitted comparable potency ratio is established. Gemcitabine is proposed/discussed, not administered in this paper.

## Exposure, BUB1B and useful null results

This study supplies no measured exposure bridge from any human regimen to 170–200 µM HeLa cultures, 500 µM mechanistic assays or 1.7 mM cell-free tubulin conditions. The authors themselves discuss insufficient exposure for the high HeLa concentrations. Their literature-based plasma estimates and an extrapolation from an overdose case are not a measured PK experiment in this study; the cited pharmacokinetic papers were not independently read here, and no overdose scaling is accepted as a dosing rationale. The low-µM pancreatic-cell signals deserve separate exposure-aware research, but nominal medium concentration alone does not establish tumor exposure, normal-tissue safety or clinical efficacy. No human dosing recommendation is made.

**BubR1 appears in the Discussion's checkpoint mechanism, not as an assayed drug-response endpoint.** There is no BUB1B RNA, BUBR1 protein/localization, target engagement or checkpoint-rescue measurement. Figure 7 is a proposed model. Its direct-binding wording is stronger than the polymerization assay alone. Mitotic errors and reduced cancer-cell viability do not establish either MVA benefit or harm to MVA tissue.

N-acetylcysteine at 2 mM is explicit for Figure5A–D mitochondrial-morphology experiments with 500 µM diclofenac for8h. Methods give a general1h NAC pretreatment. Figure5E/F show reduced fed-cell MitoSOX signal but not the starved-cell signal, yet their own NAC dose is unspecified; carrying2mM into E/F would be a cross-panel inference. Mitochondrial fragmentation was not reversed in either nutrient condition. These marker/null results must not become a general NAC rescue or antagonism claim. Figure 5E/F's caption lists rotenone/rapamycin, while its native panels and Results show the NAC/diclofenac experiment; retain that labeling conflict. Rotenone and rapamycin do belong to Figure 5G/H. The sole supplement contains three sets of uncropped LC3, p62, mTOR and actin blots, not additional combination, normal-cell or PK evidence.
