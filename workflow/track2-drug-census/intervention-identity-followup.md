# Resolving the actual intervention behind three promising abstracts

**21 September 2026.** These checks resolve source questions raised by the
[last unfiltered review](next576-findings.md). They preserve positive findings
while distinguishing what was actually administered and measured. They add
three route/compound appraisals and update the existing Eg5 route; they do not
add abstract-reading records or demonstrate MVA efficacy.

| Route | Evidence credited | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| DETA/NO and PROLI/NO | Vascular-cell proliferation suppression and representative arterial lesion morphology, in distinct donor experiments. | Retain a vascular precedent; no demonstrated skeletal-muscle function or BUBR1 restoration. |
| STLC after LIC depletion | More bipolar spindles in depleted frog embryos. | Retain conditional force balancing. Human-cell treatment instead favors monopolar spindles, and controls are disrupted. This updates an existing compound route. |
| GSK-923295 | A separate CENP-E perturbation clarifies which spindle forces matter. | Decline restoration nomination from this source: bipolar prevalence falls in depleted cells and control cells are also affected. |
| Flavopiridol / alvocidib | The resolved primary abstract reports mouse memory benefit. | Retain the functional report with access-limited qualification; neither dismiss it as marker-only nor promote it to qualified MVA benefit. |

## Nitric oxide: two donors, not interchangeable medicines

[Tsihlis et al., PMID21448667](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6959532/)
used **DETA/NO** in rat vascular smooth-muscle cultures and **PROLI/NO** locally
around balloon-injured rat carotids. The full public manuscript, all legends and
seven figure images were reviewed; no supplement was identified. These are not
experiments with inhaled nitric oxide or an arbitrary nitrate medicine.

DETA/NO reduced the proliferation readout and UbcH10 signal. Separate UbcH10
depletion/overexpression experiments support a role in proliferation, without
showing that the donor requires this mechanism. Figure6 provides representative
morphological support for less neointimal thickening with local PROLI/NO, but
there is no per-animal lesion morphometry, patency, flow or vascular-function
analysis. Protein/staining fold changes cannot supply a lesion-benefit effect
size. BUBR1 appears in a discussion hypothesis, without a BUBR1 measurement.

The animal blot uses three rats per group; replicate stains do not increase that
denominator. Independent cell-donor counts are unresolved. Figure3B labels its
outcome as 570-nm absorbance although the methods/legend describe thymidine
incorporation; the assay identity remains unresolved. Donor-parent/exhausted-
donor controls and a separate viability endpoint are not reported. Reduced
proliferation cannot be assumed to mean healthy arrest or productive renewal.

The vascular result deserves its stated credit. It supplies neither muscle
compensation nor an exposure-qualified MVA medicine. This decision is specific
to these donors and outcomes, not a claim that all nitric-oxide pharmacology is
ineffective. No pediatric regimen is inferred from the experimental exposures.

## STLC: the apparent rescue differs between embryos and human cells

[Jones et al., PMID25422374](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4242835/)
used **S-trityl-L-cysteine (STLC)** after LIC1/LIC2 depletion. Relevant primary
Results, Discussion and Methods were read; Figure9 and complete supplemental
TablesS1/S2 were visually checked. Other source images and videos were not
independently inspected. Monastrol is background in this experiment, although
it has its own [previously appraised study](intervention-qualification.md).

In frog embryos, STLC reduced minor multipolar spindles and increased bipolar
spindles; both contrasts versus depleted vehicle controls have P <=0.0001 in
TableS1. Major multipolar prevalence was not significantly reduced. Six
independent experiments included 39–41 embryos per condition, with spindles
nested within them. The depleted embryos' mitotic index did not change
significantly with treatment. Monopolar prevalence also increased (P <=0.01),
and treated embryos remained less bipolar than untreated controls. This was
partial structural compensation, not normalization.

**In HeLaM cells, fewer multipolar spindles did not mean more bipolar spindles.**
STLC predominantly produced monopolar spindles; bipolar prevalence fell versus
depleted vehicle controls (P <=0.0001). Four experiments scored 427–485 cells per
condition. The treatment was 2 micromolar for two hours; embryo injection-solution
concentration is not measured tissue exposure. Control embryos and control
human cells also developed predominantly monopolar spindles. These controls
oppose assuming a normal-tissue window.

The paper establishes conditional structural effects, not drug-treated accurate
daughter-cell production, post-withdrawal renewal or muscle output. Depletion-
only live-cell observations cannot fill the missing drug endpoints. The existing
Eg5 appraisal retains the embryo positive and the distinct earlier HMMR result,
while adding the human-cell direction and control harm.

The same paper tested **GSK-923295**, a CENP-E inhibitor, at 100 nM for 90 minutes.
It reduced bipolar prevalence and increased minor multipolar spindles after LIC
depletion (TableS2 P <=0.01 and P <=0.001), with control-cell disruption too.
Four experiments scored 412–438 cells per condition. This is counterevidence
for restoration at that regimen, not a clinical safety study or a class-wide
MVA efficacy null. The tables' figure-panel references contain an inconsistency;
the explicit model/intervention headers and Figure9 panels determine attribution.

## Flavopiridol: a real functional report with incomplete access

The CDK review resolves to [Leggio et al., PMID26875816](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26875816/).
Its complete primary abstract identifies flavopiridol and reports memory benefit
in nontransgenic mice after injected amyloid-beta oligomers. Treatment followed
the insult; this does not establish reversal of an individually documented
pretreatment deficit. The reported behavioral benefit is distinct from its
neuronal cell-cycle-marker findings.

Full text was unavailable through the checked lawful publisher/institutional
routes. **Behavioral assays, comparator details, animal counts, blinding, movement
confounds, harms and durability remain unverified**, not demonstrated omissions
in the original paper. No primary figures, legends or supplements were inspected.
The result remains a provisional functional signal rather than a negative test.

[NCI confirms alvocidib as the alias](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/alvocidib).
The review's approval claim is not accepted: a cancer-study description and an
[FDA orphan entry](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/opdlisting/oopd/detailedIndex.cfm?cfgridkey=426214)
do not establish an eligible marketed product. The orphan record addresses one
indication, not global approval status. The existing CDK1/2-inhibitor-III stromal
cell result is a different molecule and endpoint.

## Competitive consequence

These sources do not presently displace niacin's restoration hypothesis or
apitegromab's muscle-compensation hypothesis. Neither nominee has MVA efficacy;
the same requirement for useful tissue output, renewal and chromosome fidelity
applies to challengers. The distinct tissue outcomes, control harm, incomplete
primary access and product/exposure gaps explain these decisions. Original
sources and reviewer working files remain outside Git. Broader unread evidence
and identity questions remain open; this is not the submission trigger.
