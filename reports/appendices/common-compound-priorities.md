# Common-compound priority matrix

**27 September 2026 UTC · Current priority synthesis.** Ten decision-relevant candidates, not a ranking of all reviewed compounds. Separately qualified astaxanthin trials now inform its functional priority alongside the NMN and Lovaza updates. No nomination change or original-queue reading credit follows.

**Several challengers have better-controlled evidence for specific human functions than niacin; none has demonstrated a larger clinical benefit or superiority in MVA.** NR, creatine, urolithin A, resveratrol, the tested Lovaza formulation and astaxanthin study preparations are substantive comparisons. L-carnitine and calcium-HMB add narrower controlled signals. NMN and vitamin K2 have more direct compound-associated BUBR1 observations. These are separate reasons to prioritize experiments, not a single efficacy order.

Niacin’s human muscle-NAD engagement remains useful, but five myopathy patients and ten **treated** healthy controls provide no placebo-controlled functional contrast; follow-up durations also differ. Pediatric progression despite NAD correction shows why engagement alone is insufficient. Its BUBR1 bridge relies on **NMN and genetic SIRT2**, not niacin rescue. [Existing evidence table](../../reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md).

Apitegromab retains the strongest documented pediatric **primary motor-function** comparison in this selected set: pooled SAPPHIRE HFMSE difference 1.8 points (95% CI 0.30–3.32). It was measured in SMA on SMN therapy. Higher-dose standalone efficacy was nonsignificant; fractures were 5/53 versus 1/50 at the labeled-dose comparison, and EMBRAZE supplied no notable functional gain despite lean-mass retention. The inherited SAPPHIRE review used the primary abstract and full official label, **not the full trial main/SAP**; incomplete source access therefore cannot uniquely disqualify challengers. [Existing appraisal](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-review.md).

**Context-dependent route outside the ten-candidate matrix:** [sodium bicarbonate](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/sodium-bicarbonate-functional-qualification.md) has a plausible acid-base compensation rationale, but the major CKD trials did not improve primary function. A positive result in older women pooled sodium and potassium salts and cannot establish a sodium-specific benefit. Documented acid-base disturbance and a population-relevant controlled functional effect would be needed to prioritize this route; it supplies no BUBR1-restoration evidence.

**Additional exploratory route:** [sodium pyruvate](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/sodium-pyruvate-functional-qualification.md) has uncontrolled mitochondrial-disease functional observations, including one small sustained pediatric motor gain, and a controlled sports-power signal. Selected long-term cases, mixed motor trajectories, crossover-analysis limitations and incomplete later clinical reporting prevent an MVA or superiority claim. A relevant metabolic deficit and an exposure-qualified useful-function/renewal/fidelity comparison would be needed to raise priority; a NAD-related rationale alone supplies no BUBR1-restoration evidence.

**Additional exploratory route:** [oral R,R monoester](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/ketone-monoester-functional-qualification.md) retains mixed human-performance evidence; [injected ester preparations](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/ketone-sepsis-functional-qualification.md) have selected survivor-only specific-force gains. The [separate D,L sodium salt](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/sodium-dl-3hb-sepsis-qualification.md) adds a reported absolute-force contrast (P=.002) and specific-force positives, alongside persistent wasting and dose-associated electrolyte/mortality findings. Allocation, missing assays, survivor conditioning and absent sodium-matched controls limit inference. Preparations remain distinct. These outcomes earn compensation credit, while advancement still needs exposure-qualified absolute useful output, renewal and fidelity with survival accounted for. The [cholesterol follow-up](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/sodium-dl-3hb-cholesterol-qualification.md) supplies a separate mevalonate tracer, not an independent force replication. Human association and pooled cholesterol–force correlations do not establish a causal mediator or justify dietary cholesterol as an alternative.

**Additional functional reserve:** [KSM-66 root extract](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/ashwagandha-functional-qualification.md) has a small controlled 2015 strength-training signal. The later trial adds reported treadmill-derived endurance benefit, but strength narrative/table/protocol discrepancies and public-data mismatches prevent clean confirmation. All-80 posted-data calculations retain positive absolute-strength and adjusted bench-percentage sensitivities alongside nonsignificant contrasts; they do not reproduce the unidentified 73-person analysis. The linked replay makes those calculations inspectable. Preparation identity, healthy-adult training, exclusions, multiplicity and safety limits remain. This is a compensation comparison under the same justified-exposure, useful-function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the nominees, not demonstrated BUBR1 restoration or MVA superiority.

**Additional functional reserve:** [Cherrypharm cherry–apple blend](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/cherry-blend-functional-qualification.md) reduced baseline-relative strength loss after damaging exercise in a small crossover, with corrected daily comparisons. A later parallel trial found no detectable recovery benefit from a different concentrate. Small samples, sequence/missingness limits, proprietary formulation and different pre-supplementation versus pre-exercise baselines prevent pooling or generic-cherry claims. Neither study reports an absolute-force treatment interval, chronic MVA benefit or BUBR1 restoration. Retain the exact blend as a compensation comparison under the same justified-exposure, useful-function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the provisional nominees; commentary access remains limited.

**Additional functional reserve:** [POMx pomegranate extract](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/pomegranate-extract-functional-qualification.md) has reported placebo-controlled strength-recovery advantages at 48 and 72 hours after eccentric exercise. The original abstract supports those narrow functional findings; sample size, dose, washout, baseline timing, endpoint hierarchy, multiplicity and safety remain unverified. Distinct POM Wonderful juice counterevidence is retained without product equivalence or pooling. The missing full paper limits confidence but is not negative efficacy evidence or a unique exclusion rule for challengers. Retain a preparation-specific compensation comparison under the same justified-exposure, useful-function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the provisional nominees. No chronic MVA benefit or BUBR1 restoration is established.

**Additional functional reserve:** [Pomegranate juice concentrate, Machin 2014 preparation](../../workflow/track2-drug-census/pomegranate-concentrate-functional-qualification.md) has reported controlled average knee- and elbow-strength advantages after exercise in 45 young men. Both active schedules beat placebo in main treatment comparisons; no treatment-by-time interaction or significant dose difference was reported. This supports bounded functional credit, without faster-recovery, dose-equivalence or minimum-dose claims. Per-arm denominators, multiplicity, confidence intervals and systematic safety remain unreported; six exclusions concern myoglobin only. Full original text and captions were reviewed, with native pixels unverified. The tested concentrate is distinct from POMx and undiluted juice, and shared investigators prevent calling it independent-laboratory replication. Apply the same justified-exposure, useful-function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the provisional nominees; no BUBR1 restoration or MVA superiority is established.

**Three high-value unresolved questions:** resolve the three existing creatine DMD full papers and their direct contrasts; compare NMN and niacin fairly in an exposure-qualified model; establish the exact vitamin K2 preparation and whether its tissue signal reflects functional BUBR1 per cell. These remain important decision gaps during broader discovery; unresolved source access does not make new qualified evidence ineligible. The linked appraisals preserve the source detail needed to evaluate these questions.

**The NMN comparison uses the same evidence threshold as the nominees.** The earlier reopening trigger demanded an advantage over niacin without a reciprocal hurdle. The [current rule](track2-route-decisions.md#elected-hypotheses-and-within-pathway-references) separates approved-drug submission eligibility from scientific priority:

> Demonstrate actual-allele functional rescue at justified exposure using the same function, renewal and fidelity criteria as the nominees. Compare NMN with niacin without assuming incumbent superiority; assess the nominated product’s approved-drug submission eligibility separately. A useful mechanistic comparator need not become a submitted medication.

**Status and transfer:** the existing files document approved lipid-use niacin and Lovaza products and US SMA-use apitegromab; none has an MVA indication. NR is described as an OTC supplement, creatine as a trial supplement, and UA/calcium-HMB as specified trial preparations. Other product approvals are not established here. Current availability was **not checked**; family names do not identify an equivalent purchasable preparation. No selected finding establishes transfer to this child. Apply exposure, useful absolute function, renewal and chromosome-fidelity criteria equally to nominees and challengers; only a restoration claim additionally needs functional BUBR1 mediation.
