# Pectolinarigenin: primary qualification

Proposed appraisal: **`new-pectolinarigenin` — experimental gastric-cancer comparator; do not promote as BUBR1 restoration or MVA functional compensation.** This is one compound appraisal spanning two primary papers. Pectolinarigenin reduced gastric-cancer cell MTT signal and, in a later study, inhibited AGS xenograft growth. Those positives are real reported outcomes, but neither paper tests BUB1B-deficient cells, MVA, reliable chromosome segregation, normal tissue function, or survival. The BUB1B finding is a preliminary protein-spot decrease under a growth-inhibiting exposure, with unresolved identification and quantification limitations.

## Identity and model

The intervention is **pectolinarigenin (PEC)**, the aglycone, not pectolinarin, a Cirsium/Citrus extract, or a dietary intervention. PubChem identifies pectolinarigenin as CID5320438, C17H14O6,314.29g/mol; pectolinarin is the distinct glycoside CID168849,C29H34O15,622.6g/mol. The 2018 paper purchased PEC from AdooQ but gives no catalogue number, lot, purity, batch analysis, or final treatment DMSO percentage. The 2024 paper does not identify the PEC supplier/purity in its reagent section; its vehicle is5%DMSO. A current supplier specification cannot retroactively establish either batch's purity. [PubChem pectolinarigenin](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5320438), [pectolinarin](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/168849).

2018 used AGS and MKN28 from the Korean Cell Line Bank;2024 used ATCC AGS. Neither inspected methods section reports stock-specific STR authentication or mycoplasma testing. Cellosaurus classifies MKN28 as a problematic, contaminated line shown to be an **MKN74 derivative**, ICLAC-00328; it remains a gastric carcinoma lineage. This limits the two-line interpretation and does not by itself invalidate the AGS result. [Official MKN28 record](https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_1416).

## 2018: viability and BUB1B-assigned spot

[PMID30380781 / PMC6265996](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265996/), DOI10.3390/nu10111596.

- **Measured positive:** MTT signal was reduced after24h in both cancer lines, with plotted significance at25–150µM versus DMSO control. Reported IC50 values were124.79µM in AGS and96.88µM in MKN28. Figure1 shows an additional125µM dose; Methods lists200µM instead, and Results omits both125/200. Preserve that reporting inconsistency. MTT is a metabolic viability/proliferation proxy; direct apoptosis, autophagic flux, and cell-cycle experiments discussed here belong to an earlier cited paper, not newly measured endpoints in this paper. No non-cancer cell comparator, clonogenic recovery, checkpoint fidelity, segregation assay, or animal treatment is reported here.
- **Exposure carrying the BUB1B result:**100µM PEC24h versus control, whole-cell lysate, equal300µg protein, pH4–7 two-dimensional gels and silver stain, Progenesis Samespots spot-volume comparison, followed by MALDI peptide-mass identification. Authors describe triplicate gels and at least three independent experiments, but do not clearly map each gel to independently treated cultures versus technical replication. Spot-volume normalization beyond equal protein loading/software analysis is not specified sufficiently for independent reconstruction.
- **Exact result:** Table1 row8,spot1147,accessionO60566,assigned BUB1B: **1.9-fold lower**, nominal ANOVA **P=.014**,12.2%sequence coverage/12matched peptides,MOWSE2.57×10^6,theoretical mass119,546Da,pI5.2. The paper's fold magnitude and down arrow are retained; this is not a log2 fold change. No multiplicity-adjusted differential-abundance P value is supplied. Protein-identification scores and the abundance P value are different evidence.
- **Critical limit:** Figure2 places spot1147 near30kDa, far below the table's119.5kDa theoretical mass. Fragmentation, spot assignment, or labelling could explain this; no raw peptide list/spectrum or BUBR1-specific validation is supplied to resolve it. Therefore the defensible statement is a **BUB1B-assigned spot decrease**, not demonstrated loss of full-length/total BUBR1. BUB1B RNA was not measured, and BUB1B is absent from the four immunoblot targets. STRING/GO checkpoint annotations do not measure checkpoint function or show that BUB1B mediates killing.
- **Validation caveats:** LRSAM1,DDX4,PI3K-β,CIP2A were immunoblotted with β-actin normalization. The authors acknowledge that AGS LRSAM1 changes direction between proteomics and immunoblot. Figure7 labels its blots100µM, but three densitometry plots label the treated condition50µM. None is a BUBR1 blot.

No supplementary-material element, native proteomic table/spectra, or deposition link was found in the retrieved2018 article. The Europe PMC “supplementaryFiles” ZIP contains figure images only; its name is not evidence of a scientific supplement.

## 2024: actual drug-treated xenograft outcome

[PMID38361124 / PMC10869406](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10869406/), DOI10.1007/s11033-023-09046-4.

Female BALB/c nude mice,5weeks old at receipt, received subcutaneous AGS/Matrigel xenografts. At tumors of approximately100mm³, authors report random allocation to four groups,n=6each:5%DMSO vehicle;5-fluorouracil10mg/kg positive control;PEC25 or50mg/kg. Treatments were intraperitoneal,three times weekly for3weeks. These are experimental exposures, not a dietary or human regimen. Blinding is not described.

**Preserve the tumor positive.** Table1 reports final mean tumor volumes of659.14±249.27mm³(vehicle),462.09±214.34(PEC25),and321.78±102.81(PEC50),corresponding to reported growth-inhibition rates29.89%and51.18%;5-FU was237.73±119.67mm³/63.93%. These are lower final growth burdens, not demonstrated regression or improved survival. Figure1C marks the high-dose PEC and5-FU comparisons against vehicle; the25mg/kg comparison is not marked significant. Figure1D similarly marks tumor-weight comparisons for high-dose PEC and5-FU, not25mg/kg. Exact P values and animal-level source data are unavailable.

**Bound the tolerability observation.** Body weights rose similarly and authors report no significant hematological or blood-chemistry differences, including AST,ALT,urea,creatinine,TG and cholesterol. Tables2–3 provide summary values, not comparison P values. There is no healthy/non-tumor drug arm, normal-cell viability test, organ histology, developmental study, or long-term follow-up. These short-term observations in tumor-bearing mice do not establish general non-cancer-cell safety or normal-function restoration. Conversely, these data provide no basis to infer clinical or pediatric harm.

**Keep molecular claims narrower than the prose.** Tumor proteomics and in-vitro AGS qPCR concern ANXA11,CAMK2D,CTSD,EIF4E,MAPK1,RHOA. Figure4G uses0,50,100µM and GAPDH normalization; treatment duration is unspecified. It marks significance for ANXA11 at100µM and CAMK2D/CTSD at50/100µM; EIF4E/MAPK1/RHOA reductions are unstarred and close to baseline. Thus “all six significantly validated at RNA level” is unsupported. Neither inspected main text/tables nor a targeted search of all10 supplement pages identifies BUB1B/BUBR1/O60566; this is not proof of biological absence or no effect.

Material reproducibility issues remain:

- Figure1 prose refers to wrong panels; the caption/axes establish A=body weight,C=tumor growth,D=tumor weight. The high-dose mean drawn in1D is roughly145mg, whereas Table1 reports181.30±123.04mg. This visual discrepancy is unresolved; no digitization or statistical reproduction was performed.
- Supplement row580 assigns MAPK1 grouped abundances VC146,PC96.1,T191.4,T266.4; Figure4E appears to reverse the T1/T2 bar heights. Downregulation versus vehicle is shared, but precise dose/group attribution is not reconciled.
- Supplement lists582numbered proteins; Discussion says512once. Methods specifies Proteome Discoverer2.3,Results3.0. The1%peptide FDR is an identification filter, not an adjusted differential-abundance P value. Reported differential screening uses fold≥1.5 and P<.05; the PDF supplies grouped abundance values but no individual samples, abundance P values, or error estimates. Animal n=6 is not proof that six independent tumor-proteome measurements were analyzed. Pooling, biological replicate counts, and full normalization procedure remain unclear. The data-availability statement offers datasets upon reasonable request; no request was made.

## Competitive decision and reading boundary

PEC has stronger direct **antitumor** evidence than a marker-only candidate, including treated-animal outcomes. It has not demonstrated the MVA-relevant benefit sought for the provisional niacin restoration or apitegromab compensation hypotheses; those nominees also remain unproven in MVA. Neither cancer control nor a possible BUB1B-spot decrease establishes restoration of deficient BUBR1. Retain PEC as an experimental cancer comparator, with the BUB1B result as a qualified, unresolved direction-of-effect observation. Do not infer food/extract efficacy, a selective BUBR1 inhibitor, MVA benefit, or MVA harm.
