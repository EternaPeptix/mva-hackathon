# Creatine after acute ischemic stroke — final root appraisal

The ICaRUS Stroke Trial provides controlled human function evidence, but it found no significant controlled benefit for the reported function, grip strength, muscle mass, or muscle degradation outcomes under its seven-day creatine regimen. The estimates are imprecise: they do not establish equivalence or negate results from other creatine populations. The exploratory progranulin finding does not demonstrate BUB1B rescue or clinical mediation. This source-specific appraisal is admitted to the curated comparison after final independent QA.

The results paper is [MED39683542 / PMC11643803](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu16234148). The [published protocol](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-023-07248-6) and [current ReBEC record](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-9q7gg4) support the appraisal; they are not additional efficacy cohorts. The exact creatine hydrate, brand, and purity are unknown. No monohydrate, Creapure, or retail-preparation alias is added.

This readable companion preserves the findings in the frozen original report and [complete scientific adjudication](/Users/jeweled/mva-hackathon/track2-drug-census-work/creatine-stroke-root-review-20261004/root-final-source-adjudication.json). ## Exposure and applicability

The reported contrast was 10 g creatine twice daily, totaling 20 g/day for seven hospital days, against matching maltodextrin at 10 g twice daily. Both groups received milk-protein serum isolate toward a total protein target of 1.5 g/kg/day and mobility adjusted to tolerance after 24 hours. These shared treatments were not independently randomized. The statement that participants consumed more than 85% of sachets does not establish achieved nutrition, exact individual exposure, or muscle uptake. [Main paragraphs 9–11 and 18–20; root findings R02–R04 and R08]

This selected, single-center sample comprised adults aged at least 60 years, within 24 hours of acute ischemic stroke, with prestroke modified Rankin Scale (mRS) scores no greater than 2. Renal and critical-illness exclusions limit applicability. The paper reports 31 included participants, one exclusion after COVID-related transfer, and 30 following the protocol, with 15 per arm. The excluded person's allocation and exclusion timing are unknown. Endpoint-specific follow-up denominators, death scoring, and actual missing-data handling are also unclear. This convenience-sized pilot had reported low post hoc power and was not an equivalence study. [Main paragraphs 7, 17, and 25; protocol analysis sets; root R01, R05–R09, and R28–R29]

## Harms and reporting limits

The active arm included diarrhea in 3, fullness in 1, and creatinine rises exceeding 60% of baseline in 3. Source text reports that fullness improved after administration times were spaced and that creatinine resolved after hydration; diarrhea resolution is unknown. All 13 native event categories remain explicit, including severe events and three deaths that the authors attributed as unrelated. These attributions are source judgments, not proof of causality or its absence. [Main paragraph 39; Figure 5, page 10; root R24–R26 and addenda Q02/Q05]

The placebo severe category is printed as acute myocardial infarction (n = 1); hospitalization is explicitly part of the diarrhea and pneumonia labels only. Event overlap is unknown, so category counts are not summed into unique-patient totals. A creatinine rise is not an adjudicated acute kidney injury diagnosis. This small, selected, short trial does not establish broad renal safety. [Figure 5; root Q02/Q05]

The current registry's target of 60 male participants, mRS below 1, feeding-route exclusion, old recruitment status, and day-90 six-minute-walk primary plan differ from the reported sample of 30 men and women, mRS no greater than 2, and day-7 primary outcome set. Missing registered or planned outcomes remain missing rather than null. The obtained protocol is version 4, dated June 8, 2022 and published in 2023 during recruitment. Earlier versions, full amendments, the statistical analysis plan, and registry history were not obtained. [Current registry; protocol; root R28–R33]

Implementation of the planned full-analysis, per-protocol, safety, and imputation provisions is unknown in the final paper. The protocol TUG paragraph conflicts with its native schedule. Its randomization wording, “10 out of 10,” is retained alongside the planned sample of 30. [Protocol text and figures; independent addendum Q06; root Q06]

Malformed confidence-interval or P-value combinations in the Table 2 mRS interaction, Figure 3 mRS time term, and Figure 2 GDF15 time term remain unrepaired. General TUG-improvement prose conflicts with its native time P = .28. Flow reason counts and some table percentages do not resolve participant denominators. No participant-level data were obtained to repair these discrepancies. [Root R06, R12, R17, R20, and R34; original tables and figures]
