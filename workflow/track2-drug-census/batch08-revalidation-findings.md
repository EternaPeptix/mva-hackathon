## Positive results and boundaries

- **Actual anticancer results remain visible.** GSK461364 suppresses A549 proliferation and induces arrest/apoptosis (41556056). Troglitazone/ciglitazone suppress stomach-cancer growth at 40 µM; GW9662 fails to reverse it, limiting PPAR-gamma attribution (19140230). Cisplatin plus radiation improves clonogenic/xenograft outcomes (41934581), but docking/sensitivity analyses do not establish the abstract's strong claim of specific PLK1 inhibition.
- **Unnamed interventions stay unnamed.** Optimized tumor-antigen vaccines report mouse cures (40574416), KAT2A inhibition improves anti-PD1 sensitivity in animals (42350387), and a GBM conference abstract reports p53-dependent BCLXL-inhibition synergy with radiation/temozolomide (PMC7650381). Exact formulation or compound identities require primary follow-up; they are not inferred from target names.
- **Conditional correction is not general restoration.** Aurora-A/Plk4 perturbation and engineered Hec1 can permit chromosome-congression initiation without CENP-E (41120338). Completed segregation fidelity and the exact inhibitors remain unresolved. SIRT2 mutant-complementation work measures genome-maintenance defects (28461331); it does not administer niacin or a SIRT2 activator. Genetic ULK1 deletion enhances paclitaxel killing while increasing CIN (31291454).
- **Adverse and null findings constrain ranking.** Checkpoint inactivation does not prevent aurA-mutant fly neuroblast tumors (26568519). Adult-cell Mad2l1 deletion permits survival followed by lethal cancers (28318489); Hec1 overexpression also promotes aneuploid tumors (18940925). Increased MSC renewal after TBX3 expression accompanies transformation-like behavior (35145908), not demonstrated safe renewal. Molecular-expression or metabolite associations are not treatment assignments.
