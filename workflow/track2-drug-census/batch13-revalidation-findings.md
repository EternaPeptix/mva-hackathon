# Batch 13: bounded literal abstract revalidation

This batch reads the next 512 original-order records after excluding all 2,042 prior acceptances, including the separate 64-record food admission. Two readers each read 256 complete saved strings in 64 four-record outputs. The 128 outputs contain 742,033 characters; all end markers were observed. All original 3,598 selection objects and prior acceptances are preserved.

After acceptance, the cumulative count is 2,554 accepted revalidations, 4,715 outer records and 1,044 pending records. This is a bounded source-reading increment, with no automatic drug appraisal, primary-paper qualification, independent replication or clinical efficacy credit. The 143 initially empty fields retain separate recovery statuses; this batch does not re-audit or erase those recoveries.

Root independently read and compared 16 predetermined sources across both halves. One cohort wording was corrected: the study of 731,835 individuals did not state that every participant had clonal hematopoiesis. The final audit covers those 16 spot-checks only; it is not a full independent review of all 512 or an error-rate estimate.

The notes retain observed benefits as well as limits. Examples include U87 tumor suppression by 4-methoxydalbergione, salvianolic acid A-associated cancer suppression/radiosensitization, BI 2536 effects on breast-cancer cell populations, and cartilage repair from kartogenin hydrogel combined with transplanted cells. The latter does not establish efficacy of free kartogenin. Conversely, Mps1-inhibitor combinations increased polyploidization without growth-inhibition synergy, and unnamed PLK1 inhibition increased death in FUS-mutant motor neurons. Genetic complementation, nutrient deprivation, expression markers, assay reagents and computational drug nominations remain distinct from administered therapeutic effects.

There are 39 bounded source questions, not 39 newly appraised drugs. They include identity/dose/control checks, functional-outcome qualification and review-to-primary tracing. Niacin and apitegromab remain provisional and unproved in MVA; everolimus remains a conditional probe.

Saved metadata includes 2 retracted records, 4 preprint records, 14 records with 15 erratum links, 1 expression-of-concern link, 40 records with preprint/update lineage links and 3 records with explicit version metadata. PMID 35309327 and PMID 35782055 are labeled retracted; no retraction reasons are inferred from unread notices. PMID 21610853 has both expression-of-concern and erratum links. Listing a linked notice or version does not read its content or create a new independent study.

79 records carry source-issue annotations. The short saved text for PMID 37372939 was read in full as saved, including its ellipsis; this does not supply the missing article argument.

Compact integrity and full source/evidence replay both passed, along with seven narrow rejection tests: duplicate record, changed abstract hash, absent reading credit, overlap with prior acceptances, incomplete root comparison gate, missing second half and incorrect reader attribution. Machine checks establish artifact consistency; actual viewing and interpretation remain separately declared.
