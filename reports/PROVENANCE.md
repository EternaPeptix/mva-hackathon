# Report provenance and scope

**Review revision: 21 September 2026.** This directory brings the revised research reports, methods answers, prediction CSV, proposed pitch revision and unchanged approved narration into one browsable checkout. The update imports the genome-informed sequence/population analysis and adopts everolimus as the conditional Track 2 lead after comparative evidence review. It remains a private research draft; no competition submission or public release has occurred. The public HG002 depth measurement, sequence reconstruction and assumption-based simulations are distinct from patient experiments; no patient drug-response result is available.

## Evidence scope

The current [Track 1 report](jeweled_track1_report_submission.md), [Track 2 report](jeweled_track2_report_submission.md) and [methods form](jeweled_methods_form_answers.md), together with the [causal interpretation appendix](appendices/causal-interpretation.md), state the adopted interpretation. The subjective exact-pair EPCR is **0.8**. Its revision from 0.35 reflects reappraisal of the supplied clinical context and completed evidence, without new segregation or functional data. Unknown phase is retained; N1002K remains a VUS. No treatment response is claimed.

The reports in this checkout and the new [Track 2 evidence pipeline](appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md) govern the adopted proposal. Older internal report copies may be stale; this update does not claim synchronization of every working document. The imported [genome-informed analysis](../workflow/track2-model/README.md) retains its separate scope: reference-sequence reconstruction and uncalibrated population scenarios, without patient-to-drug response estimates. Everolimus selection is qualitative; no model output or literature tally supplies a numerical therapeutic rank.

The methods form retains all 22 original question headings and the existing Track 1 answers. The pitch document preserves the approved six-section narration, 24 caption segments and 293 words, and adds a clearly separated text-only proposal for the new everolimus hypothesis. This proposal is not recorded or approved and does not add or alter the media.

public-count evidence, source/download identities, a replayable statistical
pipeline, complete result tables and figures. GSE134781 contains different mouse
alleles and no case data. This independent secondary analysis of the same counts

membership outputs retain their attributed third-party terms. No unpublished
patient measurement, laboratory response or new media is added.

The prediction CSV and its provenance are byte-identical to the revised internal export. The CSV SHA-256 is `40232aa87da1b498d44e73d82caf485aa390c7d02547bf4f817c557495cf1355`. Its one row is a manually reviewed research prediction, with phase unknown; the recorded builder checks establish format and reference compatibility, not clinical validation or private-truth accuracy.

The twelve dated appendices were selected from the earlier curated review package. Their scientific bodies are preserved, with a notice explaining their historical status; they do not override the new governing evidence pipeline. Links labelled “underlying file not included” lead here deliberately: the named receipt, ledger or output is **not** included or independently reproducible from this directory. These links document a scope limit, not a substitute download or an assertion that the omitted file exists in this checkout.

Excluded material includes raw case reads, read names, alignments, pileups, raw/genome-scale case VCFs, full case ledgers, large case interval arrays, credentials and internal conversation/Git history. The included reports and sequence analysis still disclose selected candidate-level findings and must be handled consistently with the organizers' data-use terms. ## Archival material

The retained appendices describe their original dates and analysis scopes. Statements such as “unchanged EPCR,” “no submission change” or “frozen package” refer to those original analyses; the current reports and [current index](README.md) take precedence. Older Track 2 revision 11, earlier probability judgments, detailed working reports, approval receipts and prior package manifests are not copied here. Historical clinical or mechanistic assertions are not silently reinstated by an appendix link.

[REPRODUCIBILITY.md](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and the workflow sources are an archive of methods and their declared qualifications. Some exact historical launch plans depend on native paths, separately provisioned public resources or authorized case inputs. Those plans are not fresh-clone execution recipes. The [public HG002 benchmark method](../workflow/docs/PUBLIC-BENCHMARK-METHOD.md) separates measured public RTG vcfeval results from representation sensitivities and does not claim an additional hap.py comparison. Source/control evidence and the selected scientific summaries have different scopes.
