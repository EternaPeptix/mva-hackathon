# Track 2 candidate shortlist

**Decision reviewed 29 September 2026.** Submit **niacin (nicotinic acid) as the provisional BUBR1-restoration lead and apitegromab as a separate muscle-compensation comparator**. Put **NMN beside niacin in the first restoration experiment**, and retain **Amla as a high-priority laboratory comparison among reviewed consumer preparations**. These roles select a research portfolio; they are not an efficacy ranking in MVA. No candidate has demonstrated rescue of Leu737Ter/Asn1002Lys.

The current [organizer rules](https://huggingface.co/spaces/SageBio/rare-disease-real-kid-mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/tabs/rules.py) specify existing market-approved medications. Research priority and medication eligibility therefore need separate decisions: the reviewed NMN and Amla preparations have no verified qualifying approved-medication product. Supplement labels do not supply that evidence. The [rubric](https://huggingface.co/spaces/SageBio/rare-disease-real-kid-mva-hackathon-2026/blob/main/tabs/about.py) weights rigor 35%, impact 25%, innovation 25% and scalability 15%; it calls for a mechanism-supported proposal rather than completed clinical validation.

## Evidence that would change the choice

**First establish phase and usable allele function.** Confirmed cis retires the proposed compound-heterozygous explanation. In the trans model, compare N1002K with WT and corrected controls across measured physiological abundances. A partial defect at matched abundance does not by itself exclude a benefit from greater protein supply. If raising N1002K across that range fails to improve checkpoint function and productive output, deprioritize quantity-only rescue; assess the endogenous truncation product separately. A folding-correction hypothesis requires its own functional demonstration. The [allele gates](track2-evidence-pipeline.md#allele-and-model-qualification) and full pipeline govern these decisions.

Niacin and NMN should undergo distinct, exposure-qualified tests rather than precursor substitution. A restoration claim requires improved functional full-length BUBR1 and chromosome behavior, plus useful absolute muscle output with preserved renewal. Apitegromab requires its own drug engagement and functional benefit; it need not restore the initiating checkpoint defect. Amla requires reproducibility across characterized lots and explicit arrest/selection counterscreens. Apply the same output, renewal and fidelity margins to nominees and challengers.

The innovation claim remains the **absolute-output and lineage safeguards** in the [population model](../../workflow/track2-model/README.md): synthetic selection raised the chromosome-normal fraction by 16.8 percentage points while differentiated-cell time fell 11.0%. That is an assay-design counterexample, not a measured drug effect. It explains why protein abundance, favorable survivor percentages or muscle mass cannot decide this competition alone.

**Review scope:** an independent reviewer started without conversation context and assessed the current candidate summaries, linked qualifications and official rules/rubric. A separate source reviewer checked the three Amla papers. This was a candidate-selection check, not a new numerical score of the full report, methods or video. Source-reading limits remain in the linked qualifications; broader discovery is incomplete.
