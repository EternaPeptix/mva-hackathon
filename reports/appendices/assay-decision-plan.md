# Proposed MVA assay decision plan

19 September 2026. Design document supporting both tracks. **No patient material, laboratory
partner, experiment or response is established in this project.** This plan can support a
feasibility discussion; it is not a claim that the laboratory is ready to execute it.

The primary question is whether the leading BUB1B genotype causes a measurable defect that
can be improved at a justified exposure. The two branches distinguish restoration of mitotic
function from protection against downstream stress. See the [current report — archival document not included](../PROVENANCE.md#archival-material),
[arimoclomol evidence](mechanistic-bridge.md) and
[acetylcysteine evidence](nac-exposure.md).

## Gate 1: model and allele mechanism

Clinical/research collaborators would confirm consent, available material, cell identity,
genotype and phase. Patient-derived cells and engineered substitutes must be identified separately.
An engineered genotype is not evidence that the child's variants occupy that configuration.

If endogenous engineering is feasible while phase remains unknown, the informative comparison
set is: wild type, each heterozygous allele alone, the two alleles in trans, and both alleles
in cis with a wild-type partner. Independently derived clones and editing controls are needed
to distinguish genotype effects from clonal background. Corrected patient cells, if available,
offer a separate genetic-rescue comparison. A cDNA model is useful for protein behaviour but
cannot answer endogenous transcript-decay or splicing questions.

| Question | Evidence to collect | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Does the nonsense allele alter RNA processing or abundance? | Allele-specific transcript abundance and structure in an endogenous/intron-containing setting | Retain measured RNA effects separately from predicted NMD |
| Does N1002K change protein quantity, localisation or function? | Full-length/truncated protein assessment, kinetochore localisation and mitotic behaviour | Normal abundance does not exclude functional impairment |
| Can matched protein quantity restore function? | Near-physiological expression comparison with functional controls | Failure rejects an abundance-only strategy in that assay; excessive expression cannot establish physiological rescue |
| Does genetic correction rescue the chosen phenotype? | Blinded baseline and corrected-control comparison | Without an interpretable defect and rescue, do not use the assay to claim drug correction |

The complete genomic analysis remains a prerequisite for final case attribution. If the causal
explanation changes, revisit the model instead of forcing the drug hypothesis to fit it.

## Gate 2: exposure and measurement validity

Before drug screening, a pharmacologist and the laboratory would agree on a plausible-exposure
range and how it will be measured. Record the supporting formulation, population, analyte,
duration, binding and tissue assumptions. Keep deliberately high mechanistic-control conditions
separate. If a justified range or measurement method is unavailable, a result remains exploratory.

| Branch | Engagement measurements | Required interpretation controls |
|---|---|---|
| Arimoclomol | HSP70/HSP90-pathway response separately from BUBR1 abundance and localisation | Genetic rescue; wild-type exposure; spontaneous segregation as well as any checkpoint challenge; viability-controlled orthogonal pathway perturbation where feasible |
| Acetylcysteine | Defined NAC species, cysteine-related metabolism and glutathione redox state | Cell-free probe/drug controls; orthogonal stress readout; pH/oxygen controls; washout; functional benefit beyond a fluorescent signal |

Vehicle controls and each drug alone come first. Combination claims require reproducible
individual effects and a separate design that can distinguish additivity from interaction.
An acute adult drug exposure is not a selected chronic exposure for the child.

## Gate 3: functional outcome and false improvement

For the upstream branch, the primary endpoint is segregation-error frequency among observed
divisions in the original design. **The 20 September refinement replaces that single primary
readout with linked first-anaphase error frequency and yield without a scored visible error
per starting founder.** The [pilot protocol](pilot-protocol.md) defines the founder
census, competing outcomes, censoring and separate descendant follow-up. Keep counts of
divisions, viable cells and starting cells, plus mitotic timing, cell-cycle distribution, death,
micronuclei and absolute clone abundance. Image and analysis assignments should be masked,
with an independent code linking conditions to wells.

For the downstream branch, the pilot must select one functional endpoint matched to the
demonstrated defect, such as a validated mitochondrial-function measure. ROS intensity alone
cannot be the primary functional outcome. Better survival despite persistent chromosome errors
is downstream protection; it does not repair the genotype. Track abnormal clone expansion
during treatment and washout.

| Observed pattern | Interpretation and next decision |
|---|---|
| Genetic control does not rescue, or baseline defect is not reproducible | Assay/model unresolved; no drug-rescue conclusion |
| Exposure or assay recovery is inadequate | Inconclusive delivery/measurement result; do not call the mechanism ineffective |
| Engagement changes but function does not | Mechanistic engagement only; stop advancement on that evidence |
| Apparent improvement follows reduced division, selective death or abnormal-clone expansion | Does not meet the repair criterion; investigate the confound or adverse selection |
| Benefit appears only in high mechanistic-control conditions | Laboratory signal without demonstrated translational exposure |
| Reproducible function improves in the justified range with interpretable engagement and acceptable parallel outcomes | Advance to independent confirmation and relevant-cell-type review; still no clinical-benefit claim |

## Analysis commitments before confirmation

Use a separate pilot to estimate variation across clone, culture preparation and experimental
day. Randomise treatment within suitable blocks and retain failed experiments with reasons.
Cells, frames and wells are subsamples; independent cultures/clones do not create additional
patients. State the actual inferential unit and account for repeated observations of the same
lineage. Avoid pooling all photographed cells into an artificial patient sample size.

Before examining confirmatory treatment effects, freeze the primary endpoint, minimum meaningful
effect, exposure range, sample size, exclusions, missing-data rules, adverse-selection thresholds
and multiplicity policy. Those numerical choices are not yet determined. A statistician should
choose the model once the pilot reveals the hierarchy and outcome distribution. Report effect
sizes, uncertainty and all prespecified conditions, including null and discordant results.

Minimum records include model origin, genotype/phase verification, clone and culture IDs,
experimental day, masked treatment code, exposure/sampling records, division and cell counts,
lineage links, endpoint values, exclusion reasons and analysis version. Preserve raw images
and assay outputs for review. No synthetic data may be presented as a patient experiment.

A successful research assay would require a separate assessment before contributing ACMG PS3
evidence. Further translation would depend on the treating team and appropriate research review;
the proposed assays do not establish developmental recovery or cancer prevention.

The [20 September pilot/feasibility packet](pilot-protocol.md) adds a proposed
blocked allocation, exposure-table requirements, handoff fields and explicit imaging/storage
arithmetic. Its replicate counts are planning choices, not demonstrated power. Model access,
actual instrument throughput, laboratory pricing and the exposure range remain unconfirmed.
