# Bisphenol A: consumer-product exposure comparator, not a BUBR1 treatment lead

**Source-bounded appraisal, 28 September 2026.** EPA CPDat v4.0 includes BPA (DTXSID7020182) in **42 formulation product IDs**, assigned to “Food and drug” and “Home maintenance” categories. These are composition records from public documents, not confirmation of present-day product sale, prevalence, or actual exposure. The EPA now lists CPDat 4.1 as current; this product count is from the downloaded v4.0 release only. [EPA CPDat](https://www.epa.gov/chemical-research/chemical-and-products-database-cpdat), [CPDat v4.0 record](https://doi.org/10.23645/epacomptox.5352997).

The [2017 human cumulus-cell study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28951153/) cultured cells collected from 26 women undergoing ICSI and tested BPA at 0, 0.002, 0.02, and 20 μg/mL. Its abstract reports lower BUB1B, CDC20, and HAS2 expression and more apoptosis-associated outcomes at **20 μg/mL**; the authors characterize the effective concentration as very high and supra-physiological. The publisher's abstract does not provide full outcome tables, effect sizes, exposure duration, or the analysis denominator for each gene. Using BPA's molecular weight, 20 μg/mL is approximately 87.6 μM. This is an in-vitro hazard observation, not evidence that typical exposure reduces BUB1B in people.

A [2025 primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12571864/) reports reduced BUB1B transcript and protein in immortalized human bronchial BEAS-2B cells at 80 nM BPA. The same study reports dose-related loss of cell viability and interprets the overall results as cytotoxic; it did not measure chromosome segregation, checkpoint function, or an MVA model. Its separate TCGA analysis found higher BUB1B in lung tumors than normal tissue, while BPA-treated BEAS-2B showed lower BUB1B; these distinct comparisons do not establish a BPA-caused tumor mechanism. The docking results are predictions, not proof of direct BPA-BUBR1 binding, and the paper itself describes its broader exposure-to-lung-cancer interpretation as primarily correlative.

Together these reports justify retaining BPA as an **adverse-exposure comparator with a BUB1B expression signal**, not as a drug, rescue route, or competitive Track 2 candidate. Lower BUB1B in these non-MVA cell contexts is directionally contrary to restoring usable BUBR1. Neither study tests the patient's variants, muscle output, faithful division in MVA cells, or the effect of ordinary consumer-product use. Do not transfer these findings to BPB, BPAP, BPAF, BPS, or other bisphenols.

**Reading scope:** full PMC text was read for the BEAS-2B study; the human cumulus-cell study is qualified from its primary abstract and publisher-provided abstract/results excerpts, not its full article.
