## Antitumor effects require their own endpoint interpretation

BAL0891 (MED:39184047) produced prolonged TTK inhibition and transient PLK1 inhibition, accelerating aberrant mitotic exit. Intermittent IV monotherapy caused MDA-MB-231 regressions, and about 40% of 13 TNBC PDX models showed profound activity. Paclitaxel combinations produced pathologically confirmed cures; carboplatin synergy depended on schedule. These substantial tumor outcomes merit dose, denominator and selectivity follow-up, while checkpoint disruption remains distinct from chromosome-fidelity restoration.

The 10D1 anti-CD99 conference abstract (PMC:PMC9164746) reports complete tumor clearance and longer survival in patient-derived orthotopic diffuse-midline-glioma mouse models. Locoregional delivery reportedly achieved similar effects at lower concentrations; radiation increased cytotoxicity. Primary publication, dosing, clearance and survival denominators remain necessary. The resistant-cell-antigen/TLR7-agonist-prodrug nanovaccine (MED:42621703) suppressed resistant 4T1 tumors and reversed innate checkpoint-inhibitor resistance; the prodrug and treatment partners need identification. Paclitaxel describes the resistant background and cannot be assumed to identify the actual combination chemotherapy.

Abemaciclib reduced HCC proliferation and in vivo tumor growth (MED:33686022); JJ-450 suppressed AR/ARv7 transcription and CRPC xenograft growth (MED:31554654). Mocetinostat activated RB-DREAM and produced wildtype-p53-dependent NSCLC toxicity (MED:37759078). Unnamed CREBBP/EP300 bromodomain inhibitors reduced H3K27ac/lncRNA expression and breast-cancer growth (MED:33575113). Andrographolide inhibited DU145 proliferation through mitotic arrest/apoptosis; taxifolin alone had no significant proliferation effect but enhanced the combination (MED:23382917). These drug effects retain their actual antitumor or toxicity endpoints.

YM155 (MED:29976630) used 83 ng/mL for eight hours as a sublethal condition and 14.6 ng/mL for 20 hours as LC50. The 2.67-fold survivin-mRNA decline and widespread DNA-repair-program downregulation do not establish repair restoration; the source questions whether survivin decline is secondary to death. SIK2 depletion sensitized ovarian cancer to paclitaxel while impairing centrosome separation (MED:20708153). PKA was an endogenous regulator, not a named SIK2 drug. Combined CENP-E/Mad2 reduction reduced tumors while increasing missegregation and cell death (MED:24133140).

The 100-patient oncology series (MED:39457104) reports 27% targetable mutations, 8% matched treatment, and PFS over six months in 38% of treated patients—3% of the whole cohort. The therapies are unnamed. NEK2-associated treatment response (MED:38758909) needs clarification as observed versus predicted sensitivity. The two fisetin reviews (MED:37259344; MED:23121441) do not supply independent primary efficacy demonstrations.

## Direction, assay and prediction cautions

- TORC1 inactivation caused mitotic slippage in yeast and human cells, with chromosome instability reported for yeast (MED:35141499). Agents or inactivation modality are unnamed. The consolidated outcome field now preserves that species scope.
- Both Wnt inhibition and stimulation reduced U87 proliferation/survival in the ICG-001/AZD2858 study (MED:28837560). Exact drug-arm assignment and the title/body tension about apoptosis need primary review.
- Latrunculin A destabilized interpolar microtubules and delayed yeast anaphase (MED:18562692). Bub1 kinase-dead mice had segregation errors/aneuploidization without increased spontaneous or carcinogen-induced tumor susceptibility (MED:23209306). Spindle morphology, segregation and tumor endpoints cannot substitute for one another.
- FAM64A-siRNA numerical viability, proliferation, migration and invasion results are retained without invented units or percentages (MED:40537674). CCK-8 and EdU are assays. Puromycin selected clones in the inorganic-arsenic/miR-186 experiment; 100 nM arsenic for four weeks contributed to measured tetraploid/aneuploid fractions (MED:36989553).
- Dasatinib/lapatinib/pazopanib sensitivity was predicted by pRRophetic (MED:38187003); lenvatinib/regorafenib/sorafenib were docking candidates (MED:39095799); collagenase/paclitaxel/docetaxel were enrichment associations (MED:30906836). Tripterygium/Lysimachia effects were network-model outputs (MED:36339610). Agent-array occupancy does not mean experimental administration.
