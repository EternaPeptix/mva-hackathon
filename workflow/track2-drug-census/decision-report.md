# What the broad drug search changes

**Adopted research comparison, 21 September 2026.** The broad search changed the proposal: **niacin for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. The report, methods, experimental plan and revised narration now use this pair. Everolimus remains a conditional mTORC1 mechanism probe. Neither primary candidate has demonstrated MVA rescue; this is a research-priority judgment, not an estimate of treatment efficacy.

The census screens 15,357 entities plus 40 FDA 2026 product entries assessed separately, with 99 curated route/compound appraisals. Retrieval and co-mentions do not establish every drug's efficacy; missing evidence remains UNKNOWN. The revised local movie is complete. At the existing Vimeo URL, **V3 replacement is verified**. Track 2 remains unsubmitted.

## The same comparison for leaders and challengers

The separate [Broad Hub crosscheck](broad-hub-review.md) screens another catalog's
7,540 overlapping source names, without inflating the main denominator. Its
2,148 lexical mentions are discovery pointers; an unmapped name is not a new
medicine, and a co-mention is not a beneficial intervention.

We compare mechanism relevance, actual-drug functional evidence, transfer to this
genotype and tissue, exposure/eligibility, and harms. No numerical efficacy or
weighted winner score is inferred from missing experiments. A challenger does not
have to demonstrate actual-allele rescue before being considered when neither
current candidate has done so. Every candidate faces the same eventual requirement:
greater absolute muscle function with preserved renewal and chromosome fidelity.

No niacin-mediated BUBR1 or MVA rescue; healthy treated controls also gained strength in the unblinded muscle study. Pediatric prescription safety/effectiveness is unestablished. | **Retain restoration lead**, conditional on usable BUBR1 rescue. ## What remains unknown

The [primary-source qualification](lead-qualification.md) now **raises Aurora-B
balancing as an experimental functional comparator**: attachment rescue after
BUBR1 depletion is real drug evidence, but is not completed faithful division.
Geraniin retains its cellular signal with an unresolved parent-exposure bridge;
TG101209 remains a thermal probe with competing pharmacology. KP372-1 and
okadaic acid receive explicit contextual/reagent decisions rather than being
mistaken for unnamed BUBR1-restoring drugs. The comparison applies the same
actual-allele evidence gap to nominees and challengers.

The deeper [complete-saved-abstract review](abstract-review-findings.md) adds
**geraniin** as a chromosome-fidelity comparator to qualify, **TG101209** as a
protein-stability probe, **Bletilla extract** as a mixture-level reserve,
**PLK1 reduction** as a same-disease target opportunity with a limited drug precedent, and contextual
GW5074/B7E2 pharmacology. The comparison table gives the positive finding and
missing bridge for each. These are further lines from the broad search, so its
“no further leads” condition has not been met. Niacin/apitegromab remain the
nominated research pair, not proven superior treatments.

The [all-target assay review](target-assay-review.md) also identifies four
residual-activity values mislabeled as inhibition, including two swapped
compound/concentration assignments. Original records are retained beside the
source-based interpretation; target-database membership cannot decide efficacy.

The [census](README.md) enumerates public clinical-development and regulator-linked
records, plus additional literature-derived compounds. It cannot certify “every
drug in existence”: unpublished compounds, some national products, changing
approvals and unindexed experiments have no complete public denominator. Salts,
formulations, combinations and unresolved identities further prevent treating
the row count as a count of unique medicines.

Every catalog entry receives a screening/evidence state, not a fabricated
efficacy estimate. Only the explicitly appraised candidates have a manually
reasoned competitiveness decision. Co-mention, biochemical inhibition, genetic
rescue, animal function, human target engagement and clinical efficacy are
different evidence types. Thousands of unknown rows do not become excluded drugs.

## Experimental next step and delivery status

The new comparison is incorporated into the written proposal and revised narration. For apitegromab, verify target availability and actual-drug engagement, then test absolute contractile output, renewal, chromosome fidelity and matched tumor/nonmalignant responses. Elevated baseline myostatin is not a necessary condition for compensation. Niacin retains its own NAD/SIRT2/BUBR1 mediation tests. Test single agents first; no combination benefit or pediatric MVA regimen is inferred. The [pipeline](../../reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md) defines drug-appropriate controls and the four-claim confirmation family, with everolimus outside it unless prospectively promoted.

## Translation qualification

The [primary comparison](translation-qualification.md) identifies the actual
interventions behind additional rescue claims. TM5614 has adult clinical exposure
and ongoing phase III development, with Japanese unapproved status; it is not
the TM5484 muscle compound. ConA has measured chromosome-number benefit, while
tubacin also harms a control maturation outcome. Centromere rescue requires
TP53 siRNA plus 2-PCPA; the liver-function result belongs to a five-agent regimen.
These distinctions preserve real positive findings without promoting class or
mixture results to a single approved MVA medicine.

The [clearance and fidelity comparison](clearance-and-fidelity.md) upgrades
gedatolisib from a pathway-adjacent medicine to a conditional cell-size experiment
and okadaic acid from localization evidence to partial live-division rescue.
PZ15227 and DT2216 remain different compounds: equal survivor reseeding limits
the former's growth claim, while the latter has human platelet toxicity and a
suspended pediatric combination trial. SB-431542, EPZ-5676 and a nucleoside
mixture receive explicit positive findings and reasons for remaining reserves.
The [intervention check](intervention-qualification.md) qualifies all fourteen
follow-ups flagged by the final ranked selection. BI2536's reduction of
post-cytokinesis defects strengthens a conditional route, while control-cell
arrest and distinct volasertib tumor findings narrow it. BMP4 differentiation,
combination-RNA heart function and clone-specific anti-PD-1 survival are real
positive results with different intervention and tissue boundaries. Mirdametinib
is an implemented existing-active-ingredient probe; SHP099 supplies the separate
mouse metabolic benefit. Kv10.1 knockdown, MAD2 restoration and CDC20 reduction
cannot be relabeled drug rescue. The alternatives remain substantive; no
absence-of-further-leads claim or new submission follows.

## Muscle benefit recovered by the next unfiltered pass

The [additional 576 assessments](next576-findings.md) bring outer coverage to 1,393 source/ID records, leaving 4,366 originally saved abstracts unread. The [primary comparison](next576-primary-qualification.md) admits **ursolic acid as a substantive functional reserve**: its mouse grip and absolute-force benefit and small randomized human handgrip signal deserve credit alongside the separate human null. It challenges niacin on empirical muscle benefit; niacin is retained for the distinct restoration hypothesis. Apitegromab retains stronger pediatric disease-associated functional evidence. None has demonstrated MVA efficacy, and ursolic-acid product/exposure eligibility remains unresolved.

**MG132** has separate alignment and ex vivo muscle-proteolysis benefits, without demonstrated completed faithful division or contractile improvement in the inspected studies. **DHMMF** has RACK1-associated tumor-cell activity, with phospho-BUBR1 rising during damage. These endpoints do not justify muscle-restoration election. The same primary comparison upgrades the existing **bortezomib** entry: bortezomib plus humanin prevented neonatal-denervation contractures, but mortality and reduced normally innervated muscle growth keep it a combination-specific reserve. No active-force or monotherapy benefit is established. The ledger adds three appraisals and revises this existing entry, leaving 96; these are not 96 approved medicines or closed efficacy questions. Further source flags and unread literature remain; publication and submission remain pending.
