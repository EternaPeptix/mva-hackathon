# Protein stability, tissue support and neuromuscular response

**22 September 2026. Six primary papers qualify seven new compound/tool entries and update metformin, taking the ledger from 109 to 116 appraisals.** These are distinct exposures, not seven independent replications. bFGF and collagenase share one paper; salbutamol and ephedrine share one family's sequential treatment report. No current research nomination changes. None establishes MVA efficacy, and patient variant phase and Asn1002Lys function/abundance remain unresolved.

The positive observations below are retained on their own merits. Niacin-induced BUBR1 restoration remains unproven; apitegromab's functional evidence is from another disease. Neither nominee is an eligibility gate, and no head-to-head comparison follows from these papers. Laboratory regimens describe the evidence, not patient dosing.

## Protein-stability results

**CX-4945 / silmitasertib — add `new-cx4945`.** [Kildey et al., PMID34050247](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8163776/) report increased CDCA3 protein in most of eight NSCLC lines, except CRL-5889, alongside reduced CK2-motif phosphorylation and Cdh1 binding. The protein comparison has eight paired cell-line values averaged from three experiments (P=0.0126). Methods and the supplementary legend specify 5 µM for 12 h; Results says 24 h. The direct turnover chase belongs to cisplatin, not CX-4945. Actual CX-4945 at each line's IC25 plus cisplatin lowers reported median cisplatin IC50 from about 19 to 10 µM after 48 h (ATP-based viability, P=0.0138). Source data confirms four analysed values per arm without clearly assigning their sampling identities. Genetic CDCA3 overexpression's approximately twofold/sixfold cisplatin/carboplatin sensitization is separate. Preserve the protein and antitumor pharmacology positives; no normal-cell margin, BUBR1 restoration, muscle outcome, faithful division or absence of drug-induced cell-cycle redistribution is demonstrated.

**Metformin — update its component of `prior-27`.** [Wu et al., PMID30022161](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6430198/) show slower TET2 decay and increased 5hmC with 5 mM metformin in high-glucose A2058 melanoma cells genetically restored with full-length TET2-WT. Metformin also reduces growth and, at 2–4 mM, soft-agar colonies. Drinking-water metformin at 500 mg/L, started seven days before implantation, further suppresses WT xenograft growth in diabetic and nondiabetic mice; mock/S99A/knockdown controls constrain the effect. This is genuine drug stabilization with downstream tumor outcomes. Limits include engineered target restoration, high cellular exposure (authors' comparison: up to 5 mM versus approximately 20 µM human circulation), preventive timing and unexplained final n=4–5 versus Methods n=6. Actual consumed dose and plasma exposure are unknown. The separate A769662 result must not be credited to metformin. Preserve the prior MASTERS finding of blunted resistance-training hypertrophy in older adults and separate AICAR evidence; this paper neither overturns those results nor demonstrates BUBR1 or muscle rescue.

## Organ-repair signals with functional benefit unresolved

**Wy-14643 / pirinixic acid — add `new-wy14643`.** [Hu et al., PMID42019507](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13325914/) administered 50 mg/kg intraperitoneally every 12 h from 24 h before to 24 h after 70% partial hepatectomy in male C57BL/6 mice, n=3 per condition. Cell-cycle-associated staining and ACSL1/FOXM1/HIF1α protein increased. Preserve that regenerative molecular response. The liver/body-weight recovery curve is a separate model-characterization experiment, n=4 per time point; no drug-assigned liver-mass, organ-function or survival improvement is shown. Age, vehicle formulation and exposure are unstated. Regenerating-cell Bub1b transcripts and computational FOXM1 links do not establish Wy-induced BUBR1 protein or faithful division. Do not transfer the result to another PPAR agonist or infer patient benefit/harm from proliferation alone.

**Eplerenone — add `new-eplerenone`.** [Gravez et al., PMID25564371](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4330055/) studied male rats operated at three weeks of age and assessed three months after aortic constriction. Dietary eplerenone returned excess cardiac Ki67 toward sham levels; 61% is the constriction-induced increase, not the drug's percentage benefit. Groups were sham 5, sham+drug 4, constriction 5 and constriction+drug 6. Most cycling nuclei had endothelial localization. Dose and exact treatment initiation are unspecified. No contractility, perfusion, exercise or survival benefit is reported; the physiological consequence remains unresolved. Aldosterone-induced Bub1b transcript elevation in mice and spironolactone-treated endothelial cultures are separate interventions. The sham+drug control is useful but does not establish developmental, renal or electrolyte safety.

## Functional stromal support and clinical locomotor response

**bFGF — add `new-bfgf-stromal-support`; unspecified collagenase — add `new-collagenase-matrix`.** [Mailloux et al., PMID24014875](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4346227/) added bFGF at 20 ng/mL for 72 h to LGL-leukemia-derived stromal cultures before progenitor co-culture. Collagen deposition decreased and healthy progenitor proliferative support was partially restored. A separate supplementation growth curve improved stromal growth; its exact concentration is unspecified. Growth curves represent three experiments/donor pairs; collagen/support analyses specify n=4 iterations with independence incompletely described. The 24-person clinical cohort is not a drug-treatment denominator: zero patients received these interventions in this study. Carryover/direct-progenitor controls and washout are insufficiently specified; no engraftment, corrected blood counts, muscle function or BUBR1 protein restoration follows.

Separate collagenase matrix removal restored progenitor proliferation/division indices (n=4 iterations). Retain unresolved concentration (Methods 20 versus Results/legends 60 U/mL), CD45-gating, culture-timing and donor-accounting discrepancies. The unspecified Worthington preparation is not identified with a licensed collagenase product; its aliases are deliberately left unmatched. bFGF's catalog name match likewise does not establish reagent/formulation equivalence. These two exposures support one paper, not independent replication.

**Salbutamol — add `new-salbutamol-agrn-cms`; ephedrine — add `new-ephedrine-agrn-cms`.** [Zhang et al., PMID28937031](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5634075/) report three adult siblings, aged 27, 29 and 31, with homozygous AGRN p.A1768P. Ephedrine reportedly improved symptoms after three days; dose, route and individual response denominators are unstated. Availability prompted a switch to salbutamol, 2 mg three times daily for more than one year in all three; route is not explicit. Sustained movement status was reported, and one sister's walking distance changed from less than 50 m to more than 500 m after treatment. Credit the clinical locomotor signal, while retaining unresolved drug-specific attribution/timing, lack of a standardized walk protocol or control, and absence of numerical post-treatment strength or structured safety assessment. Do not derive an exact percentage gain or count the two drugs as independent family replications. Saved aliases match salbutamol to both albuterol and levosalbutamol; the join remains ambiguous, with no levalbuterol-specific efficacy claim.
