# UMK57: a real fidelity result with limited durability

**22 September 2026.** Retain UMK57 as a mechanistic research lead. It improves
chromosome-segregation outcomes in the studied cancer cells, including a
post-division assay. The evidence does not yet support an MVA medication
nomination. Neither niacin nor apitegromab has demonstrated MVA benefit either;
a different mechanism is not a reason to exclude a competitor.

Primary source: [Orr et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27829147/),
[complete article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5130158/).
An independent reviewer read the complete main text, methods and four main
figure legends and inspected all four main figures. The integrating reviewer
also inspected Figures 1 and 2. Supplementary panels were not independently
inspected; results supported only by those panels are identified below.

| Question | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|
| Actual intervention | UMK57 potentiates MCAK-dependent microtubule depolymerization. The main working concentration was 100 nM against 0.1% DMSO. Inactive analog UMK95 and MCAK-depletion controls support the target relationship. Protein overexpression is a separate intervention. |
| Acute fidelity | Figure 1A prints 36% lagging anaphases with DMSO and 17% at 100 nM in the U2OS titration. Figure 2B independently presents significant acute reductions in U2OS, HeLa and SW-620: more than 300 anaphases, three independent experiments. These are functional segregation outcomes, although not an all-chromosome daughter-cell count. |
| Completed division | Figure 2D reports significantly less mis-segregation of one tagged chromosome in HCT116, among more than 600 daughter-nucleus pairs. Cells underwent 15 hours of monastrol arrest, washout and eight hours of recovery. This measures a restricted completed-division endpoint, not total faithful daughters per starting cell, survival or division yield. No numerical bar height is treated as exact data. |
| BUBR1 measurement | Figure 2H measures kinetochore BubR1 normalized to Hec1, with at least 150 kinetochores from 10–20 cells, without a significant acute change. It does not measure restoration of total BUBR1 in deficient cells. The earlier draft's claim that BUBR1 was never measured is withdrawn. |
| Attachment mechanism | Photoactivation measurements use 5 micromolar MG132. Metaphase attachment half-life falls; this arrest-based mechanistic assay remains separate from the post-division result. |
| Durability | In Figure 3D–F the acute benefit is no longer significant at 72 hours in the three cancer lines. Two U2OS single-cell colonies per condition, grown for 6–8 weeks, show no reduced modal-copy-number heterogeneity for chromosomes 2 and 3. Neither finding proves an exactly zero effect or resolves every schedule. |
| Combination | Figure 4E shows 250 nM ZM447439 with UMK57 retaining significant benefit at 72 hours in U2OS, but not at 96 or 120 hours. The running text reports a delay in SW-620 and none in HeLa; those supplementary panels were not inspected. This is combination- and model-specific, not a single-agent Aurora-inhibitor benefit in MVA. |
| Adverse/context effects | High concentrations produce prometaphase accumulation; MCAK overexpression increases spindle/alignment defects with UMK57. These observations require exposure and genotype controls. They do not establish a class-wide contraindication. |

No BUB1B-hypomorphic model, actual-allele experiment, muscle outcome, animal
therapeutic benefit or human exposure was established in this paper. The checked
catalog has no exact UMK57 alias match; catalog absence alone cannot establish
worldwide regulatory or clinical status. The observed short-term benefit earns
a research lead, while durability, transfer and medication eligibility remain
open. Current evidence is insufficient to replace the niacin/apitegromab
research comparison or to prove those nominees superior.

The next discriminating experiment would measure segregation and absolute
productive output in affected and corrected cells, including repeated exposure
and washout. Attachment stabilization/destabilization must be chosen from the
observed defect, not inferred from a target name. In this case phase, endogenous
BUBR1 abundance and Asn1002Lys function remain unresolved.
