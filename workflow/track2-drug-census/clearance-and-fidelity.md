# Which rescue results survive compound and endpoint checks?

**Research decision, 21 September 2026:** keep niacin and apitegromab as the
restoration/compensation comparison. The additional evidence below supports
specific experimental alternatives. It does not establish that either nominee
is clinically superior, or that the broad search found no further leads.

## Clearance: count what survives from the original population

**PZ15227** is the CRBN-recruiting BCL-XL degrader tested in the COPD study;
**DT2216** recruits VHL and is a different compound. PZ15227 produced target
degradation, apoptosis and senescence-marker changes in patient-derived airway
cells. The growth assay pretreated cells, then reseeded **10,000 cells per
well** before impedance monitoring. Increased growth after equal reseeding does
not establish increased net output from the original population. Ki67 is a
fraction; impedance also depends on cell size and adhesion. Lung slices showed
local airway-marker improvement, without a demonstrated organ-function outcome.
The authors did not test platelet effects. Nonsenescent non-COPD cells and
separately senescent non-COPD cells also had different responses. Clinical
DT2216 findings cannot supply the missing safety evidence for PZ15227.
[Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13078126/).

**DT2216** has actual human target-engagement evidence: its adult phase I trial
measured BCL-XL loss in peripheral leukocytes. It did not measure senescent-muscle
engagement. Among 20 participants, thrombocytopenia occurred in nine and caused
eight treatment interruptions; one event was a grade 4 dose-limiting toxicity.
No major bleeding or platelet transfusion was reported. Stable disease in four
participants, without objective responses, does not establish restorative
efficacy. A correction disclosed previously omitted competing interests, not
changed outcomes. The separate **DT2216 plus irinotecan** pediatric/young-adult
trial is **suspended for “Unacceptable Toxicity”** (registry update 24 August
2026, checked 21 September). No results are posted: causation by DT2216 alone and
the event type remain unresolved. Retain an investigational comparator, without
calling it platelet safe or transferring its findings to PZ15227.
[Adult trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12613848/),
[correction](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13045-026-01837-z),
[combination-trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06620302).

**Navitoclax has positive organ-function evidence and a contextual tumor
counterexample.** After paclitaxel in mice, it improved neurovascular, blood-brain
barrier and cognitive outcomes. Grip, gait and rotarod were assessed, but the
injury control did not show a deficit; these therefore do not demonstrate muscle
rescue. Methods and abstract give inconsistent treatment timing, which remains
unresolved. In a different study, chemotherapy-pretreated mice subsequently
inoculated with five T-ALL cells progressed faster after navitoclax; this was a
minimal-cell challenge, not recurrence of a previously treated endogenous tumor.
The four drug cohorts had ten mice each. Methods and a figure caption disagree
on dose. Neither result establishes MVA benefit or harm. Together they require
tumor-, host- and timing-specific testing rather than an assumption that clearing
senescent cells is uniformly beneficial.
[Functional study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10352561/),
[tumor-context study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6818774/).

## Gedatolisib: a stronger conditional experiment, not general mTOR rescue

A **preprint** reports that gedatolisib limited enlargement during prolonged
palbociclib arrest and reduced subsequent segregation defects in RPE1 and MCF7
cells. It used 30 nM and 7.5 nM, respectively; both drugs were washed out before
the mitosis assays. Several outcomes used three experiments, and single-cell
copy-number analysis examined 24 RPE1-p53-knockout clones per condition. This is
stronger than expression-only evidence. It prevents a drug-induced size defect;
it does not demonstrate reversal of existing aneuploidy or BUB1B-mutant muscle
rescue. BUBR1 concentration and kinetochore recruitment did not show a clear
size-dependent reduction. Nucleosides improved entry but did not correct the
reported mitotic delay/checkpoint weakness. The observed benefit must remain
assigned to **gedatolisib**, not everolimus or rapamycin.
[Preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.07.01.662622),
[author-uploaded primary text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/393355597_Cell_enlargement_causes_mitotic_errors_and_aneuploidy_in_cells_that_evade_senescence_after_CDK46_inhibition).

Gedatolisib received FDA approval on **14 July 2026**, in specified adult
breast-cancer combinations. Its label describes **class I PI3K and mTORC1/2**
inhibition, so this is not a selective mTORC1 experiment. The label warns about
stomatitis, dermatologic reactions and hyperglycemia; pediatric safety and
efficacy are unestablished. Approval makes this an identifiable repurposing
implementation, not a pediatric muscle-benefit result. **Retain a conditional
cell-size/fidelity comparator** if abnormal enlargement is demonstrated in the
relevant progenitors. Require benefit after drug washout, faithful descendants
and preserved absolute muscle output before promotion.
[FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-gedatolisib-fulvestrant-or-without-palbociclib-hr-positive-her2-negative-locally),
[label, sections 5, 8.4 and 12.1](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/219908Orig1s000lbl.pdf).

## Mitotic balancing requires the correct defect and endpoint

| Intervention | Positive finding | Decision and limiting evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Okadaic acid** | In HeLa cells under partial CDK1 inhibition, 20 nM increased visually normal completed mitoses from 3% to 20% with 1 µM RO3306, and from none to about 10% with 3 µM RO3306. | Upgrade the earlier localization-only appraisal: this is partial live-division rescue. Most treated cells were still abnormal; there is no sustained faithful lineage, muscle or therapeutic-exposure result. It is a perturbation-balancing reagent, not a BUBR1-restoring medicine. [Primary Results/Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4050138/). |
| **Hesperadin / ZM447439 / BI2536** | Following B56-PP2A depletion in RPE1 cells, 50 nM hesperadin, 1 µM ZM447439 or 40 nM BI2536 restored cold-stable kinetochore fibers (45-minute exposure; three experiments, over 80 cells per condition per experiment). | This extends the existing Aurora/PLK1 comparison. The cells were held with MG132: attachment stabilization is not completed accurate division. B56 depletion is a different perturbation from BUB1B deficiency; kinase effects cannot transfer without defining the actual imbalance. [Primary Figures 4–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3186838/). |

## More transcription, more divisions, or fewer chromosome defects?

| Intervention | Positive finding | Decision and limiting evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **SB-431542** | In Tgif1-null mouse embryonic fibroblasts, 1 µM increased Bub1b RNA approximately 1.5-fold (visual estimate; triplicate cultures). A separate 0.2 µM, three-day experiment increased growth (quadruplicate cultures). | Retain a transcription-regulation reserve. These are different regimens, without measured BUBR1 protein mediation or drug-treated chromosome correction. The authors report that treatment beyond one passage inhibited growth in both WT and null cells; sustained restoration cannot be inferred. Culture counts do not establish independent embryo counts. [Primary Figures 7–8 and Discussion](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0035460). |
| **EPZ-5676 / pinometostat** | Continuous DOT1L inhibition extended maximum BJ fibroblast population doublings by roughly four to five (visual estimate); Methods specify 500 nM, and Figure 2g also labels a 100 nM curve. | Retain a cellular-lifespan reserve: this is more than an expression result. The drug-lifespan panel has no stated independent-culture n or error bars. It delays arrest during serial culture, without establishing reversal, faithful daughter-cell genomes, muscle function or BUBR1 mediation. Rapamycin enhanced a transcriptional response; that does not establish synergistic lifespan benefit. [Primary Figure 2 and Appendix S1](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.70343). |
| **Uridine + cytidine + adenosine + guanosine** | The mixture reduced anaphase bridges, micronuclei and DNA-damage foci in SV40-LT-expressing BJ/tert fibroblasts (48 h; reported 50 µM, component-versus-total concentration ambiguous). | Retain a replication-stress/fidelity comparator. The viral mechanism involves **BUB1**, not BUB1B; component attribution and faithful euploid output are unresolved. The DNA-damage cell counts are not the bridge assay's replication count. The cell-size preprint used a different commercial nucleoside preparation (EmbryoMax, 1:100) and different endpoints without checkpoint rescue. Benefit cannot be assumed across these contexts; this is not a controlled same-mixture comparison. [Primary Figure 8 and Methods](https://journals.asm.org/doi/full/10.1128/jvi.02224-13). |

## Consequence for the proposal

The clearance findings sharpen the proposed founder/lineage assay: record loss,
renewal and useful output from the **starting** population, even when a study
shows more favorable survivors. The gedatolisib result adds cell size and timing
as measurable conditions for a pathway experiment. Neither result calls for
combining medicines or expanding the four primary niacin/apitegromab claims
without a separate prospective decision.

## Reading limits

PZ15227, DT2216, navitoclax and mitotic-balancing assessments used relevant
primary Methods/Results, tables and figure captions; original figure images,
raw data and complete supplements were not independently inspected. Source
timing/dose discrepancies remain unresolved. DT2216 trial status was checked
against the official registry API, with no posted results. Gedatolisib used
accessible author-uploaded preprint text and targeted official label text;
original preprint figures and supplements were not inspected. This is source
appraisal, not experimental reproduction. Original assessments and public
identifiers are exported; source bodies remain outside the repository.

SB-431542 and EPZ-5676 checks additionally included selected original figures;
the latter included Appendix S1 and supplemental Figure S2. Their graph
estimates are labeled, and biological replication gaps remain explicit. The
nucleoside paper's public publisher narrative and captions were read, but
Figure 8 pixels were unavailable; no bar-height estimate is asserted.
