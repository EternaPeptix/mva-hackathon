# jeweled — Track 2: testing two ways to preserve muscle

**Research proposal, revised 1 October 2026. Packaging updated 8 October 2026 to reconcile current coverage counts; scientific conclusions and candidate choices are unchanged.**

We provisionally nominate **niacin (nicotinic acid) for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. The aim is useful muscle function with preserved renewal in a child with recorded muscle atrophy and failure to thrive. Everolimus remains a conditional signaling probe. Neither primary candidate has demonstrated MVA rescue; this is a research comparison, not a treatment recommendation. The [candidate shortlist](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-candidate-shortlist.md) explains the selection and competing hypotheses.

**Media updated:** the report and final eight-slide deck use this pair. The accompanying V4 movie is 180 seconds and reuses the previously reviewed narration and caption text. The pitch presents a research proposal; it does not report a biological drug experiment.

## The genetic condition on this proposal

**BUB1B encodes BUBR1, which helps cells distribute chromosomes correctly.** Our [Track 1 hypothesis](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/jeweled_track1_report_submission.md) combines Leu737Ter, predicting a 736-amino-acid protein, and Asn1002Lys, still a variant of uncertain significance with unmeasured function. [Patient-cell experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2887387/) support low BUBR1 from reduced transcript supply and unstable missense proteins in selected MVA1 cases, not drug responsiveness here.

**Confirmed cis would give `[L737Ter;N1002K]/WT` and retire this pair as the compound-heterozygous explanation.** Translation stops before Asn1002Lys. Continuing a BUB1B explanation would require an independently demonstrated additional pathogenic allele in trans or dominant mechanism. Uncertain dosage cannot preserve the discarded biallelic premise. Phase, endogenous RNA/protein and allele function require measurement.

## Niacin: test restoration of working BUBR1

The 2010 HeLa titration found missegregation in most cells near 6% BUBR1 but little effect near 13%; these assay-specific levels are not treatment thresholds. [North's experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194088/) linked NAD-dependent SIRT2 to BUBR1 stabilization. NMN increased BUBR1; genetic SIRT2 elevation improved selected cardiac outcomes and male survival in hypomorphic mice. Neither was niacin treatment or demonstrated skeletal-muscle rescue.

[Two human NMN trials](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/nmn-human-functional-qualification.md) add a reported short-term treadmill-walking advantage over placebo and a longer physical-function null despite blood NAD augmentation. Their differing preparations, populations and tests, and reporting limitations, prevent a pooled efficacy conclusion; neither reports muscle BUBR1 rescue. This supports a fair experimental comparison with niacin, without establishing a winner.

An [earlier NMN trial](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/nmn-igarashi2022-qualification.md) adds exploratory gait/grip signals and a six-week chair-stand benefit, but a dispensing error left only 20 of 42 participants in the 12-week analysis; its registered skeletal muscle index primary was nonpositive. These findings warrant consideration with substantial exposure and analysis limits.

A separate randomized crossover study tested a mixture providing 207.5 mg/day niacin equivalents from tryptophan, nicotinic acid (4 mg/day) and nicotinamide in physically compromised older adults. Muscle NAD+ did not rise and objective mitochondrial, SPPB and cycling outcomes were nonpositive; one perceived-function domain was positive among multiple questionnaire domains. The low nicotinic-acid dose and mixed ingredients mean this does not test the higher-dose niacin regimen or BUB1B rescue. It cautions against treating NAD precursors as interchangeable. [Full qualification](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/nad-precursor-mixture-muscle-qualification.md).

[Pirinen's small open-label mitochondrial-myopathy study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32386566/) showed niacin-mediated muscle NAD correction. Patient knee-extension strength rose about **1.1-fold after ten months**, with larger changes elsewhere, but niacin-treated healthy controls also gained strength. Without placebo, this is engagement evidence, not disease-specific functional rescue. A [pediatric case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39173541/) progressed despite biochemical correction. Niacin must therefore improve useful muscle, with BUBR1-dependent evidence to claim restoration. Other NAD precursors cannot supply its missing evidence; prescription-niacin pediatric safety and effectiveness are unestablished.

A [2025 nicotinic-acid experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40062928/) adds a different human-cell precedent: toxin-exposed oocyte maturation averaged 56.7% with co-treatment versus 31.7% with toxin alone and 73.5% in controls. The figure does not label the direct treatment-versus-toxin significance test. This is partial culture protection, not clinical fertility benefit; the mouse mechanism work concerns SIRT1, without SIRT2 or drug-induced BUBR1 restoration. The [primary appraisal](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/unfiltered-findings.md) keeps this positive result separate from the missing MVA muscle evidence.

## Apitegromab: test compensation in surviving muscle

Apitegromab blocks activation of promyostatin/latent myostatin. The full [SAPPHIRE report](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/339849/1/Safety%20and%20efficacy%20of%20apitegromab%20in%20nonambulatory%20type%202%20or%20type%203%20spinal%20muscular%20atrophy.pdf) confirms a primary HFMSE difference of **1.8 points (95% CI 0.30–3.32; p=0.019)** for pooled doses in nonambulatory SMA participants aged 2–12 on nusinersen/risdiplam. The other prespecified primary comparison, 20 mg/kg alone, was not significant; the testing hierarchy then stopped. Consequently the label-dose 10 mg/kg estimate (2.2 points, nominal p=.012) and secondary outcomes were exploratory, not a separate confirmatory win. This supports a compensation hypothesis, not transfer to MVA. [Full-source qualification](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-sapphire-fulltext-qualification.md).

The [US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b1cd8ab8-8a10-48ed-9806-75a44f22b963) covers SMA from age two on SMN2-targeted treatment. Fractures occurred in **5/53 at 10 mg/kg versus 1/50 on placebo**. Regular intravenous access and one-to-two-hour infusions every four weeks add burden. This describes labeled use, not an MVA regimen. Bone, developmental and tumor risks remain material.

A [related antibody in SMN-restored SMA mice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6423420/) increased absolute torque without improving torque per muscle mass; a more affected model responded despite lower latent myostatin. Thus **excess myostatin is not required** for a compensation hypothesis. The assay must nevertheless contain the precursor/activation system and demonstrate actual-drug engagement. [EMBRAZE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04440-4) retained lean mass without notable functional differences: muscle size alone cannot pass. No myostatin-blockade rescue in MVA was identified. The [appraisal](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/apitegromab-review.md) retains these intervention and disease boundaries.

## Everolimus: a conditional mechanism probe

[Sieben Figure 5C](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/126863/figure/5) shows stronger phosphoprotein bands in monoallelic carrier muscle, **two mice per genotype**, with Ponceau loading control but no total-substrate denominators, quantification or inferential statistics. This does not establish harmful mTORC1 excess or rescue. Partial genetic normalization must qualify that premise before drug attribution. The [rapamycin observation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3785294/) was parenthetical and unpublished; [everolimus-associated BUBR1 loss](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10149375/) occurred in late-passage nonmutant cells, without the early-passage decrease. Neither establishes MVA benefit or harm. [Reserve-route decisions](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-route-decisions.md) retain arimoclomol and conditional senolysis.

## What the completed work established

The completed comparisons support credible research alternatives; the [candidate shortlist](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-candidate-shortlist.md) explains the current nominations and what would change them. Amla (*Phyllanthus emblica*) earns a high-priority laboratory comparison among reviewed consumer preparations. One study in spontaneously immortalized NCM460 colon cells reported higher BubR1 mRNA and fewer mitotic/genomic abnormalities, using different exposure durations for the RNA and checkpoint-proxy assays. Two same-group papers add cancer and chemotherapy context, rather than independent BUBR1 replication. Arrest and removal of damaged cells can lower damage counts while reducing productive cells; MVA cells could respond adversely. BUBR1 protein, causal mediation, MVA muscle, systemic exposure and equivalent retail preparations remain unestablished. The [qualified comparison](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/common-compound-priorities.md#amla--phyllanthus-emblica-fruit-extract--priority-consumer-derived-assay-challenger) retains these distinctions.

The most useful distinctions are the measured endpoint, the administered preparation and the treatment contrast. A controlled functional positive can matter without a BUBR1 assay; a molecular increase can matter without proving faithful division. Neither becomes clinical superiority through a pathway argument. The table summarizes the comparisons that affect research priority. The [extended comparison appendix](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-extended-comparisons.md) preserves the earlier route-specific evidence, qualifications and source links. The [priority matrix](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/common-compound-priorities.md) identifies useful comparisons and the key unresolved questions.

Nomination is a research choice; none of these results establishes treatment efficacy for the patient.

This is a selection effect with output loss, not a drug result or patient forecast; faithful individual-lineage tracking remains an experimental requirement.

Walking and chair-rise performance were nonpositive. Analysis excluded eight of 102 randomized participants; late registration and incomplete safety reporting remain limitations. This is a substantive strength comparator, with no demonstrated MVA transfer or equivalence to Lovaza or generic fish oil.

[Astaxanthin](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/astaxanthin-functional-qualification.md) advances beyond its earlier culture-only evidence: a nursing-home trial reports a randomized walking interaction (24 of 30 completed), and a ten-person crossover reports longer cycling endurance. Strength/mass nulls, different combination products and reporting errors limit a general muscle-rescue claim. It is a compensation challenger under the same criteria.

The [census and source audits](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/README.md) bound discovery coverage: **15,357 main catalog/supplement entities**, separately **6,467 NIH ingredient groups**, **3,971 FDA food rows** and **249 SGC labels**—not unique-molecule totals or efficacy assessments. All **234 food-query rows** are annotated across 213 source IDs; **212 available abstracts** were read and [one unavailable abstract](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/fatty-acid-binding-source-limit.md) receives no credit. The ledger has **310 appraisals**, including source-qualified BPA, NSAID, MnBP and ozone adverse-direction transcript comparators that are not treatment leads. The dated [EPA CPDat v4.0/v4.1 Formulation and Article overlays](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/workflow/track2-drug-census/cpdat-v4.1/README.md) add consumer-product discovery; exact-name co-mentions are not efficacy findings. Primary studies report lower BUB1B/Bub1b RNA after high-concentration ibuprofen, naproxen and MnBP exposure in Sertoli or granulosa cells, and after acute high-dose ozone exposure in neonatal whole lung. None measured MVA muscle rescue, faithful chromosome segregation or BUBR1 protein/function, and the exposure models do not establish ordinary-use harm. They do not change the candidates. ## A partner experiment that can answer the question

**Laboratory partnership and patient-derived model access are unestablished.** The [pipeline](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md) starts with affected/corrected myogenic cultures, procedural controls, allele-product measurements and a reproducible functional deficit. Apply the cis stop early; engineered models must retain unresolved alternatives. Failed model qualification is not drug failure.

Test the candidates separately with measured, justified exposures: niacin versus vehicle; apitegromab versus matched nonbinding isotype. Qualify NAD/BUBR1 dependence or myostatin engagement. Measure absolute evoked muscle output, repeated-stimulation performance, progenitor replacement and chromosome fidelity in both differentiating progenitors and established muscle.

**Either cohort may improve if the other, renewable reserve and fidelity meet prespecified preservation margins.** Marker changes, arrested divisions and favorable survivor percentages cannot substitute. Freeze one regimen per candidate; confirm in independent preparations with masking and Holm correction across four drug-by-cohort claims. Everolimus remains exploratory unless separately prespecified. General benefit in corrected and affected cells may count, but does not establish BUBR1 restoration. Inadequate exposure or an absent target system leaves a nonresponse uninterpretable.

Recorded rhabdomyosarcoma requires [tumor/regimen compatibility review](https://github.com/EternaPeptix/mva-hackathon/blob/73e997056a9b12d8a4f307b26a676b7992a66dc3/reports/appendices/track2-evidence-pipeline.md#rhabdomyosarcoma-evidence-and-treatment-compatibility); current status and regimen are unknown. Culture benefit cannot settle fracture, developmental or recurrence risk. The reusable contribution is the sequence-to-mechanism-to-function workflow and its absolute-output decision rule, not a new drug discovery. Drug exposures and tissue models require requalification; models remain the experimental bottleneck.

Claude Code and Codex assisted literature review, analysis and writing on personal subscriptions, with training disabled according to the user; exact historical model/plan names are unverified.
