## Positive evidence preserved

- **Capsaicin, PMID 32909881:** local 1% pretreatment reduced rat incisional pain behavior, with dermal/epidermal denervation. This is a functional noncancer positive with a local sensory tradeoff, not generalized tissue regeneration.
- **C-type natriuretic peptide, PMID 38136917:** pretreatment improved goat oocyte maturation and spindle morphology, with redox/mitochondrial-associated changes. Chromosome-number and offspring outcomes are not supplied; native CNP is not automatically equivalent to clinical analogs.
- **Quercetin, PMID 38526326:** 20 mg/kg intraperitoneal treatment reduced injury/inflammatory and mast-cell readouts in an LL-37 rat bladder-injury model. Pain/voiding function and proof that Lpl mediates the drug effect are not established by the abstract. The route does not establish a food or oral-supplement equivalent.
- **Glucosamine hydrochloride plus chondroitin sulfate, PMID 25719429:** a randomized, double-blind crossover study in 18 healthy overweight adults reported a 23% CRP reduction (P = 0.048) after 28 days, while other inflammatory/oxidative biomarkers were nonsignificant. This is a human combination biomarker result, not monotherapy or functional muscle/cancer-incidence evidence.
- **AICAR/AMPK stimulation, PMID 23199370:** increased mouse-oocyte maturation outputs, but did not change peripheral spindle movement. Faster maturation alone does not establish chromosome fidelity or embryo competence.
- Anticancer positives include **choline salicylate plus low-dose selinexor** with reported nonmalignant-cell and mouse-organ sparing (PMID 33507295), **T521** A549 xenograft suppression (PMID 27902479), **abemaciclib or palbociclib plus osimertinib** culture synergy (PMID 42511755), and **JQ1 plus volasertib** cell death/proliferation effects (PMID 28061448). These benefits remain tied to their models and exposures.

The Eucalyptus triterpene extract and iron-quercetin complex records (PMIDs 38293365 and 38658734) retain current molecular/cellular findings separately from functional animal or conditioned-medium benefits cited as prior work. Mixtures, constituents and complexes are not interchangeable.

## Nulls, adverse effects and attribution limits

Most of 30 ovarian cancer models resisted **ML-792**; five were highly sensitive and seven intermediate (PMID 40487451). **Gamma-secretase inhibitor plus vincristine** increased HeLa death, but the unnamed inhibitor alone had no viability effect (PMID 24200971). **Trichostatin A** caused spindle defects in the tested mitotic cells, while genetic HDAC3 rescue required active enzyme (PMID 18326024). Silver nanoparticle/ionic-silver toxicology and alkylphenol-mixture adverse phenotypes are retained without turning them into therapeutic candidates; the latter exposure did not induce tumors in the stated xenograft experiment.

Genetic tumor suppression is not assigned to a nearby compound: ZINC3809191 has an explicit in-vitro result, while the mouse arm requires qualification (PMID 41868655); PRC1-shRNA xenograft effects are not automatically assigned to piperlongumine (PMID 28190297). Protein-level changes, predicted sensitivity and pathway enrichment remain separate from functional treatment outcomes. BUB1 is distinguished from BUB1B; chemical-genetic synchronization agents, toxic challenges, specimen reagents, amino-acid residues and endogenous metabolites retain their actual roles.
