# TG101209: retain early post-challenge lung protection, with selective functional positives

[Ye et al., 2026; PMID 41909672/PMC13021449](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1755208) supports refining the existing TG101209 appraisal beyond its separate thermal-probe role. It reports reduced injury after an LPS challenge, two positive respiratory readouts, and a favorable survival-curve direction. It does **not** establish BUB1B restoration, MVA benefit, muscle rescue, broad safety or clinical suitability. Native figures contain important nulls that narrow the prose.

## Actual exposure and comparison

Young male C57BL/6 mice (6–7 weeks) received intratracheal LPS **2 mg/kg in 50 µL PBS**, then TG101209 **40 mg/kg intratracheally 2 h later**. Controls were saline, LPS alone, TG alone and LPS plus dexamethasone **5 mg/kg** at the corresponding time. Outcomes were assessed 72 h after challenge; a separate cohort was followed for 7 days. This is early **post-challenge** treatment, not pretreatment or demonstrated repair of established injury: no pre-dose injury measurement or delayed-treatment arm is shown. No repeat-dose schedule is stated.

TG101209 came from MedChem Express, but catalog/lot, purity, salt and drug vehicle are not reported. PBS is specified for LPS, not authenticated as the TG formulation. The named reagent must remain distinct from TG101348/fedratinib and any clinical product. RAW264.7 cells received LPS 50 ng/mL for 24 h with/without TG; 1 µM was selected for mechanistic work. Sequential addition or washout is not described.

## Function, survival and injury: retain positives and nulls

The native Supplementary Figure S1 labels the **LPS+TG versus LPS** comparisons as follows (n=5 mice/group, 72 h):

| Published respiratory measure | Direction and native significance |
|---|---|
| Tidal volume | Higher, **P<.01** |
| FEV100/FVC | Higher, **P<.05** |
| Minute volume | Numerically higher, **ns** |
| Peak expiratory flow | Numerically higher, **ns** |
| FEV100 | Numerically higher, **ns** |
| Lung resistance | Numerically lower, **ns** |

Thus, the broad Results claim that respiratory deficits were reversed exceeds the endpoint-specific significance shown. Peak flow and FEV100 also lack a significant LPS-versus-control difference. These remain actual published functional readouts, not histology alone, but acquisition is underdescribed: Methods specifies a Buxco whole-body plethysmography pressure chamber without the forced maneuver/calibration/derivation needed to independently validate the reported FEV100/FVC and resistance measurements. This gap does not establish that the data are invalid.

Native Figure 1C favors LPS+TG over LPS. Its day 7 levels, combined with caption n=7/group, are consistent with **5/7 survivors for LPS+TG, 1/7 for LPS, 4/7 for LPS+dexamethasone, 6/7 for TG alone and 7/7 for saline**. These are transparent visual/count inferences, not recovered event records. No curve-specific P value or survival-test method is supplied; the displayed statistical caption refers to LPS comparisons, and the general Methods gives ANOVA/Tukey. Retain the favorable curve direction, but not independently validated statistical superiority to dexamethasone. The apparent TG-only death is retained with unknown cause, rather than attributed to proven toxicity or erased by the safety narrative.

Evans-blue leakage, wet/dry ratio and blinded lung-injury scores improve versus LPS in Figure 1; representative histology supports less injury. BALF total cells, neutrophils and macrophages decrease significantly, while the TG lymphocyte comparison is **ns**. Wet/dry preparation is inconsistent: Methods says 45 °C until stable; Figure 1 caption says 60 °C for 48 h. No corrected protocol or exact bar values are invented.

## Mechanism and safety boundaries

Lung marker RNA/protein/IF and flow cytometry support treatment-associated changes toward a less inflammatory macrophage-marker profile. This is not lineage tracing or proof that individual M1 cells converted into M2 cells. In native Figure 4C, the LPS-versus-control M2 fraction is **ns**, despite prose saying it decreased. In culture, TG retains positive CD80/CD163 protein, displayed marker-qPCR and cytokine contrasts, but the iNOS and Arg1 protein comparisons are **ns**. In Figure 6, cultured p-JAK2/JAK2 and p-STAT3Tyr705/STAT3 decrease significantly; p-STAT3Ser727/STAT3 is **ns**. All three displayed lung-tissue phosphorylation ratios retain significant treatment contrasts.

No genetic JAK2/STAT3 perturbation, resistant target, macrophage depletion, lineage-specific necessity test or orthogonal rescue establishes exclusive pathway mediation. The study does not directly measure target binding/selectivity here. Figure 7 is a proposed-mechanism schematic. Separate earlier TG pharmacology remains relevant, but no cross-assay clinical safety margin is inferred.

Supplementary Figure S3 shows a large CCK-8 signal decrease at **10 and 20 µM** after 24 h, with near-control signals at displayed concentrations through 5 µM; this is a metabolic viability proxy. Supplementary Figure S2 provides representative heart/liver/spleen/kidney images, not quantified chronic safety. No body-weight trajectory, organ chemistry or cause-of-death record is supplied. The favorable histology and low-concentration culture readout do not establish general safety.

## Units, reproducibility and BUB1B relevance

Methods states randomized mouse-group assignment and two blinded pathologists for histology; allocation concealment and blinding of other endpoints are not specified. Bars use n=5 mice/group, survival n=7 in a separate cohort, and cell experiments n=3 independent replicates. Multiple endpoints use reported ANOVA/Tukey; no prespecified primary endpoint, across-endpoint multiplicity plan, sample-size justification or raw respiratory/event data is supplied.

Two caption-to-axis mappings differ: Figure 3B/C names CD80/iNOS in the caption but iNOS/CD80 on native axes; Figure 5F–I caption order differs from native Cd80/Nos2/Cd163/Arg1. Figure 4 fluorophore labels also differ from Methods. These are documented source inconsistencies, not an image-manipulation claim. The source's Discussion also contains a pathway-direction sentence inconsistent with its model; it cannot substitute for a necessity test.

Across the complete inspected scientific main/captions, all seven native figures and the four-page supplement, **no BUB1B/BubR1 RNA, protein, localization, stability or checkpoint/fidelity assay is reported**. Table S1 contains 10 other genes. No chromosome segregation, muscle force, BUB1B-mutant or MVA assay is present. This is a finite-source scope statement, not an all-literature null. The earlier PMID39818011 thermal signal remains separately limited: heat resistance is not unheated abundance, slower turnover or restored function, and its original figures were not read anew here.

**Disposition:** the existing TG101209 entry is refined to a lung-protection context comparator plus the separate experimental thermal probe. Preserve two respiratory positives, four respiratory nulls, a descriptive survival advantage, formulation uncertainty and the absence of a BUB1B causal bridge. No new compound identity, original-queue reading credit or nominee promotion follows.
