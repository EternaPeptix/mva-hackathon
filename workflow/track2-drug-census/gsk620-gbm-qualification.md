# GSK620: credit the mouse survival result without transferring it to MVA

**23 September 2026.** GSK620 now has a qualified pharmacological survival
result in a glioblastoma xenograft model. It advances from a cell-only evidence
pointer to an experimental anticancer comparator. This does not establish
BUBR1 restoration, prevention of MVA-associated cancer, or muscle compensation.
The niacin/apitegromab research comparison remains provisional and unchanged.

## Published experiment and earlier version

The controlling source is [Vadla et al., Neuro-Oncology 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/noaf169)
(PMID 40686478, PMC12700489). Its complete main text and figure captions,
supplement text/captions/methods, main images 1, 5 and 6, and supplementary images
S5 and S6 were examined. Other figure images and the downloaded supplementary
spreadsheet were not independently analyzed. No new independent reviewer or
biological replication is claimed.

| Evidence | What was actually administered and observed | Limit on inference |
|---|---|---|
| **Mouse survival, Figure 6E** | GSK620 versus vehicle in female BALB/c nude mice bearing intracranial GSC11 tumors; treatment after tumor establishment. Main Methods specify 30 mg/kg oral dosing, five days/week. Survival favored GSK620; caption states n=5 and the graph shows P<0.05. | Exact P, hazard ratio, confidence interval and numerical median survival are not supplied. The caption does not explicitly say whether n=5 is per group. Morbidity-triggered euthanasia is the endpoint. This is a small, immune-deficient cancer model. |
| **Cell invasion and clonogenicity** | GSC11 invasion fell after 0.5 µM, 24 h (P=.0369); GSC11 and GSC23 colony formation fell after the stated 0.5–5 µM treatment range (P=.0174 and .0203). | TS576 PTEN-KO colony comparison in S6B is P=.0503, not significant at .05. Colony reduction is a tumor endpoint, not productive normal-tissue renewal. |
| **Chromatin engagement** | At 1 µM, 24 h, GSK620 reduced chromatin-associated BRD2 and its occupancy at selected mesenchymal-gene promoters; BRD4 promoter comparisons were nonsignificant. | GSK620 is a BET BD2-directed compound. A preferential cellular BRD2 result does not establish exclusive BRD2-protein selectivity. No BUBR1-restoration or chromosome-fidelity result is reported in the inspected narrative and figures. |
| **Radiation combination** | GSK620 pretreatment plus irradiation reduced growth more than the displayed single treatments in three GSC models. | Drug-alone growth comparisons in Figure 6G are nonsignificant. No formal interaction estimate or drug-plus-radiation animal survival experiment establishes synergy. Genetic BRD2/RelA results remain separate. |

The [2023 preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.07.03.546613)
(PMID 37461511, PMC10349949) described a different 10 mg/kg, every-other-day
regimen and reported no survival improvement. It is an earlier version of this
work, not an independent negative replication. The published positive result
replaces that preprint as the current study result; the regimen change is
documented, but cannot by itself explain the difference causally.

The published vehicle is 10% DMSO, 40% PEG, 5% Tween-80 and 45% saline.
Supplement S6D reports random assignment after tumor-burden imaging. Blinding,
attrition accounting and an exact post-implantation treatment-start day are not
explicitly supplied in the inspected material.

## Exposure and normal-cell evidence

S6C reports a total brain/plasma AUC ratio of 27.2%, plasma half-life of 1.05 h,
plasma peak 1.86 µM and brain peak 0.40 µM. The PK dosing amount is not stated in
the inspected methods, so it is not assumed to equal the efficacy-arm dose.
Total brain concentration is not unbound exposure or evidence of a reachable
human treatment window.

The authors describe normal neural-progenitor sparing. S6F actually supplies
three-day ATP-based growth curves at 0.5 and 1 µM without an equivalence bound
or statistical annotation; treated curves are lower at the final time point.
This is limited comparative evidence, not proof of normal-cell safety. The
study does not assess pediatric development, muscle renewal, faithful chromosome
segregation, long-term hematological toxicity or intact tumor surveillance.

## Keep each compound's evidence separate

| Compound | Drug-specific observation in this paper | Competitive disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **GSK620** | Cell engagement, invasion/colony results and the actual mouse survival experiment above. | Retain as an anticancer research comparator. It has organism-level drug evidence, but no demonstrated route to correcting BUBR1 insufficiency or improving MVA muscle output. |
| **GSK778** | BET BD1 comparator in ATP-based proliferation assays: reported IC50 values 6.47 µM in GSC23 and 11.63 µM in GSC11; 8.4 µM in the supplementary iGBM-MES model. | Add a distinct cell comparator. Its stronger growth inhibition than GSK620 does not establish selective killing of abnormal MVA cells or normal-tissue benefit. No GSK778 mouse survival arm here. |
| **ABBV-744** | Mesenchymal-gene suppression and radiation-combination growth curves in S5B/S6E, using the reported 0.5 µM, 24 h pretreatment. | Retain alongside its separately qualified macrophage/vascular findings. COL9A1 in S5B is nonsignificant (P=.107501), and S6E has no displayed inferential statistics. Do not assign GSK620's survival result to ABBV-744. |
| **Ipatasertib** | At 1 µM for 24 h in U87 cells, representative fractionation blots show lower chromatin-associated BRD2/BRD4/RelA (Figure 1D, n=3). | Add an upstream AKT mechanism probe. This figure is not a drug-specific survival, BUBR1, chromosome-fidelity or muscle-rescue result. |

Neither GSK620's anticancer success nor the current nominees' different-disease
results decide the MVA comparison. A promotion would require a relevant
MVA-associated tumor or normal-tissue model, a demonstrated benefit for that
indication, and a usable exposure window without loss of tissue function or
chromosome fidelity. The same transfer limits apply to niacin and apitegromab;
they have not demonstrated MVA rescue either.
