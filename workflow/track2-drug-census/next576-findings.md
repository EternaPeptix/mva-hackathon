## What changes the next decision

Ursolic acid opens a substantive muscle-compensation comparison. Its discovery
record concerns liver fibrosis, but targeted follow-up found actual muscle-force
experiments and mixed controlled human evidence. The separate
[primary comparison](next576-primary-qualification.md) records the study-specific
results, reading limits and decision. MG132 supplies a different lesson:
improved chromosome alignment can coexist with failure to complete mitosis.
Neither a higher checkpoint marker nor a better-looking arrested cell is enough
to nominate a muscle-restoration drug. A distinct bortezomib-plus-humanin study earns contracture-prevention credit, with mortality and normal-muscle growth tradeoffs; this revises an existing route rather than adding another medicine entity.
