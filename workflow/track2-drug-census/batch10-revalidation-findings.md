## Positive findings and their limits

- **SP012 — PMID 25024138:** the source reports dose-dependent inhibition of endothelial tube formation and reduced growth, development and vascularization of human endometriotic lesions in alymphoid mouse xenografts versus PBS. This is a genuine noncancer lesion result. The abstract does not give a dose, systemic safety, muscle endpoint or BUB1B/MVA rescue.
- **Claramine — PMID 34029592:** a cellular screen identified improved surface insulin-receptor availability, reduced receptor cleavage, altered BACE1 trafficking and increased autophagy. Diabetic-mouse liver observations supply disease context; the abstract does not establish a Claramine-treated mouse glycemic or muscle benefit.
- **Nutlin-3a and exercise — PMID 38989952:** both reportedly improved physical function in mice bearing tumors derived from MDM2-overexpressing glioma cells; Nutlin-3a also inhibited glioma proliferation. Preserve the organism-level functional result. Tumor context, assay, group contrasts, dose, ambiguous efficacy-reversal wording and the source's natural-product characterization require qualification before a broader compensation claim.
- Cancer-directed benefits also include a **DM1-based CD98hc ADC** that largely spared normal colon organoids (PMID 37559159), **RPS6-V-PMO** growth inhibition in cells, patient-derived organoids and xenografts (PMID 39309509), and **pectolinarigenin**-associated gastric cancer cell death/proteomic changes (PMID 30380781). These outcomes do not transfer automatically to normal tissues, dietary products or MVA.
- **Selenomethionine versus methylselenic acid** produced distinct prostate cancer transcriptional/cell-cycle responses (PMID 17222674). The observed arrest/reporter effects are not prevention or tumor-regression evidence, and selenium compounds are not interchangeable.

## Nulls, harms and identity distinctions retained

Reducing MAPKAPK2 activity protected colorectal cancer cells under glucose limitation and weakened trametinib efficacy (PMID 41662531). This cancer-cell survival advantage is not a normal-tissue benefit. Galectin-7 silencing reduced invasion/migration but did not change proliferation or apoptosis (PMID 28454344). Gene-expression prognostic markers failed independent IHC confirmation in lung adenocarcinoma (PMID 40333815).

Removing an additional centromeric Aurora B pool restored checkpoint-silencing timing while increasing chromosome missegregation (PMID 31868888). The RecurIndex comparison of 100% versus 99.7% survival had P = 0.60 and was retrospective/nonrandomized (PMID 41675005); it establishes neither chemotherapy harm nor benefit. Genetic rescue results involving Hsp72, BTG3, DAXX or centrobin remain distinct from drug administration.

Auxin is an engineered-degron reagent (PMID 32917881), dTAG compounds act on engineered tagged PPP2CA (PMID 38450154), and imiquimod is a psoriasis-model challenge here (PMID 21483750). Amino-acid residues, isotope labels, endogenous metabolites and protein-name expansions are not inferred therapies. The exact ERK, JNK, MAPKAPK2 and radiosensitizing compounds remain unnamed where the abstract leaves them unnamed.
