## Proposed narration — not recorded

### Preserve useful protein and productive cells

Our question is whether preserving usable BUBR1 can sustain chromosome segregation and muscle renewal. The genomic hypothesis contains a stop variant and N1002K; phase and surviving protein function remain unresolved. We need a qualified cell model before claiming a drug can help.

### Compare two concrete candidates

We nominate niacin, the nicotinic-acid medicine, with arimoclomol as a challenger. Human muscle studies show that niacin can raise NAD. Separate experiments connect NAD and SIRT2 to BUBR1 stability. Those links do not yet demonstrate mutant rescue. Arimoclomol might improve protein quality, but its BUBR1 link is also unproved. Test them separately.

### Establish what is rescuable

Measure the actual full-length and truncated products. Compare genetic correction and controlled allele expression. More protein can help, do nothing or interfere. Count segregation outcomes and productive divisions, including deaths, arrest and lost lineages. Fewer errors among a shrinking group of survivors are insufficient.

### Follow through to muscle function

Measure actual drug exposure, then absolute muscle output from the starting progenitors. Test mature-muscle function separately and follow both after withdrawal. NAD, heat-shock markers or protein abundance alone cannot pass. Genetic rescue with drug failure rejects the implementation without discarding the mechanism.

### Keep the strongest alternatives visible

Senescent-cell clearance improved tissue function in BubR1-deficient mice, but an eligible drug must reproduce that benefit selectively. Everolimus now requires additional pathway evidence; our RNA analysis did not corroborate its target, and published counterexamples weaken its lead.

### Ask for the decisive experiment

Rhabdomyosarcoma history requires testing tumor protection and relevant treatment interactions. No drug experiment has run. We seek a consented cell model and a partner laboratory to determine whether either candidate preserves useful tissue function at justified exposure.

## Approved narration — unchanged recording

### Turn a genomic hypothesis into a useful test

Every proposed treatment starts with a question: which cellular problem are we trying to improve? Our working hypothesis involves BUB1B and chromosome instability. We propose a useful experiment for this child, with clear reasons to advance or stop. No drug-response experiment has been performed.

### Resolve the two gene copies

Whole-reference calling is complete and its outputs verified. Two methods recover a BUB1B stop change and a missense change from the four-lane alignment. Their phase and missense function remain unknown. The pair is our primary research hypothesis, not a confirmed diagnosis. Other explanations remain open.

### Separate two possible routes to benefit

A qualified cell model and genetic correction control must first establish the defect. One hypothesis asks whether improving protein quality restores chromosome segregation. Another asks whether reducing measured stress improves function while chromosome errors persist. More protein or a lower stress signal alone cannot establish rescue.

### Test each drug against its own hypothesis

Arimoclomol would probe a demonstrated protein-quality defect; acetylcysteine would probe a demonstrated oxidative-stress defect. Both have approved uses elsewhere, but neither has demonstrated benefit for this genotype. Test them separately, with measured exposure and separate functional readouts. High-exposure-only effects would not support practical repurposing.

### Follow the first division from each starting cell

Count visible-error first anaphases among scorable first anaphases, alongside observed yield without a scored error per starting cell. Track death, no entry, ongoing mitosis, slippage, unscorable events and tracking loss. Neither count proves a normal karyotype. Measure function, viable-cell output and lineage changes so arrest, selective killing and abnormal-clone expansion remain visible.

### Test whether tumor cells are protected

Rhabdomyosarcoma is recorded, so test tumor protection and interference with any confirmed cancer treatment. HSP70 inhibitor studies motivate this check, but do not show that arimoclomol promotes cancer; its label reports negative animal carcinogenicity studies. Nonmalignant benefit cannot outweigh a credible adverse tumor signal. Inconclusive tests remain inconclusive. Laboratory access is unestablished, and independent confirmation and translational review would still be required.
