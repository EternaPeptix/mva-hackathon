# Track 2: testing two ways to preserve muscle

**21 September 2026. Research proposal; Track 2 has not been submitted.**

The [adopted drug-census decision](../workflow/track2-drug-census/decision-report.md) is incorporated into this package. We nominate **niacin for BUBR1 restoration and apitegromab for downstream muscle compensation, tested separately**. Everolimus remains a conditional mTORC1 mechanism probe. Neither primary candidate has demonstrated MVA rescue. Their shared goal is greater absolute muscle output with preserved renewal and chromosome accuracy.

Niacin retains the more proximal hypothesis; apitegromab has stronger actual-drug human functional evidence from treated SMA, with age/dose limits, fracture risk and intravenous access burden. It requires a suitable myostatin activation system and drug engagement, not mandatory excess baseline myostatin. The linked census, updated 23 September 2026, retains 15,357 entities plus 40 separately assessed FDA product entries and 233 curated route/compound appraisals. This is measured retrieval and review coverage, not efficacy evidence for every medicine.

Completed work comprises allele-product reconstruction, literature and route
comparison, synthetic analysis of misleading cell fractions, and a reproducible
public mouse RNA reanalysis. The RNA analysis did not corroborate an mTORC1
transcriptional program; the synthetic example informs measurement design.
The next milestone is an interpretable affected-versus-corrected muscle model.
Laboratory and patient-model access remain unsecured.
