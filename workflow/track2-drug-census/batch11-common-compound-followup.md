# Batch 11: common-compound functional challengers

**Provisional source follow-up, 28 September 2026.** This note records three targeted full-text follow-ups surfaced by the functional eligibility screen. One root reader reviewed relevant Methods, outcomes, analysis and limitation sections and the available trial records; no independent review has yet occurred. These are not ledger admissions, a completed all-drug census, or evidence of benefit in BUB1B-related MVA.

The fixed 160-record abstract screen passed source-identity, abstract-hash, anchor, raw-origin and notice-routing checks. Its provisional lanes were 74 controlled human-function reports, 6 uncontrolled human-function reports, 16 human mass/mechanism reports, 3 preclinical-function reports, 1 preclinical-mechanism report, 59 context/review/protocol records and 1 unresolved record. All 160 available abstracts were read by one reviewer. The batch remains pending independent review; the repo's accepted coverage remains 1,160 screened records. See the [coverage record](outcome-first-discovery.md) for the accepted count and pending-review status; record-level screen/source bindings remain in the external working archive.

| Preparation | Relevant human result | Why it is a candidate | Why it stays behind the current pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcium-HMB**, as tested in the 2023 sarcopenia trial | In 34 adults aged at least 60 with sarcopenia, 18 received calcium-HMB and 16 placebo for 12 weeks; both groups performed resistance exercise twice weekly. The paper names grip strength at weeks 8 and 12 as primary: the week-8 contrast was non-significant (**+1.49 kg**, 95% CI −0.43 to 3.41; p=0.124), while week 12 favored HMB by **+4.61 kg** (2.93–6.28; p<0.001). Secondary week-12 differences favored HMB for gait speed (**+0.11 m/s**, 0.02–0.20; p=0.014) and five-times chair stand (**−3.65 s**, −5.72 to −1.58; p=0.001). It found no significant difference in skeletal muscle mass, index or other body-composition measures. The administered powder provided 1.5 g calcium-HMB per sachet twice daily; free-acid and retail-product equivalence are not established. | Best newly surfaced common-supplement functional reserve: a controlled primary strength contrast plus secondary task measures in people with low muscle function. | One small, single-centre adult trial; all participants received exercise; missing values were handled with last observation carried forward; several outcomes were tested; and no MVA/BUBR1 or chromosome-fidelity outcome was measured. The article cites ChiCTR2000028778, but the registry page returned HTTP 405 in this pass and its historical fields are not bundled here. Do not use registry-versus-paper discrepancies as settled facts until the original record is recovered. [Primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37248756/), [cited trial record](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showproj.aspx?proj=47571). |
| **Vitamin D2 repletion**, conditional on documented deficiency/malabsorption | A single-centre, double-blind RCT randomized 84 adults with chronic intestinal failure and 25(OH)D <30 ng/mL to placebo or IM vitamin D2 600,000 IU every two weeks for 10 weeks. At week 12, versus placebo, reported differences included grip **+2.3 kg** (95% CI 0.629–3.971; p=0.008), 6-m walk speed **+0.12 m/s** (0.037–0.203; p=0.005), appendicular mass index **+0.4 kg/m²** (0.108–0.692; p=0.008), and SF-36 physical score **+6.2** (2.966–9.434; p<0.001); authors report Bonferroni correction. | A relatively strong multi-endpoint function signal, but it tests short-term correction of measured deficiency in a malabsorption population using a very high-dose injection schedule. It is a conditional repletion route, not a general MVA muscle treatment. Only 12-week safety was measured; no BUB1B/MVA or chromosome-fidelity outcome. [Primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41449463/), [pre-enrollment trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06283615). |
| **Inulin**, a common prebiotic fibre | In a 2×2 randomized knee-OA trial, 136 people were randomized to inulin (20 g/day), app-based exercise, both, or maltodextrin for six weeks; 117 completed follow-up. Inulin-alone improved pain versus placebo (−1.11 NRS points; 95% CI −2.18 to −0.04; p=0.045) and secondary grip strength (+4.62 kg; 1.67–7.57; p=0.002). Inulin alone did **not** improve timed-up-and-go or 30-second chair stand; the task-test gains were in the exercise arms. | Accessible food-derived compound with a controlled symptom and grip signal; worth retaining in the broad compound census. | Primary endpoint was knee pain, not muscle function; the grip result was secondary and the study was not powered for secondary outcomes or interaction. Dropout was higher in exercise arms (17/81 versus 2/55), and allocation probabilities were changed during later recruitment. The study began 1 May 2022 but was first posted to ClinicalTrials.gov on 4 January 2023. No BUB1B/MVA or chromosome-fidelity result was tested. [Primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41829888/), [trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05670314). |

**Priority decision:** among these three full-text follow-ups, vitamin D2 has the most directly quantified multi-endpoint function signal, but only in adults with measured deficiency and chronic intestinal malabsorption; consider it only if a comparable deficiency is documented and a feasible exposure is justified. Among common non-prescription compounds, calcium-HMB is a more direct muscle-function reserve than inulin in this two-source comparison. These are conditional reserve priorities, not MVA-efficacy rankings or a demonstrated global ordering of every census candidate. Keep **niacin** as the BUBR1-restoration hypothesis and **apitegromab** as the downstream function comparator, tested separately. None of the three newly followed preparations displaces that pair on current evidence.

**Evidence limits and next review:** relevant primary Methods, results and limitation sections were read once by the root reviewer; this is not independent source verification. The inulin and vitamin D2 ClinicalTrials.gov v2 records were retrieved and hashed. The HMB paper cites a Chinese registry record, but direct access failed during this pass; obtain and preserve that record before relying on its registration details. An independent reader should check the trial contrasts, hierarchy, missing-data handling, registration history and whether HMB belongs in the proposal's reserve table. No drug dose or patient use is proposed.
