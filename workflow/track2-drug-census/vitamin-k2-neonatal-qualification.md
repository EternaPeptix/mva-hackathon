# Vitamin K2: direct Bub1b RNA and BUB1B immunofluorescence in an injury-prevention model

This **partial exact-source qualification** meaningfully strengthens the existing vitamin K2 appraisal. [PMID 40273626](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006291X2500498X) supplies actual **Bub1b mRNA and BUB1B-labeled immunofluorescence responses**, alongside behavioral benefit, rather than hub-gene membership alone. The inspected evidence supports protection in the neonatal surgery/anesthesia/analgesia model. It does not establish BUBR1 stabilization, increased functional BUBR1 per cell, checkpoint rescue or BUB1B/MVA efficacy.

The exact menaquinone subtype, dose, administration route and supplier remain **unresolved** because the main text/Methods were inaccessible. Corn oil is explicitly identified as the VK2 solvent/control in the authenticated supplement. Do not assign MK-4, MK-7, a medicine or a consumer supplement by inference from the family name or another vitamin K2 paper.

## What the molecular finding means

Bub1b is also visible as a downregulated transcript in the injury comparison and an upregulated transcript in the VK2-versus-injured-vehicle comparison in Figure 4. Figure 5 supplies network/heatmap context and direct mRNA panels; S3 supplies the additional protein-associated IF evidence. Thus a description confined to “one restored hub gene” misses experimental validation that is now accessible.

However, these are **tissue-level or tissue-normalized measurements**. The IF antibody identity, specificity controls, normalization denominator, image sampling and blinding cannot be checked from the accessible materials. The images do not establish full-length functional BubR1 abundance, kinetochore localization, protein half-life or per-cell restoration. Altered numbers or proportions of proliferating/neural cell types could contribute to both tissue RNA and fluorescence changes. This is a limitation of interpretation, not a claim that the figures are erroneous.

No BUB1B perturbation, causal dependence, chromosome-segregation/fidelity endpoint or MVA genotype is established by the inspected abstract/figures/supplement. The inaccessible main could contain additional information; this check does not claim a whole-paper negative. The measured protective outcome and upward molecular response should both be retained, without importing an unrelated cancer mechanism or inferring unmeasured harm.

The NAc P11 readouts and P30 behavior are different measurement time points; individual-level mediation or a paired molecular-to-behavior relationship is not demonstrated by the accessible figures. Biological n=3 for S3 refers to mice, not image fields, but litter structure, randomization, field selection and technical nesting remain unknown. Multiple panels and targets are not independent efficacy replications.
