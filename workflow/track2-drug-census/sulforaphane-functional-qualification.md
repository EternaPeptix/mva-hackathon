# Source-labelled sulforaphane: functional qualification

Appraisal: `new-source-labelled-sulforaphane`. Source qualification date: 2026-09-27. This comparison combines four frozen source qualifications at the reading depths stated below. The catalog-identity check uses immutable baseline `e5a4e1cc72681c7af7f799fb8142719324c92f9b` (261 appraisals).

The retained evidence includes positive actual-treatment findings: Bose2020 reports better aged-mouse performance, cardiac function and survival over 12 weeks, and Wang2020 reports significantly improved diabetic-mouse grip at original-abstract depth. These deserve a bounded functional comparison. Swiderski2021 contributes diaphragm histology and ex vivo colon physiology, alongside oral-regimen nulls. Three withdrawn Sun papers supply no positive efficacy or replication credit. A separate high-concentration preprint experiment lowers PLK1 and phospho-BubR1; it establishes neither BUBR1 restoration nor clinical harm. No source establishes MVA benefit, actual-BUB1B-allele rescue or superiority over provisional niacin/apitegromab.

## Bose2020: aged-mouse performance, cardiac function and survival

[Original article](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13261), PMID 33067900, PMCID PMC7681049, Aging Cell 19(11):e13261, online 2020-10-17. The accepted source reading covered the complete scientific main narrative, six captions and table narratives/footnotes through full-text XML. Native figure pixels, usable local PDF, supplements, raw data and all numerical transcript-table cells were not read. Independent review accepted this same bounded scope.

Male C57BL/6 mice, described as C57BL/B6J in the Discussion, began at 2 months or 21–22 months and received control Teklad TD 96163 diet or D,L-sulforaphane diet ad libitum for 12 weeks. Old endpoint age was 24–25 months. **The dose denominator is unresolved:** Methods state 442.5 mg/kg body weight, while Figure 5 states 442.5 mg/kg diet for 3 months. Neither is silently repaired or converted into consumed mg/kg/day, a human dose or tissue exposure. Catalog, lot, purity and measured stability are unavailable.

## Wang2020: diabetic grip positive at original-abstract depth

[Original PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32445760/), DOI [10.1016/j.lfs.2020.117823](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lfs.2020.117823), Life Sciences 255:117823, online 2020-05-20, issue 2020-08-15. The complete original abstract and official identity metadata were qualified and independently accepted by root. The main, Methods, native outcomes, supplements and raw data remain unread; lawful bounded access stopped without a usable main.

The abstract describes SFN or DMSO treatment in db/m and littermate db/db mice and reports a **statistically significant grip-strength increase** in db/db mice using a grasping forcing device. It separately reports increased lean and gastrocnemius mass, improved fiber organization and molecular changes involving Nrf2/HO-1, inflammation and apoptosis. Lean mass is not total body weight, and mass-endpoint significance is not supplied explicitly. The grip result is not an isolated-muscle evoked-force or verified mass-independent effect.

Supplier, catalog, purity, stereochemistry, route, dose, frequency and duration are unknown at this depth. The secondary description **0.5 mg/kg daily intraperitoneally for one month remains unverified and is not an admitted regimen**. Do not inherit D,L identity from Bose or Swiderski. Exact allocation, age/background, n, units, effect, CI, P, statistical test, factorial contrast, normalization, limb/pull procedure, randomization, masking, multiplicity and exclusions are unavailable. Male is a PubMed MeSH index term, not independently checked Methods. No genetic Nrf2 intervention is described in the abstract; necessity is unproved.

No explicit nulls or harms are given in the abstract; that does not mean the main has none. db/m responses are not quantified, so normal-control sparing and safety are not established. Retained PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref and bounded searches identified no exact-paper notice. Crossref attributes NSFC award 81851380; complete funding/sponsor role is unverified, and PubMed states no conflicts. Access limits do not erase the reported functional positive or become negative efficacy evidence.

## Swiderski2021: distinct histology, ex vivo physiology and oral nulls

[Original article](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13124559), PMID 34960110, PMCID PMC8706299, Nutrients 13:4559. Complete original main narrative and five captions were read through publisher PDF text/Europe PMC XML and accepted independently; native pixels, supplement ZIP, raw observations and the primer table were not read.

Male C57BL/10 and mdx mice began at age 4 weeks. The actual D,L-sulforaphane regimen was 2 mg/kg gavage in matched 2% DMSO/corn oil, five days/week for four weeks. These are research conditions, not a clinical regimen.

Ex vivo colon contraction counts fell across proximal, mid and distal regions of both genotypes with 5 µM SFN after a saline-control period: four 15-minute recordings in each period, n=6–7/genotype/group, paired Wilcoxon. Resting diameter was unchanged. Fixed treatment order, a nonsignificant disease-control baseline difference and counts rather than force amplitude prevent disease-specific normalization or skeletal-force claims.

After the oral course, contraction, diameter, fecal-pellet-weight and cecal-mass benefits were not detected (n=9–12/genotype/group). The vehicle context erased the expected genotype contrast, and a separate corn-oil control affected contractions. This is a regimen-specific null. Diaphragm collagen/fibrosis decreased in treated mdx mice, with Figure 4 reporting two-way ANOVA/Bonferroni and P<.01. Methods n=8/group versus caption n=11–12/group is unresolved; magnitude and CI were not established. Histology is not measured respiratory or contractile output.

No lean/tested-limb-muscle-mass benefit was found, and CK/AST remained elevated. The record must not be reduced to “all other endpoints null”: normalized weight gain and free-fluid percentage fell, diaphragm Tnfα transcript rose and relative liver mass rose in both genotypes. Relative liver mass does not itself diagnose toxicity.

Sampled oral Nrf2 protein/targets and 4-HNE results did not establish intended tissue induction, without proving pathway-wide inactivity. Separate cultured-myotube 5–10 µM SFN findings raised Nrf2 protein/targets and lowered inflammatory transcripts in main-text descriptions; the unreviewed supplement and different exposure prevent transfer to oral target engagement or strength.

Initial n=20/genotype versus subsequent n=12/group plus n=8/group is unresolved. Body mass n=20/group, composition n=8, tissue weights n=10–12, serum n=6–8 and protein n=4 remain endpoint-specific; RNA Methods n=6/genotype/timepoint versus caption n=8/group is inconsistent. Randomization, masking, global endpoint correction, prospective hierarchy, full exclusions and treatment CIs were not supplied. Funding was Duchenne Parent Project Netherlands; authors declared no conflicts. No exact Swiderski notice was identified in bounded checks. Its XML retraction-forward link belongs to cited Sun reference 36, not this article.

## He preprint: high-concentration PLK1/phospho-BubR1 context

The exact experiment was read in [Research Square v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-5055115/v1), posted 2025-10-08, 46 pages, with its linked 27-page SupplementaryInformationR2.pdf. Relevant Results/Discussion/Methods, native main physical pages 33/34/38 and supplement page 6 were inspected. The full preprint, all synthetic details/spectra and raw data were not read. The same-titled [published article](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.5512891), PMID 42116263, appeared online 2026-05-11 and in the 2026-07-01 issue; only its complete abstract/metadata were accessible. Exact experimental identity across versions and confirmation in the published main are unverified. Root independently checked the central Figure 4 claims only, not the whole source.

In HEK293T, **parent SFN 100 µM for 4 hours reduced PLK1 and phospho-BubR1 Thr680**, with WT/shRNA-plk1 and DMSO/SFN contexts, separate total BubR1 and GAPDH bands. Antibodies were ab200061 for phospho-Thr680 and ab183496 for total BubR1. This representative blot lacks experiment-specific biological n, densitometry, P and CI. Qualitatively retained total bands are not equivalence. There is no checkpoint-competence, faithful-segregation, BUB1B-allele or muscle-output assay.

Parent competition reduced post-lysis ITCyne labeling at PLK1 C67 (representative H/L 9.5; QTRP biological duplicates, CV<40%, threshold H/L≥1.5). Direct C67 adduct mapping used **SFyne**, not unchanged parent SFN, at 100 µM for 4 hours. Abundance changes confound pure occupancy inference. Purified-kinase inhibition occurred only above 200 µM, with staurosporine IC50<1 µM; this does not establish potent direct PLK1 inhibition at the cellular exposure.

PLK1 mRNA was not detectably reduced and Cyclin B1 was qualitatively preserved. MG132 5 µM for 24 hours before SFN 100 µM for 4 hours alleviated PLK1 loss. Engineered **PLK1 C67S**, not a BUB1B variant, attenuated/resisted protein loss across 0–100 µM over 4 hours; p-BubR1 rescue itself was not shown. qPCR used three independent samples with technical triplicates.

Native MTT IC50 labels were WT 16.53 versus C67S 53.59 µM; separate Extended Data comparisons were parent SFN 22.88 versus SFyne 68.91 µM and WT 14.53 versus PLK1 knockdown 52.94 µM. These experiments are not pooled. General Methods specify 24-hour compound exposure plus 4-hour MTT; Figure 4j omits duration and Figure 3f separately labels a 4-hour screen. Biological triplicates, SD and two-sided t tests are reported, without fit model, CIs or dose-wise multiplicity. MTT is a metabolic viability proxy, not a direct death count. Partial resistance does not make PLK1 the sole target.

QTRP probe concentration, vehicle wording, supplement numbering and C67S “abolished” versus “attenuated” inconsistencies remain in the JSON. Selected supplementary NRF2/ROS context has incomplete dose/time/gating/statistical detail and supplies no normal-cell safety result. HEK293T and the supplementary HEK293 label do not establish normal primary muscle effects. High-concentration transformed-cell findings are mechanistic context, not clinical harm, food-dose risk, BUBR1 restoration or MVA benefit. Funding and no-competing-interest declarations are retained source-specifically; bounded metadata/queries identified no notice, without proving latest-version completeness.
