# BET compounds: retain vascular benefits, separate the BUB1B observation

**22 September 2026.** Seven administered compounds merit separate entries,
but they share one [macrophage/atherogenesis study](https://doi.org/10.1093/jleuko/qiag017).
ABBV-744 has a controlled mouse plaque result. The other six have cell results
in that study. Neither result establishes BUBR1 restoration or MVA muscle benefit.

A [23 September GBM follow-up](gsk620-gbm-qualification.md) now qualifies a
separate GSK620 mouse survival result and ABBV-744 cell evidence. The findings
below remain scoped to the macrophage paper and its earlier assessment.

## Results and interpretation

ABBV-744 was given orally during 60 days of high-fat feeding in AAV-PCSK9 mice,
with a separate YAP/TAZ-overexpression model and matched solvent controls. The
reported plaque reduction is substantial, approximately twofold in the first
model and over 50% in the second. This is prevention during the diet challenge,
not demonstrated reversal of established plaques. Twelve animals per group were
allocated; individual tissue assays used three to five. There is no muscle-force,
survival or chromosome-fidelity endpoint. Randomization and blinding are not
described in the main Methods.

The supplementary cell comparisons support reduced inflammatory secretion and
lipid uptake, with variable effects across compounds and conditions. S3 has no
significance annotations; S7 includes an iBET-762 null in unstimulated mouse
macrophages. Numerous measured cells do not represent that many independent
experiments. Near-preserved short-term MTT signal does not establish preserved
renewal or long-term safety. Figure 5J also narrows the broad cytokine claim:
IL6 in the standard model has P=.083 and MCP1 in the YAP/TAZ model P=.053.

Crucially, the **BUB1B RNA decrease belongs to genetic YAP, TAZ or BRD4
knockdown**. It is not a drug-specific BUBR1 protein result. The source therefore
establishes neither protein restoration nor drug-induced BUBR1 depletion.
Systemic dosing also leaves the exclusive cellular mediator unresolved.

## Apabetalone: preserve the human trial alongside the cell result

In [BETonMACE](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2763951),
2,425 adults with diabetes and recent acute coronary syndrome were randomized
to apabetalone or placebo added to usual care. The primary cardiovascular
endpoint was not statistically significant: HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.65–1.04, P=.11.
This is not proof of no effect. Adverse-event discontinuation was 9.4% versus
5.7%; ALT elevations above five times the upper limit were 3.3% versus 0.7%.
These findings belong to apabetalone in that population, not every BET compound.

## Competitive decision and review depth

Retain ABBV-744 as a vascular-inflammation research lead and the six distinct
cell comparators. None currently supplies a measured BUBR1 or useful-muscle
advantage in the relevant model. Reconsider a compound if a matching lesion,
compound-specific engagement and functional benefit are demonstrated with
preserved renewal and fidelity. This is a prioritization decision, not proven
inferiority to niacin or apitegromab; those nominees remain unproved in MVA.

The full main article and all main legends, plus the 18-page supplement text,
were read. Main figures 4–5 and supplementary S3, S7 and S12 were visually
inspected; other images and raw data were not independently analyzed. BETonMACE
review covers main design, efficacy and safety, not its protocol or supplements.
No new independent reviewer or biological replication is claimed. These
follow-ups add **zero abstract-reading credit**. Structured identities, source
hashes and limitations are in [the accompanying assessment](bet-macrophage-qualification.json).
