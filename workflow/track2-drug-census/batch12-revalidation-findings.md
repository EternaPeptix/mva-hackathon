## Actual benefits and their bounds

- **ON-01910.Na, PMID 33573708:** retinoblastoma culture and mouse xenograft suppression, with reported non-tumor-cell sparing and structural/functional ocular preservation at a local 0.8 nmol intravitreal threshold. Preserve both the antitumor result and normal-organ comparator. Target selectivity, exact local regimen and systemic/pediatric transfer require qualification; preservation is not restoration of impaired muscle or fidelity.
- **Vorinostat plus irradiation, PMC7715707:** a pediatric ependymoma conference case reports near-complete disease resolution at 15 months, followed by mild growth in two lesions at 18 months. The combination response cannot be assigned to vorinostat alone or treated as a controlled efficacy estimate.
- **Ursolic acid, PMID 34858165:** reduced TNBC culture proliferation, migration and invasion and induced arrest/apoptosis; the abstract also reports in-vivo growth inhibition without describing that model or regimen. This adds cancer-context evidence, separate from previously qualified muscle-function observations.
- **MNF, PMID 25505565:** the specific (R,R′)-4-methoxy-1-naphthylfenoterol derivative reduced C6 glioma xenografts and proliferation. It is not ordinary fenoterol or a cannabinoid product, and postimplantation tumor treatment is not a cancer-incidence prevention experiment.
- Actual cancer-cell combination effects include **MGCD0103 plus Nutlin-3** (PMID 27283770), **CFI-402257 plus duvelisib** (PMID 39836493), and **vorinostat plus flavopiridol** (PMID 21159612). Exact drug-treated models, genotype dependence, exposure, synergy and normal-cell windows remain source questions. Nutlin-3 cannot be silently equated with Nutlin-3a.
- **N-acetylcysteine, PMID 18663006:** reduced ROS and gammaH2AX foci in infection-challenged A549 cells. This is a molecular protective effect, not demonstrated completed repair, faithful segregation or normal-lung/MVA functional rescue. Functional Treg expansion (PMID 27281795) is retained separately as an ex-vivo cellular result; proposed IL-2/cell therapy is not a reported randomized treatment success.

## Nulls, harms and attribution

**Suramin** changed tumor-cell transcripts but its mouse tumor-growth result was nonsignificant, P = 0.18 (PMID 41668712). Suggested underpower does not convert that result into efficacy. CHK1 inhibition enhanced adriamycin sensitivity in triple-negative cells but failed to sensitize ER+/PR+/HER2− cells, which instead showed monotherapy activity (PMID 32210727). NT5C silencing did not restore pemetrexed sensitivity (PMID 22905093); lipocalin-2 did not significantly predict docetaxel response or survival (PMID 33542838).

Vismodegib reduced post-hepatectomy liver regeneration (PMID 35723318); MLN4924 impaired oocyte maturation (PMID 31111756); ochratoxin A disrupted mitosis (PMID 21551354). Raw areca-nut extract, particularly with a lime preparation additive, produced cancer/aneuploidy-associated harm in mice (PMID 23805780). These exposure-specific results do not establish general dietary, pediatric or MVA harm. “Lime” is not presumed to mean citrus fruit.

Marker restoration is kept separate from function: zinc or an unnamed HDAC inhibitor restored MT3 expression without a stated tumor-control endpoint (PMID 20885975). Genetic p53/NEK2 sensitization, MYPOP gene-transfer effects, and DNAJA2 insulin-receptor trafficking remain genetic evidence. The latter does not establish that pharmacologically changing uptake benefits a BUB1B lesion. Drug identities are left unresolved when the source supplies only a target/class. A one-sentence 123-character saved summary (PMID 30766204) was read in full but receives no invented clinical-study detail or full-paper credit.
