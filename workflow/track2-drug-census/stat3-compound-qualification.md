# SC-43 and garcinone D: different directions, qualified by experiment

**22 September 2026.** Both compounds receive explicit appraisals. SC-43 earns
antifibrotic and survival evidence in mice; garcinone D earns protein-associated
and proliferative effects in defined cell models. Neither has demonstrated
BUBR1 restoration, MVA benefit or superiority to the provisional nominees.

| Compound | Positive evidence retained | Decision, weakness and reopening condition |
|---|---|---|
| **SC-43** | Suppresses MAD2L1-associated malignant-cell phenotypes; a separate study reports less mouse liver fibrosis and improved survival against vehicle. | Retain as an SHP-1/STAT3 and antifibrotic research comparator. No relevant fibrotic lesion, muscle benefit or BUBR1-restoration bridge is established here. Reconsider if affected tissue shows a matching mechanism and drug treatment improves absolute function without a renewal/fidelity penalty. |
| **Garcinone D** | Increases MAD2L1 protein bands and restores proliferation after MAD2L1 knockdown in leukemia cells; separate neural-progenitor proliferation evidence. | Retain as a natural-product mechanistic lead. Malignant-cell growth is not therapeutic rescue; final exposure in the leukemia study is unclear, and direct/selective STAT3 agonism is unproved. Reconsider only with qualified exposure, endogenous BUBR1/checkpoint measurements and productive tissue output in affected/corrected models. |

This comparison does not require challengers to prove MVA efficacy while
excusing that gap in niacin/apitegromab. All remain hypotheses for this disease.
The available observations support different tasks and cannot produce a
calibrated clinical-response probability or a universal rank order.

## What the corrected MAD2L1 paper actually tested

[Zhu et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11797693/) applied SC-43 to
MAD2L1-overexpressing RS4;11 leukemia cells and garcinone D to MAD2L1-knockdown
Nalm6 cells. The latter raises MAD2L1 bands and reverses reduced growth/EdU and
transwell endpoints; SC-43 acts in the opposite direction in the other model.
Figure 5E is qualitative: no displayed densitometry or replicate denominator.
**MAD2L1 is the measured protein; BUB1B is a discovery-stage hub-gene entry.**
Neither protein half-life nor chromosome-faithful daughter-cell output was
measured. Animal survival belongs to genetic manipulation, not these drug arms.

The [2026 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12764733/)
replaces a duplicated Nalm6 migration image. Its corrected Figure 5 is the
version used here. Authors report unchanged conclusions; raw-data validation
is not claimed. Supplement Table S3 identifies both MCE products but lists
`10 mM(DMSO)` under working concentration without a clear final dilution or
schedule. We do not infer a 10 mM cellular exposure. Missing factorial drug
controls, an unstated drug-panel replicate n and inconsistent transwell-coating
descriptions further limit the interpretation.

## SC-43's separate organism-level benefit

[Su et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-01572-z) report less
fibrosis in CCl4 and bile-duct-ligation mouse models. In the CCl4 treatment
experiment, SC-43 followed two weeks of injury and continued alongside toxin
for six weeks, orally five days/week. At 10 mg/kg, survival exceeded vehicle
(log-rank **P=0.0291**), with no established superiority over sorafenib
(P=0.0671). Figure 3 gives n=6–10/group; no at-risk table is supplied.
Histology and collagen measurements support the tissue result.

Cell-culture data support stellate-cell apoptosis as a proposed mechanism,
with SHP-1/STAT3 involvement; selective in-vivo removal is not established.
Restoration of a deficient mitotic protein was not demonstrated. The human biopsies are
observational. Some secondary outcomes are weaker: alpha-SMA reduction in
Figure S4 is nonsignificant (P=0.0595), and BDL hydroxyproline has no significant
reduction established by S6 (no test is reported). Toxin withdrawal also causes
regression in controls.
These limits preserve the actual survival positive without converting it to
muscle regeneration or a favorable therapeutic window in MVA.

## Garcinone D and the natural-product extension

[Yang et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27177723/) report C17.2 neural
progenitor proliferation, more S-phase cells and p-STAT3/Cyclin D1/Nrf2/HO-1
changes. [Publisher snippets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304394016303111)
describe opposite viability directions at lower
and higher concentrations. Only the complete abstract and identified snippets
were accessible here; no full-methods or figure qualification is claimed.

[Nauman et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7854934/) report
garcinone D inhibition of cell-free CDK2/CyclinE1 (main-text EC50 28.23
micromolar), alongside cancer-cell viability effects in a nine-xanthone study.
The available main text was read; its supplementary methods/tables remain
unread. Alpha- and gamma-mangostin, beta-mangostin, gartanin, 8-desoxygartanin,
garcinone C, 9-hydroxycalabaxanthone and 3-isomangostin are retained as related
source pointers, not eight additional qualified MVA treatments. These results
do not justify treating garcinone D as a universally growth-promoting, selective
STAT3 reagent. Purified compound is not interchangeable with mangosteen fruit,
extract or supplements.

## Identity, supply and human evidence

[Selleck SC-43](https://www.selleckchem.com/products/sc-43.html) (S0445,
CAS 1400989-25-4) and [garcinone D](https://www.selleckchem.com/products/garcinone-d-stat-activator.html)
(E2794, CAS 107390-08-9) have research-product listings. The leukemia supplement
names MCE HY-136657 and HY-N6953 respectively. Listings establish catalogue
availability, not delivery, historical lot equivalence, clinical formulation
or safe use. Conflicting supplier inhibitor/activator headings do not replace
primary evidence.

SC-43 already appears as **CHEMBL2063215**, with a phase-1 development record.
A current registry query finds [NCT03443622](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03443622)
withdrawn with **zero actual participants**, and
[NCT04733521](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04733521) at **UNKNOWN** status
(last known not yet recruiting; last update February 2021). Neither returned
record has posted results. The second protocol refers to earlier monotherapy
and PK data not retrieved here; registration does not verify those results,
and this check does not prove that no human evidence exists elsewhere.

## Reading boundary and next evidence

The [structured assessment](stat3-compound-qualification.json) records exact
source identities, limitations and reading depths. Zhu: extensive indexed
primary sections/all legends, complete supplement/correction and corrected
Figure 5, without a verified continuous complete main copy. Su: complete main
and supplement text, five key main figures and all supplemental figures.
Yang/Nauman retain the access limits above. No raw articles, abstracts or
figures are redistributed. A targeted independent check supplements the
source appraisal; it is not experimental replication.

Resolve exposure and model relevance before advancement. A relevant mechanism
and controlled improvement in absolute productive output are needed; a supplier
label, more cells or higher checkpoint-associated protein alone is insufficient.
