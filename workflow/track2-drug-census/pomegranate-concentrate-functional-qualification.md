# Machin 2014: pomegranate concentrate and post-exercise strength

**Retain a preparation-specific controlled functional research reserve.** Machin et al. report higher average post-exercise knee-extensor and elbow-flexor strength with both tested pomegranate-juice-concentrate schedules than with placebo. These are main treatment effects in baseline-relative strength; treatment-by-time interactions were nonsignificant. The paper does not establish a faster recovery trajectory, dose equivalence, a minimum effective dose, chronic benefit or MVA superiority. [Original publisher main](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2014/271959), [original seven-page PDF](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1155/2014/271959), DOI **10.1155/2014/271959**.

## Design, preparation and denominator limits

The reported study recruited **45** healthy, nonsmoking, non-resistance-trained, recreationally active young men (mean age 22.3 ± 4.1 years). It is a double-blind randomized **parallel** study with one eight-day period, not a crossover. After three days of strength-test familiarization, men were assigned to three groups. The article does not give per-arm sample sizes, random sequence generation, allocation concealment, masking-success assessment, a screening/participant-flow diagram or recruitment dates. **Do not infer 15 per arm.** The strength and soreness captions give total n=45; the authors state all participants completed the 40 elbow-exercise repetitions. Neither statement provides a complete endpoint-by-time missingness account or an intention-to-treat analysis plan.

| Schedule | Morning drink | Evening drink | Source-reported daily polyphenols |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1x | 30 mL pomegranate juice concentrate, diluted with water | 30 mL placebo concentrate, diluted with water | 650 mg gallic-acid equivalents (GAE) |
| 2x | 30 mL active concentrate, diluted with water | Same active serving | 1,300 mg GAE |
| PLA | 30 mL placebo concentrate, diluted with water | Same placebo serving | 0 mg GAE |

All groups took **two drinks daily**, about twelve hours apart. The active source was Wonderful-variety California pomegranates, POM Wonderful Inc., LLP, supplied in 500-mL concentrate bottles. Each active serving is reported to contain 650 mg GAE with a polyphenol profile of 95.5% ellagitannins, 3.5% ellagic acid and 1% anthocyanins. Both active and placebo servings contained **24 g carbohydrate**, with added color/flavor for masking. The final dilution volume, lot/catalogue number, placebo ingredients beyond this description, independent batch assay and profile-measurement method are not supplied. GAE is an assay-equivalent quantity, not a dose of pure gallic acid or a demonstrated active constituent. No retail equivalence or interchangeability with POMx extract, ordinary juice or urolithin A is established.

On **day 4**, after supplementation had already begun, blood, strength and soreness baseline measurements preceded exercise. The challenge combined 20 minutes of downhill running (4 km, −10% grade) with 40 bilateral eccentric elbow-flexion repetitions. Assessments followed at 2, 24, 48, 72 and 96 hours. This post-supplementation/pre-exercise baseline differs from a pre-supplementation baseline. Results were the peak of three bilateral isometric tests at 65° knee flexion or 45° elbow flexion, expressed as each participant's percentage of baseline. No absolute-force treatment estimate is published. Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant and multivitamin supplements were stopped one week before and throughout testing; participants were reminded to take drinks, returned in a similar postprandial state and avoided caffeine. Quantified adherence and dietary control beyond these statements are not reported.

## Direct functional results

Numbers below are **reported means ± SD averaged over the post-exercise observation period**, not single-day results or treatment-effect intervals. The source does not fully specify how the reported SD combines participant/time variation.

| Outcome | 1x | 2x | PLA | What was actually tested/reported |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Knee-extensor strength, % of day-4 baseline | 93.9 ± 6.1 | 91.6 ± 5.6 | 87.1 ± 7.9 | Three-group treatment effect P<.05; text and Figure 2 caption report each active group higher than PLA, P<.05. |
| Elbow-flexor strength, % of day-4 baseline | 83.6 ± 10.4 | 85.6 ± 7.2 | 78.4 ± 6.7 | Three-group treatment effect P<.05; text and Figure 4 caption report each active group higher than PLA, P<.05. |
| Knee soreness, 0–10 | 2.3 ± 1.3 | 2.3 ± 1.1 | 2.3 ± 1.4 | No detected group difference, P>.05. |
| Elbow soreness, 0–10 | 3.0 ± 1.5 | 2.9 ± 1.1 | 2.9 ± 1.2 | No detected group difference, P>.05. |
| Serum myoglobin, ng/mL; reduced total n=39 | 79 ± 56 | 53 ± 27 | 56 ± 22 | No detected group difference, P>.05 after outlier exclusion. |

Simple subtraction of reported means gives **+6.8 and +4.5 percentage points** for 1x/2x versus placebo in knee strength, and **+5.2 and +7.2 points** in elbow strength. These descriptive differences are not absolute-force gains, relative percentage improvements, model-derived effect estimates or confidence intervals. No interval is reconstructed from the SDs or an assumed arm n.

The article reports time effects and strength below baseline at every post-exercise measurement, including 96 hours. It explicitly reports **no significant treatment-by-time interaction for any measured variable**. Accordingly, the defensible finding is higher average baseline-relative strength during the post-exercise period. The main effect does not independently demonstrate that the groups' recovery slopes differ or that every individual time point has a significant active–placebo contrast. Figures 2/4 captions explicitly label their symbols as main treatment effects; no time-point pairwise P values are supplied.

Myoglobin results exclude people with values described as four times the group mean at any time point, reducing n from 45 to 39: **six excluded**, by subtraction. Per-arm exclusions, exact threshold implementation, prespecification, reasons for extreme values, and an all-participant sensitivity analysis are not supplied. This is an endpoint-specific exclusion, not evidence that six people left the whole trial. Strength and soreness remain captioned n=45. A myoglobin null after exclusion is not proof of absent structural injury; the paper supplies no tissue assay confirming structural protection.

## Dose inference and statistical reporting

Methods specify two-way ANOVA for treatment, time and interaction, alpha <.05, SPSS, and mean ± SD. They do not name the pairwise procedure, state a multiplicity adjustment across groups/outcomes/time, report exact strength P values or F/df, provide a power calculation, or identify prospective registration/protocol/SAP and an endpoint-testing hierarchy. Those details remain **unreported**, rather than being silently assumed corrected or uncorrected. The statement of a primary purpose in the paper is not proof of a prospectively registered statistical hierarchy. The repeated-measure covariance/sphericity treatment and analysis-population definitions are not described.

The discussion reports **no statistically significant difference between 1x and 2x** for either strength measure. It gives no exact dose-comparison P, CI, equivalence margin or equivalence/noninferiority test. The direction even differs descriptively: 1x minus 2x is +2.3 percentage points for knee strength but −2.0 for elbow strength. Two tested doses with an undetected difference cannot establish equivalence, a plateau, equal potency or a 650-mg threshold. The paper's suggestion that half the prior dose may be sufficient is an author interpretation; it is not adopted as a minimum effective dose or clinical regimen.

## Safety, funding, cohort boundaries and earlier studies

No systematic adverse-event/tolerability counts or ascertainment plan are reported. Health-related exclusions and completion of exercise do not establish safety, including pediatric, renal, cancer-context or long-term safety. POM Wonderful funded the study by grant; the authors state the company did not have final approval over paper content. The authors also declare no conflicts. Both disclosures should remain visible; sponsor independence is not inferred.

This is a separately described 45-person parallel study, not the previously qualified nine-day POMx crossover or the 36-person Sheffield Lamb trial. It shares investigator/laboratory lineage with Trombold/Coyle and commercial source context; it is **not an independent-laboratory replication of POMx**. Individual participant overlap with earlier UT work cannot be audited without identifiers/recruitment dates. The different design/sample supports treating it as a distinct reported study, not claiming proven participant-level independence or duplicating either earlier cohort's reading credit.

- **[POMx 2010](pomegranate-extract-functional-qualification.md) remains original-abstract-only.** Its reported elbow-strength advantages at 48/72 hours are preserved. This 2014 paper's retrospective statements about earlier doses do not fill the original 2010 formulation, denominator, baseline, masking or analysis gaps. Concentrate and extract are not aliases; no pooled effect is computed.
- **[Lamb 2019](cherry-blend-functional-qualification.md) remains full-source counterevidence.** It tested undiluted POM Wonderful juice in a separate three-arm parallel trial and found no detected group/interaction strength effect. Its strength n=11 per arm, omnibus tests, missingness and measured chemistry remain as previously qualified. It is not an exact-concentrate replication, and the shared brand does not establish formulation equivalence. No phenolic-dose explanation is inferred across trials.

The study adds controlled actual-function evidence beyond biomarkers. That deserves compensation-oriented comparison with niacin's uncontrolled functional observation under the same source standards, while healthy-adult short-term recovery does not establish larger clinical benefit than either niacin or apitegromab. No BUB1B/BUBR1 result, actual-allele rescue, MVA treatment, chronic weakness benefit or nominee replacement is established.
